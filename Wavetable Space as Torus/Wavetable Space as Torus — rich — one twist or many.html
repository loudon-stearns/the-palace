fn = n·f₀·√(1 + B·n²)
(partial n’s frequency) = n × (the fundamental) × the square root of (1 + B × n²); B = 0 is a harmonic tone, and the page’s inharmonicity grows with B
en = 2π·n·(√(1 + B·n²) − 1) ≈ π·B·n³
(how far partial n turns past its whole turns in one lap of the fundamental) = 2π × n × (that square root, minus 1), which is about π × B × n³ while B·n² is small
en = n·β
a single twist β of the cycle, φ slid on by β each lap, turns partial n by n × β: every partial in step with its number
mismatch = 1 − Σ an²·cos(en − n·β) ⁄ Σ an²
(how unlike B’s cycle and the twisted cycle are) = 1 − their correlation: 0 when the twist matches B’s lap exactly, 1 when the two cycles are unrelated. Here an = 1 ⁄ n, a sawtooth’s partials
