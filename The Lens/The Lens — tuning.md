13. **A person glass may invent a scene, and some person pages are living people.** The mandate licenses every glass to speak "from your own life" and to invent a scene, flagged in the distortion check (`lens-mandate.js:108-110`); a cold code review of a145f1a4 (finding 5, `The Lens/proofs/2026-11-03-bold-mandate/code-review.md`) noted that this reaches `People/` pages for living people. Canon forbids fabricated quotes ([[Making a Palace Citizen]]: "real quotes only, no fabrication"; [[SCHEMA — Reference]] §8 `speech`), and the closest it comes to a scene rule is [[Annie Dillard]], a living writer: "Do not invent a warm, conversational, talk-show Dillard … that would be fabrication." It says nothing about invented scenes, and The Lens has only endorsed one for Simondon, who is dead. **Owed, open, Loudon's ruling:** may a glass for a living person invent a first-person scene, and if so, may it ever put new words in their mouth as a quotation?
