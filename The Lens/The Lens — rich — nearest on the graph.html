How near is near, on this graph?

The section says the palace already holds the signal for which pairs spark: the typed-link graph. "Already-linked or two-hop-apart pages are reliable sparks; distant unlinked pages are mostly noise." STIGMERGY's [L]ens picker ranks every glass by hops from the subject. Here is that ranking on the real graph, walked out from one entry until the claim can be tested: how much of the palace sits two hops away, and how much is really far.

The picker's eight

The [L]ens picker shows eight chips, "nearest on the graph". The ranking is by hop count alone, so every one-hop page ties, and the code breaks a tie by the alphabet (lens-suggest.js: a.distance − b.distance || a.title.localeCompare(b.title)). The amber dots are the eight it shows for this subject.

Is two hops a filter?

The test, over every entry as the subject. A filter worth the name keeps a short list. For each entry, the share of the rest of the palace within one hop and within two, and the share that can't be reached at all, which is where "distant unlinked" would live. Then the sparks on record, beside the distance of each pair.

pairs = N × (N − 1)
(ordered lens-pairs) = (entries) × (every other entry). A glass and a subject swap, so each pair counts twice.
share≤2(s) = |{ e ≠ s : d(s, e) ≤ 2 }| ⁄ (N − 1)
(how much of the palace two hops reaches from subject s) = (entries at most two links from s, counting a link either way) ÷ (every other entry)
