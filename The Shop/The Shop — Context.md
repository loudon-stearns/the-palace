# The Shop — Context

Why [[The Shop]] is shaped as it is. Read this before you edit the page; read [[Maker — Context]] before you edit the foreman.

## A founding statement that stayed put

The Shop and the [[Maker]] were born in one commit on 2026-05-09 (`06b5dac1`), and they split the work then the way they still do: this page is the pattern, and the Maker and the Specialists are its instances (The Shop.md:98). Nearly everything the Shop learned afterwards landed on those instances. The Maker grew from 13 KB to 50 KB; this page went from 9.4 KB to 13.3 KB, and most of the difference is the generated roster. Apart from the roster, the hero, one tuning-ledger sentence (`9f9d1cf1`, 2026-10-15) and FLUX's name where Midjourney's stood, the prose is the May text.

So the page speaks in the voice of the week it was written, and some of it was written ahead of the work. The Kokoro "RNBO" gotcha it uses as its example was a hypothetical; Kokoro's own page still asks it as an open question (Shop/Kokoro.md:200). The body's Forward Vectors still name the first round-trips and the first comparison as ahead, though the Maker's Recipes record them run in May and the FLUX–ComfyUI comparison closed on 2026-06-01 (`e2773ac8`). That is mostly the page doing its job: it states what holds across every Specialist, and those claims have not moved.

## Where the weight sits

Three claims carry the page, each here because it holds for the whole Shop rather than one tool. The tier triplet is the shape of every cost–quality conversation. Gotchas accumulate in the entry, not the conversation. And the studio register: art words, chosen because they carry depth over coverage — the passage where this page meets [[Quality Manifesto]], as this weave's hub reader found.

The Specialist Anatomy is the only place the Specialist template is written down; `Shop/Maker/_TEMPLATE/` holds only a test plan. The folder readers found the June-to-October Specialists leaner than the list, each in its own way. Read the anatomy as the May template, not a checklist to pad against.

## What it leaves to others

- **Operations.** Intake, routing, house standards, host checks and the cascade in full live on the Maker. This page names a three-layer cascade; the Maker articulates four layers and a Layer 0 beneath them, and calls them what this page "anticipated" (Shop/Maker.md:161). The hub keeps the idea; the foreman keeps the table.
- **Membership.** Each Specialist declares `member-of [[The Shop]]`, and `Shop/Maker/build-roster.mjs` computes the roster block from those declarations. The block is committed text, so it goes stale when a Specialist changes and nobody re-runs the script; the doc-drift linter's E4 fails the gate until someone does (Shop/Maker.md:284). Never hand-edit between the markers.
- **Loudon's correction.** The page records that the singular Maker came from a question Loudon asked. What he said next — bandwidth is one axis; taste, values, the joy of working in teams, and a mind formed making sound rather than image are others — is quoted in [[Diversity of Thought in Many-Agent Systems]] (line 46). The page points there rather than carrying it, and that is the right split: one entry holds the bet, the other holds the doubt.

## Two contradictions the page holds without naming

**The hub it declined.** At birth a `Shop.md` hub was turned down because "hubs are justified only when they hold something the spokes can't" (The Shop.md:156). Since June this concept page is where every Specialist's `member-of` points, and it holds the one thing no spoke can: the computed roster. No `Shop/Shop.md` exists, and the decline was right about that; the hub's single job arrived here anyway. Whether The Shop becomes a hub and moves stage is open on 2026-10-24, for Loudon to decide at the October weave's second sitting.

**One foreman, five Makers.** "The Current Bet" speaks of one Maker; the page's own roster line names five `maker` pages (The Shop.md:105). The four newer ones bear on the bet differently. The [[Shopkeeper]] is the Maker's deliberate supply-side pair and calls the pairing a probe of the Diversity question (Shop/Shopkeeper.md:99). [[Frame Designer]] is the Maker's first per-medium Designer — the split the Open Questions anticipated (Shop/Frame Designer.md:66). The [[Loudon Live Post-producer]] is a Producer that dispatches through the Maker (Shop/Loudon Live Post-producer.md:139). The [[Hero and Avatar Maker]] owns its own render path. The front door stayed singular, and plurality came back beside it and beneath it, one earned page at a time — the rule [[BLUELINE — Specialists and Seams]] states as "start as a recipe; promote to a thin foreman only when it earns one." Nothing in git shows a run of the side-by-side experiment the Diversity entry proposes (its line 52). The bet is neither won nor lost; it has stopped being one bet.

## For whoever edits next

The page is short on purpose. A new house rule, route or gotcha goes to the Maker or the Specialist that owns it; this page changes when the pattern changes. Its Open Question about the Maker at ~15 Specialists now reads against a roster of 31.
