# The Shop — tuning

What using the bench taught the Shop. A Specialist's lessons go on that Specialist's ledger; this ledger is for the bench itself, which has no ledger of its own. Opened by the Concierge on 2026-11-17 because a round's findings about the bench had nowhere to land. If the Shop would rather keep them elsewhere, this is one file to move. Newest last.

## From the second light round's first five pieces — 2026-11-17

Found by five makers and five challengers working through `node Shop/shop.mjs run`, gathered by the working session in `Enrichment/light-round-2026-11-2/pauses/pause-1.md` § What the bench could not do, and carried here by the Concierge. The first six are the session's, in its words; the last four are the Concierge's, from placing and presenting the five.

- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · A project's own instrument has no place in a chain. The bench refused "Mycelial Reverb plugin engine (Algorithm.h)" unless it began "hand:", so a project earns no ledger line. Loudon wants light pieces to use more of the projects (2026-11-11); the bench cannot yet say when one did. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · `check` resolves a file against the first job that lists it. When a picture got its own job, `check` showed it as CHANGED though its job matched. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · Provenance's chain text is not updated when a rerun adds a step; only hashes are compared. One piece's record does not name the step that lowered the file 1.40 dB. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · Inputs are hashed only if the job lists them, and nothing says when they are missing. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · A maker who iterates through the bench leaves a run line each time. There is no quiet rerun for the same job on the same day; one job left four lines on two ledgers. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · A wrong `--taught` line cannot be withdrawn by the one who wrote it. One said "0.2 to 1.3 s" where the measurement was 0.04 to 1.34 s; it took an elder's correction line. · owed a review
- from the Concierge · 2026-11-17 · A `--taught` lesson is copied to every Specialist in the job's chain, whoever it is about. A lesson about Stable Audio Open's rate and one about the bench itself landed on Drawn SVG's ledger. · owed a review
- from the Concierge · 2026-11-17 · `present` cannot leave a page's scroll out. Presenting a piece made for a project writes a link on that project's scroll, whose plan is Loudon's and whose trail is its steward's. Mycelial Reverb's piece was left unpresented for that reason. · owed a review
- from the Concierge · 2026-11-17 · The link `present` leaves on a page's scroll says "Nothing here is wired into the page" and lists every job's outputs. Neither is true once a piece is placed in the page's rich face and a first version has been sent back: Walk Ceremony's scroll now lists its withdrawn first version beside the placed one, and the jobs file's `notes` do not reach that link. · owed a review
- from the Concierge · 2026-11-17 · Placing copies "made with" into a piece's `by` line by hand, because the rich-face manifest has no field for it and the renderer shows none. The placed copy also takes a new name, so `check` on a page's bundle finds nothing that accounts for it; the manifest carries a `made_in` block pointing back at the job. · owed a review

## From the second light round's second five — 2026-11-17

Relayed by the working session from its makers and challengers, carried here by the Concierge. The second repeats a finding above and is kept because it happened again.

- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · `check` counts copied project inputs as files nothing accounts for, though the job lists and hashes them. Animate the Background's five copies of BLUELINE's files read as "5 that nothing accounts for" beside 3 that match. · owed a review
- from the light round's makers · 2026-11-17 · Again: a `--taught` lesson is copied to every Specialist in a chain, relevant or not. Kokoro's two lessons, one about ffmpeg's loudnorm and one about re-rendering a past take, are on Drawn SVG's ledger and Sound from Code's too. · owed a review
- from the Kokoro maker · 2026-11-17 · ffmpeg's two-pass loudnorm, asked for `linear=true`, ran in dynamic mode on an 11.4 s read and lifted the quiet between words: a 140 ms quiet measured on the raw take was 110 ms on the saved file. Measure a timing claim after loudness, and check `normalization_type` in the second pass's JSON. The lesson is on ffmpeg's ledger in the maker's words; it is here because it changes what the bench's house numbers cost a piece about timing. Another session met the same switch the same day, the other way round, on a clip under 3 s. · owed a review
- from the Animate the Background maker · 2026-11-17 · STIGMERGY's `/api/file` still serves mp4 as octet-stream, while `/rich/` serves it correctly. Reported by the maker; not checked by the Concierge. A finding for the app more than the bench, kept here until it has a better home. · owed a review
- from the Concierge · 2026-11-17 · A face has no way to offer a still in place of a loop on a small screen: one piece is one file at every width. Animate the Background's still went in as a second piece beside its loop. · owed a review
