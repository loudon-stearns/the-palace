Leverage Points Framework · Meadows' Core Insight · a model, not a measurement

Same effort, three places

The section says that small efforts at high-leverage points create massive change. Here a creator's practice is a small stock-and-flow model, and the same small effort is spent at a parameter, at a delay and at the goal. Every number on this sheet is this model's, run here, not a platform's and not a person's. The model's structure is the maker's choice; what it can show is how that structure behaves.

One stock, one inflow, one outflow

Start with Meadows' bathtub. Wellbeing is a stock. Rest refills it toward full; the grind of promoting drains it. You cannot set the level, only the flows. Move the share of the week spent promoting and watch the level settle somewhere new.

W(t+1) = W + r·(1 − W) − b·u²·W settles at W* = r / (r + b·u²)
Each day wellbeing refills by a share r of what is missing, and drains by b times the square of the promotion share u. Doubling the promotion share costs four times the drain.

Four stocks, steered by hand

Add three more stocks: depth of work, a community of people who come back, and followers. Deep work needs time and energy; community grows from good work seen by someone who has the energy to answer; followers pile up from views. Views themselves are a flow, and a noisy one, a lottery each day. Still by hand: you pick the promotion share and leave it.

V = y·u·(0.3 + S)·noise S += l·(1 − u)·W − dS·S C += 0.01·S·W·√u − C/365 R += 0.01·V − R/365
Views V are the promotion share times how good the work is, times a yield y (thumbnails, timing), times a daily lottery. Depth S grows from unpromoted time and energy and fades slowly. Community C grows from depth and energy, seen a little. Followers R pile up from views and fade over a year.

More promotion buys followers and spends everything else. The structure decides that, not the person.

Close the loop: the creator steers by what they watch

Now the creator sets the promotion share, the way people do: try a little more, look at the numbers, keep going if they went up, turn back if they went down. They look every τ days, and what they watch is a goal signal G. This creator checks daily and watches only views: the page's starting place.

G = (1 − g)·V/V₀ + g·½·(C/C₀ + W/W₀) every τ days: if mean G rose, keep going; else turn back. u += h·direction
The goal weight g says how much of what they watch is views and how much is community and wellbeing, each read against where this creator started. τ is the delay between looks. h is how big a step they take.

Three knobs, the same nudge

Three places to push. A parameter (#12): the views each post earns, raised by better thumbnails. A delay (#9): days between looks. The goal (#3): how much of what you watch is community and wellbeing. The same effort means the same share of each knob's range: the yield runs 0.5× to 2×, the delay 1 to 64 days (both on a log scale), the goal 0 to 1. Effort is a convention here, and it is the model's weakest joint: a knob's distance is not what a change costs a person.

The measure, D: how far the four stocks end, over the last 90 days, from where the same 40 creators end with no nudge (same daily lotteries), as a root-mean-square of the relative changes. D = 0.10 means the stocks moved about 10% on average. The dashed line is twice the noise floor: two untouched groups of 40 creators differ by that much on luck alone.

The parameter's change is exact, not luck: with the same daily lotteries, more yield means exactly that many more followers and nothing else. The floor line guards the other two, whose changes run through the loop. It comes from two independent groups of creators, so it overstates the luck in a same-lottery comparison; read it as a caution, not a cut.

The test

Stated before the first trial. For each effort size, across 64 draws of the model's six free constants (recovery, burnout, learning, fading, the views' noise, the step size, each 0.5× to 2×), take Q = the goal's D per unit effort ÷ the parameter's D per unit effort.
The core insight holds in this model if Q ≥ 3 in at least 80% of draws. It fails in this model if Q ≤ 1 in at least half. Anything between is mixed.
