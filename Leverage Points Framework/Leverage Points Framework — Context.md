# Leverage Points Framework — Context

[[Leverage Points Framework]] entered git on 2026-03-25 in the restructure that brought in the 4 Pillars artifacts (`46d904f`). It arrived as a working document of about 440 lines, written in the second person to Loudon. Its section titles show the register: "Real Examples from Your Practice", "How You Succeed", "Teaching This to Your Community". Its body has been cut once, hard, and not touched since.

## What the shortening took out

The 2026-05-28 audit's shortening pass took the page from 441 lines to 146 and kept all twelve points (`b41c7c5`). The pass was told to cut padding and stay careful where the content is the entry's purpose. Here, what went was mostly the scaffolding around each point:

- **Each point's lists.** Every point had its own heading, an Examples list and an "Our approach" list of rules and routines. These were folded into prose, and the twelve points were paired into tiers. The detailed routines behind the points went with the lists: the weekly schedule, the dashboard and its trigger threshold, the written rules of the personal constitution. Only a few survive in passing.
- **The six-step Application Framework.** It became one paragraph.
- **One of three worked decisions.** "How do I grow my audience faster?" was cut. The Instagram and brand examples stayed, shortened.
- **Three motivational sections.** "Why Most Creators Fail", "How You Succeed" and "The Social Media Strategy as Leverage Point Intervention" were folded into the platform section.
- **The teaching plan.** "Teaching This to Your Community" laid out how the weekly 4 Pillars content would teach leverage points, starting with the Kick Drum Paradox as week one. It was cut whole, with no destination named. The one propagation line that remains came from the next section, The Meta-Leverage of This Framework, shortened. The Kick Drum Paradox itself lives on in [[FOUR PILLARS]] and its Context.

The vector was written on 2026-04-07 (`1c96439`), before the shortening. It asks for one worked example from each of the four pillars. The shortening took the worked examples from three to two, which is the opposite direction. Nobody has taken up the vector's request in the body since.
