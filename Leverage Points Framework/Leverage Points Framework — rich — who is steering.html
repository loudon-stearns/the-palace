Leverage Points Framework · #9 Delays · a model, not a measurement

Who is steering

Delays (#9), as the page has it: instant feedback (likes, views) hijacks slower, more valuable feedback (student growth, wellbeing), and the intervention is temporal discipline: weekly reflection, monthly assessment, no daily metric checking. Here one creator steers by two signals at once, and we watch which one does the steering. The creator, the stocks and the steering rule are the same model as Same effort, three places, beside the core insight. Every number is this model's.

One signal, no delay

First the creator watches only the slow thing: community and wellbeing, read the moment they change. Every day they nudge their promotion share, keep going if it helped, turn back if not.

slow signal L = ½·(C/C₀ + W/W₀) every τ = 1 day: if mean L rose, keep going; else turn back
L is community and wellbeing, each against where this creator began, averaged. The creator can feel the true direction, so they walk to where it is good.
promotion share uslow signal L ÷ 3

Put a delay on the slow signal

Growth in students and wellbeing show up late. The creator now sees L as it was some days ago, and acts on old news.

perceived L(t) = L(t − Dₛ)
The slow signal reaches the creator Dₛ days late.

Add the fast signal beside it

Now views come in too, every day, noisy and instant. The creator cares about both: half of what they steer by is views, half the slow signal, 30 days late.

G = (1 − g)·V/V₀ + g·L(t − Dₛ) g = how much of what they watch is the slow signal
With g = 0.5 the weights are equal. The model is not: views answer posting the same day and in proportion, while community and wellbeing take months to move. That difference in timescale is what the page means by instant.
promotion share uslow signal L ÷ 3

Who decided?

Each look ends in a decision: keep going or turn back. For each one, ask what the views alone would have decided, and what the slow signal alone would have. A decision that ignored a signal agrees with it half the time, by chance.

Stated before the first trial. Chance level: 0.5 agreement. The creator is hijacked when both hold: (1) their decisions agree with the views at least 0.20 more often than with the slow signal, and (2) their slow composite K (community and wellbeing over the last year) ends at or below 90% of what the same creator reaches steering by the slow signal alone, with the same looks and the same delay.

Temporal discipline, scored by the same rule

The page's intervention: weekly reflection, monthly assessment, no daily metric checking. Two arms, scored by the same hijack rule. In the first, the creator only looks less often, every 7 or 30 days, and views keep half the weight. In the second, views are also demoted to 1% of what the creator watches (g = 0.99), and the creator looks daily or weekly.

The demoted arm. Its rule was written after the challenger's numbers were known and before this sweep was run. Temporal discipline with the metric demoted works in this model if weekly looks are not hijacked in at least 80% of 64 draws (20 creators each) while daily looks are hijacked in at least half, so that the cadence, not the demotion alone, does the lifting. It fails if weekly looks are hijacked in at least half.
