The question this asks the page

Put Third alone on the drawbars and listen to the bent curve, then the stack. Same note, same colour: one object read two ways. Now pull the reach down. Only the curve changes, and the third harmonic comes out mostly as the first. Is the reach a seam in the identity, or a knob the curve has that the stack never will?

Identity, because the two readings are one equation under x = cos θ: the null is exact to the last bit of a double, for any drawbars. The reach is not a crack in that. It moves the reader off the circle, out of the coordinate where the two are one, and what it hands back is a control the stack of cosines does not have. This is the maker's verdict; your sit may overturn it.
