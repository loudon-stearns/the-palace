The lattice read back

Light a few points on a grid of whole-number pairs. Two generators turn each point into a frequency, m × g₁ + n × g₂, and the torus plays them. Then the analysis is given the sound and nothing else. Its job: find the fewest generators that explain every partial, and lift each partial back onto its point. Does the grid that comes back match the one you drew?

What the proof measures, live

The question this asks the page

At √2 the analysis hands back your points, rank 2. At 3 ÷ 2 the two rows fall onto one harmonic series and the rank drops to 1: the torus line closed. Now try 7 ÷ 5 and slide whole numbers up to slowly down: somewhere the rank jumps from 1 to 2. Nothing in the sound changed, and the rank changed. Then light the Diagonal field and see your points come back counted along different steps. Is the rank a property of the sound, or of the sound and how much you are willing to count?

Identity, for the lattice. What comes back is the set of points that made the sound, at the frequencies that made it. Two things qualify it. The generators come back only up to a change of basis: (g₁, g₂) and (g₁, g₂ − g₁) draw the same lattice, and the analysis may pick either, so it recovers the lattice, not the hand that drew it. And the rank is measured against a bound: with whole numbers allowed to grow without limit, every finite set of peaks is rank 1 with a small enough fundamental. The page says the rank is “a property of the source”. The proof says it is a property of the source and a bound you choose. This is the maker’s verdict; your sit may overturn it.
