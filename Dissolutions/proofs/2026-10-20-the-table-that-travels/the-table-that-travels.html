The table that travels

One stored cycle, kept two ways. A wavetable holds the cycle still and moves its reader across it. A delay loop moves the cycle and holds its reader still: the samples travel round and pass a tap. Same relative motion, same sound. Then put a filter in the loop, and watch which of the two can follow.

What the proof measures, live

The question this asks the page

With the loop filter at none, play their difference: silence, for as long as you like. Then turn the filter up. The table cannot follow, because a table has nowhere to put a filter; the loop takes it on every trip, and its partials walk off the grid to exactly where the equation sends them. Is the wavetable a loop with its filter slot left empty, or is the filter what makes the loop a different thing?

- 2026-10-20 · wavetable oscillator / lossless waveguide loop · identity · knob: the loop filter's phase response, a stored dispersion curve that moves the partials off the harmonic grid to where the round-trip condition puts them · Dissolutions/proofs/2026-10-20-the-table-that-travels/

Identity, because with nothing in the loop the two give the same samples, not nearly the same: the difference is zero, exactly, at every sample. The identity is strict about one thing. A period that is not a whole number of samples already needs a filter in the loop, so it is exact only when the loop is a bare delay. That edge is where the knob begins: the slot a fractional length forces open is the slot the dispersion curve goes in. This is the maker's verdict; your sit may overturn it.
