The equation read backward

One line: today’s sample is a weighted sum of the last few, plus whatever that sum missed. Know the voice, and the line gives you the miss, the residual. Know the residual, and the same line, with the same numbers, gives you the voice back. A made voice sings ah, ee, oo so you can hear both directions.
