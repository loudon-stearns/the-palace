One curve, three doorways

One stored curve and one knob. Three readers take turns with it. If the hub is right, the knob should feel like one thing heard three ways, not three knobs that happen to share a formula.

5.0 convex
f(t, c) = ( e−c·t − e−c ) ÷ ( 1 − e−c )
(curve height) = ( e−(curvature) × (where the reader is) − e−(curvature) ) ÷ ( 1 − e−(curvature) )
Curvature 0 is the 0 ÷ 0 case. Its limit is the straight line, and every doorway goes plain there at once.

Envelope

A slow ramp reads the curve once per note. You hear it as the loudness of a pluck.

t = (time since the note) ÷ (note length)
read once, start to end, about every 1.2 seconds

Oscillator

A ramp reads the same curve 110 times a second. Now the curve is the waveform itself.

t = (110 × time) wrapped to 0…1
read start to end, 110 times a second

Effect

A sine wave is the reader now. It swings back and forth across the curve, as far as its level reaches. The curve is a waveshaper.

t = (1 + x) ÷ 2, x = L × sin(2π × 110 × time)
(where it reads) = (1 + input) ÷ 2, input = (level) × (a 110 Hz sine)
