### 2026-11-15 — A four-panel Kuramoto figure at 300 dpi, on Graphite and on paper

Three things the card had not tried: a multi-panel publication figure, 300 dpi, and the house style on a ground other than Graphite. Panel (a) is how in step 2000 oscillators get as the coupling rises, the simulation beside the textbook curve; (b) to (d) are where their phases sit at three couplings. The simulation lands within 0.0007 of theory once coupling is well above the threshold. Every label is inside the frame, every bit of text is JetBrains Mono, and a rerun gives the same bytes. Matplotlib 3.11 is out; it drew the same figure, after a stale font cache first made it fall back to a default face without a word.

**Nobody but its maker has seen these.** The question for your eye: does the paper figure read as a page you would print, and do the pale middle dots in (b) to (d) disappear on paper?
