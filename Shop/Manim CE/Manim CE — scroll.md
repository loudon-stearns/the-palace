### 2026-11-15 — Beats: a teaching clip made by three Specialists, each for what it does best

Seventeen seconds. Manim draws the mathematics: two waves a little apart in pitch (2.00 and 2.25 Hz), their sum swelling and fading inside a dashed envelope, a brace over one beat, then the identity that explains it. Kokoro reads one sentence in af_heart: *"Add two waves a little apart in pitch, and their sum swells and fades: it beats exactly as often as their frequencies differ."* ffmpeg joins them at the house loudness. Nobody has watched or heard it.

**The seams, named.**
- *Kokoro → Manim: the narration is the clock.* Kokoro hands back its own word timings, and every Manim cue is read from them (`cue("sum")`, `cue("swells")`…), so a new sentence re-times the drawing with no hand edits. No Whisper pass: these are the timings Kokoro planned, not measured ones. Measured afterwards on the joined file, the voice starts at 1.51 s; planned was 1.2 s of lead plus Kokoro's own 0.31 s breath before "Add". The seam held.
- *Manim → ffmpeg: a silent render, copied untouched.* 1920×1080, 30 fps, 17.000 s, the video stream copied, not re-encoded.
- *Kokoro → ffmpeg: loudness once, at the end.* Kokoro's raw 24 kHz read (−25.5 LUFS) is delayed 1.2 s, padded to the video's length, resampled to 48 kHz, and two-pass loudnormed **on the joined track**, not per Specialist, so nothing is normalized twice: −16.1 LUFS, −1.0 dBTP, both inside the house's numbers.
- *A trap at the Manim seam.* Manim's `-qh` renders 1080p at **60** fps, not the 30 its card says; the clip is rendered `-qh --frame_rate 30`. Owed a review on [[Manim CE — tuning]].

**Measured.** Kokoro: 6.0 s to load, 1.3 s to read 8.25 s of speech. Manim at Study (1080p30, TeX warm): 21 s for 17 s of video. ffmpeg: 0.6 s to measure, 0.9 s to apply and mux. Load average on the machine was near 100 on 10 cores: other sessions were rendering, so the times are upper bounds.

**A question for your eye and ear.** Watch it with the sound on: does each wave arrive on its word ("two waves", "sum", "swells"), or does the drawing feel early or late against the voice?

### 2026-11-15 — Manim's first 4K60: the beats clip at four times the pixels and twice the frames

The card said 4K and 60 fps had never been rendered. The beats teaching clip's own scene, unchanged, rendered at `-qk`: 3840×2160, 60 fps, 1018 frames, 17 s. The same voice is joined under it, copied from the 1080p30 clip. Nobody has watched it.

**Measured.** 217 s to render, against 21 s for the 1080p30 clip: about ten times the cost, with the machine's load average near 100 for both. The file is small, 2.9 MB silent, because the picture is mostly flat dark ground. In a still crop of the busiest moment, enlarged, the 1080p30 frame (upper) and the 4K frame (lower) differ only in how fine the thin lines are.

**Found on the way.** Manim's `-qh` renders 1080p at **60** fps, not the 30 the card says. The 2026-05-30 Study and Piece were 30 only because their source sets `config.frame_rate = 30`. So 60 fps had in fact been rendered once this morning, by accident. Owed a review on [[Manim CE — tuning]].

**A question for your eye.** On the screen you will publish to, does the 4K60 clip look better than the 1080p30 one by enough to pay ten times the render?

### 2026-11-15 — Old against new: Manim 0.20.1 against 0.22.1, and LaTeX against Typst

Manim 0.21.0 (2026-08-10) added Typst math, which needs no TeX at all, and claimed a Cairo renderer about 2.2 times faster; 0.22.1 (2026-10) is the newest. Installed beside the old one (`~/.venvs/manim-0.22`, with its `typst` extra); the palace's 0.20.1 was not touched. Same machine, same source, run one after the other.

**Typst is the real gain.** The beats clip's two equations, set cold: MathTex through the TeX bridge took 57.8 s; MathTypst took 3.0 s, with TeX taken off the PATH entirely. That removes the page's two worst gotchas at once, the dvisvgm path bridge and the cold LaTeX cost, for anything Typst can set. The Typst row's fractions are larger only because I wrote `\tfrac` for TeX and a plain `/` for Typst.

**The speed claim did not show here.** Drawing the beats clip at 1080p30 with caching off, three runs each, alternated: medians 5.2 s on 0.20.1 and 4.9 s on 0.22.1. Within the noise of a loaded machine (load near 45). The scene is mostly static lines; the changelog's 2.2 times was measured on animation-heavy scenes.

**Same picture, not the same bytes.** The two versions' renders differ in bytes and agree to SSIM 0.9995.

**A question for your eye.** In the still, do the Typst equations (lower) read as well as the LaTeX ones (upper) for a Loudon Live clip?
