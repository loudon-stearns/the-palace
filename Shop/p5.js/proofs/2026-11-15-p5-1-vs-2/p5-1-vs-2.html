One sketch, two p5s: 1.10.0 (the shelf) beside 2.4.0 (npm latest)

A closed curve leans toward your pointer, then a warp shader ripples it by how far right the pointer is. Each panel runs the same idea in its own version's words. Both are still until you press start.

The question: do the two panels look and move the same to you, and if one is better, which one, and what do you see?
