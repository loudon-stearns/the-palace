A field you press — WebGL mode, mouse and finger alike

Press start, then move the mouse over the field, or put one or more fingers on it on a phone or tablet. The boxes under you sink and warm, and spring back when you leave.

The question: does a finger press the field the way the mouse does, the same weight and no lag, with the page holding still under your finger? (On the Mac, the mouse half alone is still worth a look.)

still. One frame drawn.

Measured headless by check.mjs (measured.json and screenshots beside this file): that the canvas is WebGL2, that the mouse presses the boxes under it, that two fingers press two places at once, and that a finger dragged on the field does not scroll the page while one dragged on this text does. How it feels in the hand is yours.
