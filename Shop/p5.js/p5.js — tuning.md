# p5.js — tuning

What each job taught the p5.js page. Each item names what the job showed and what changed because of it, if anything did. Newest last. These are the page's dated gotchas, moved here on 2026-11-08 when its Gotchas became present-tense rules. The page's `last_tested` date, the Shop harness's smoke on 2026-06-16, is kept as the first run line.

## From the two-phasor coupling explorer — 2026-05-10 — Sketch, the first job

1. **A background tab throttled `requestAnimationFrame` to about 1 Hz, and the simulation looked frozen.** With `dt = clamp(now - lastTime, 0.05)`, a Kuramoto-style integrator advanced only ~0.05 s of simulation time per second of wall-clock; to a programmatic browser driver or a screenshot flow that reads as a hung sketch. The clamp is right, guarding against a catastrophic first frame; the visible behaviour misleads. Changed: two mitigations named — drive the simulation off a `setInterval` timer when verification needs it, or say in the Sketch that focus is required. Sketch tier accepts the throttle as a known limit. Item 3 later set the timer aside for animated sketches.
2. **The first canvas under-used its vertical space.** At 1080×480, phasors at y=90 and the trace baseline at y=320 left ~70 px empty below the trace axis. Changed: cosmetic at Sketch; flagged for promotion — at Study or Piece, re-aspect the canvas or move readouts and labels into the unused band.

## From the Flocking expressive Sketch — 2026-05-29 — the second p5 job

3. **A headless harness paused `requestAnimationFrame` outright, not just throttled it.** In the Claude Preview static-server harness for [[Flocking]], rAF callbacks never fired (a probe counter stayed at 0). p5's `draw()` loop is rAF-driven, so the canvas showed only `setup()`'s background. Exposing the instance (`window.__flock = p` in `setup()`) and forcing frames with `__flock.redraw()` pumped 450 frames, and the flock rendered correctly. Changed: a sketch that must be verified headlessly gets a labelled redraw seam, not a `setInterval` loop, which would degrade trail smoothness on real tabs. The sibling [[Observable Plot]] sketch keeps `setInterval`, rightly: it is a slow analytical re-render, not smooth animation.
4. **Fading trails: veil, don't clear — confirmed again.** A low-alpha full-canvas `rect` each frame (`fill(h,s,b,14)` in HSB) left the fading trail; `background()` or `clear()` each frame destroys it. Trail length depends on framerate: the veil accumulates differently at 30 fps than at 60. Changed: nothing new beyond the note that trail length moves if a sketch goes to a fixed-rate timer.
