### 2026-11-15 — The mount block, and two edges that pass without a word

The Haptic Handwheel's mount block, untried until now: an 80 mm square plate with a bore and four bench holes, drawn on a sheet with its checks. Six of six checks pass and a rebuild gives the same bytes. The motor's bolt pattern has never been measured, so the sheet says HOLD in red and no printable file is written.

Two edge cases: a ring at zero clearance, and one that interferes, both build without complaint; a view at three times scale spills off the sheet, also without complaint. And on the real sheet, the label for the four holes covers one of them.

**Nobody but its maker has seen these.** The question for your eye: on the mount block sheet, can you find all four bench holes, and does the red HOLD read clearly enough to stop you sending it to the printer?
