What had to change to move the same scene from r128 to r187:

  1. The loading. There is no classic script any more: examples/js went in r148 and build/three.min.js in r161. r187 comes in as an ES module through an import map, and THREE.OrbitControls becomes an import from three/addons/.
  2. Nothing in the scene code. The function that builds the scene is the same text for all three panes.
  3. The look, unless you ask for the old one. Since r152 three manages colour (a hex is read as sRGB and written back as sRGB) and since r155 its lights are physical (a point light falls off with distance). The box sets the four things that undo it: ColorManagement.enabled = false, outputColorSpace = LinearSRGBColorSpace, every light's intensity times pi, and the point light's decay = 0. Unticked, you see what happens when an r128 recipe's light numbers are copied into r187: the knot goes dark.
  4. The WebGPU pane also waits for await renderer.init() before its first frame, and turns the materials into node materials by itself.

What a script measured (check.mjs, headless Chromium, 2026-11-15, where WebGPU found no adapter, so the third pane ran on its WebGL 2 fallback): unticked, the knot in r187 was less than half as bright as in r128 (mean luma 33 against 71 of 255); ticked, the three frames agreed to within 2 of 255 on average, compared as 32 by 24 cells, too coarse to see a thin line drawn differently. The numbers are in measured.json beside this file. On a Mac with WebGPU the third pane runs on WebGPU itself, and nobody has looked at that, or at any of this, yet.
