# Probe: a Live Set from a made thing (2026-10-20 → 21)

The ableton-session session of the 2026-10-20 fan-out, in the Shopkeeper's workshop. Loudon's commission: "hand me fully ready Ableton sessions so that all I need to do is open the Ableton session and the result of their work is there ready to play." What is here: the writer (`live-set.mjs`), its templates with their sources named, a ride recipe for the frustration model, the interface for the two consumer sessions (`INTERFACE.md`), three Sets under `out/` with the captures and logs of their proof, and the Specialist draft at `Shop/Shopkeeper/drafts/Ableton Session Writer — draft.md`.

## The prior art, and what was adopted

GoButton (Loudon's show-control repository `GoButton Local`, a sibling folder of the palace, commit `555c96b`) holds the only Live-Set-writing craft on this Mac: `tools/v2-convert.js` (§ 2 THE XML: clone a track and its clips from the set's own track, renumber every pointee id from `NextPointeeId`, never overwrite, then `v2-check` and open in Live 12), `v2/device/showset.js` (the reader), `tools/v2-timecode.js` and `v2-colour-clips.js` (the same move on other elements), with the file lessons in `docs/lessons-files.md`. Its proof discipline is Loudon's: open the copy in Live, read the log, speak in bars. GoButton edits copies of saved sets; it never makes a device, a sample reference or a plugin, so those are the seam authored here. In the palace, the one earlier Live-XML seam is `Projects/Generative Preset Development/ableton-wavetable/wavetable_writer.py` (read a gzipped `.adv`, change one value, gzip back; Loudon confirmed a changed preset loads in Live). The Max pause of 2026-10-16 carves out "Ableton sets, racks and presets of Ableton's own devices", so a Set of Live's own Sampler is open ground; no `.amxd` was touched or read as a device.

Adopted whole: gzip in and out; clone what Live wrote and change only what must change; the pointee rule and its regex; never overwrite; the check tool after every write; the CONTENTS header and numbered sections in the script. Authored: the blank set from Live 12's own `DefaultLiveSet.als`, the Sampler and Limiter from Live 12.4's Core Library defaults, the MIDI track, VST3 device, MIDI clip and sample part shells (taken from sets Live 12.4.6 saved, emptied by `make-templates.mjs`, nothing personal left in them), the SFZ reader, the WAV reader, the JUCE parameter-id and class-id rules, the JUCE state block, and the two jobs.

## What was made and how it was proven

Three Sets, each read back by `live-set.mjs check` (unique pointee ids below `NextPointeeId`, every sample on disk, the plugin installed, the device off until played, 100 bpm, Arrangement view) and then opened in a **second Live 12 Suite 12.4.6 instance** (`open -n`, so the set Loudon had open in his Live 12 and the 860 show in his Live 11 were never touched), played ten seconds from a per-process key event, stopped, and quit. Captures and logs are in `out/proof/`.

| Set | Opened | Played | What the capture shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `out/Simple Synth.als` (the VST Template's other plugin, a clean-signed install) | yes — Live's log: "plugin processor successfully loaded … Restored: Simple Synth", so the JUCE state block the writer makes is one the plugin accepts | yes — transport at 1.3.2 with Tune already sweeping (−17.19 st), lanes on Tune and Pitch env | `simple-synth-device-view.png`: the device with its eight configured parameters, the three named section clips, 100 bpm |
| `out/Frustration.als` | yes — "Loaded document was created by Ableton Live 12.4.6"; but "VST3: Restore 1 failed: Frustration" and the device reads "The VST3 plug-in could not be found" | the transport ran the four sections; no sound, the device had no plugin | `frustration-plugin-not-found.png` |
| `out/Tear and Heal.als` (four instruments, 240 samples) | yes — no missing-file warning in Live's log | yes — the first track's meter lit, playhead at 6.1.1 after ten seconds | `tear-and-heal-playing.png` |

**Why the Frustration plugin was not found.** Live's scanner (`~/Library/Preferences/Ableton/Live 12.4.6/PluginScanner.txt`, 23:50:08) logged "Failed to load plugin: a sealed resource is missing or invalid / VST3: not a plugin" for `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Frustration.vst3`. `codesign --verify --deep --strict` confirms it: the bundle was signed before JUCE's manifest step added `Contents/Resources/moduleinfo.json`; the built copy in the VST Template session's worktree fails the same way, and the AU copy says "code has no resources but signature indicates they must be present". Simple Synth, built by the same machinery at 23:32, verifies clean. The fix is in that session's build or install step (sign after the manifest), then a rescan in Live; the VST Template session was told (message of 00:05). This session's own attempt to re-sign the installed copy was refused by its permissions and was not retried. The Set itself is right: once a clean-signed Frustration is installed and scanned, Live will load it as it loaded Simple Synth.

**Two faults found and fixed on the way.** The blank set's Main track carries a tempo automation lane at 120 that Live shows over the mixer's tempo; the writer now sets both. A device cloned from an `.adv` preset has no `Id`; a device in a chain carries its position as `Id`; the writer adds it.

## What stayed silent, and what is not proven

- Nobody has listened. Live played both Sets through MacBook speakers for ten seconds each while the screen was locked; whether the Sampler's loops sit right and whether the ride through the frustration model says what the field guide says is Loudon's ear. The Sampler device view was not captured (the keystroke that should have toggled it opened Live's Settings window instead — nothing was changed there).
- The ride for Frustration (`frustration.live-set.json`) was drawn from the field guide's words, not heard: hold under the edge, ride coupling past it, breathe across it on a log scale, four strikes. Its numbers are the sweeps' (edge near 1.8e-5 at 40 partials and 6 Hz), on the plugin's defaults (40 partials, warm source, partial 1 free).
- Drum Rack and Simpler are unbuilt; AU is untargeted; macros are not used (automation lanes are).

## How Live was driven without touching Loudon's

Both Lives share the bundle id `com.ableton.live`; the desktop's app-control tool, asked for Live 12, bound to Live 11 (the 860 show, a live production), so it was released and never used on Live. Instead: `open -n -a "Ableton Live 12 Suite" <set>` (Live logs "Another instance of the same Live version is running" and carries on), `windows.swift` to find the new instance's window, a brief switch to its Space, `screencapture`, `postkey.swift` (a key event posted to that process only: space, then cmd+Q), and the previous app brought back. Each proof held the screen for about fifteen seconds. Loudon's own Live 12 (Untitled, with a Max device) and Live 11 (860_10OCT26) were left exactly as they were.

## 2026-10-21 00:16 — the Frustration Set loads its plugin

After the VST Template session re-sealed the installed bundles (its commit `aa51252c`) and Live had rescanned, `out/Frustration.als` was opened once more in a second Live 12.4.6 instance (`out/proof/prove-open.sh`: open, one capture, quit; no playback). Live's log, 00:16:02: "VST3: Going to restore: Frustration" → "VST3: plugin processor successfully loaded: Loudon Live 'Frustration' v0.1.0 (cid: {ABCDEF01-9182-FAEB-4C6F-756446727374})" → "VST3: Restored: Frustration". The Set was not changed between the failed opens and this one, which settles the cause as the bundle's seal, not the device block. `out/proof/frustration-restored.png` shows it open at 100 bpm with no "plug-ins disabled" banner; the instance quit three seconds after cmd+Q, Loudon's own Live 12 and Live 11 untouched. Still unheard: that Live 12 instance ran with no audio device, and nobody has played the ride.
