The whole d3 library against just the modules it needs

Since 2026-11-03 every d3 module ships as one ES-module file, so a piece can load only the parts it uses straight from the CDN, hashed and pinned, with no build step. Left: the shelf's whole d3@7.9.0, one script. Right: only d3-force 3.0.1, d3-selection 3.0.1 and the three small modules d3-force leans on, the files d3 7.9.2 is built from. Both draw the same 120-node graph from the same seed. Nothing moves until you press the button.

The question for you: press Draw both. Do the two graphs look the same to you as they settle, and does either one feel slower?
