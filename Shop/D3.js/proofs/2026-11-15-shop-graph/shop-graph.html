The Shop as a relaxed graph — d3-force doing its own job

Every page in the Shop folder, and every page its frontmatter links to, joined by the typed links the pages declare (…). Nothing moves until you press Relax. Then d3-force lets the graph settle: links pull, nodes push apart, and the layout cools to rest. Drag a node and it settles again.

The question for you: once it has settled, can you find your way round the Shop by eye? Pick one Specialist you know and say whether its neighbours sit where you would expect them.

still: nodes at d3-force's starting spiral

Keyboard: Tab into the graph, ←↑→↓ move to the nearest node that way, Enter or Space shows its links, ] and [ walk through its neighbours, a letter jumps to the next name starting with it, Esc lets go.
