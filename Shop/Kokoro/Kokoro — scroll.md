### 2026-11-15 — RNBO, three ways

Each is loudnormed to −16 LUFS and −1 dBTP, and each landed at −16.0 LUFS, −1.0 dBTP. Nobody has heard these.

Measured beside it: the letter-by-letter take was rendered twice in one process. Same length, not the same samples; the largest difference between the two was 0.12 of full scale, so two renders of one sentence can be told apart by a script, if not by an ear.

The question: **which of the three is how you say RNBO, or is it none of them?** If none, say it aloud once and the next take writes it in.
