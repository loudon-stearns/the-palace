How many grains before it crackles?

Each voice here is one of Murmuration's grains: a wavetable read at four corners and blended, under a Hann window, panned. Murmuration plays 60. This page starts at 16 and adds more on a click. The figure under the buttons is how much of each audio block's time the voices took, read by a clock inside the audio thread.

The question: add voices until you hear it crackle, stutter or drop out. At what voice count does it start? Press "it crackles here" at that count.
