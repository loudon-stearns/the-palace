The same grains, in blocks of 128 or bigger

The audio thread has always handed process() 128 frames at a time. A newer Chrome lets a page ask for bigger blocks: fewer wake-ups for the same sound, at the cost of a little more delay. Here is one seeded cloud of Murmuration's grains, the old way (128) or a new way (256, 512, 1024). Changing the block size restarts the sound from the same seed.

The question: raise the voices until 128 crackles. Then, at that same count, switch to 512. Does the crackle stop?
