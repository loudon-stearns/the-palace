Observable Plot 0.6.17 against 0.6.19 — the same three charts

The shelf pins Plot 0.6.17 (February 2025). Plot 0.6.18 came out on 2026-11-03 and 0.6.19, a packaging fix, two days later. Each chart below is written once and drawn by whichever version you pick. Each uses something 0.6.18 added, so under 0.6.17 that part is quietly ignored or drawn differently. The data is the seed-7 flock at three alignment weights.

One question: switch back and forth. Is the 0.6.19 drawing plainly easier to read than the 0.6.17 one, enough that the shelf should move to it?

1 · ridgeline — areaY with line: true

0.6.18 lets the area mark draw its own line on top and lightens the fill. 0.6.17 has no such option and draws a solid area.

2 · three lines, three tips, one legend: true

Hover over the right half where the lines bunch. 0.6.18 shows only the nearest tip; 0.6.17 shows one per line, stacked. The plot-level legend: true is new too; 0.6.17 ignores it.

3 · a line coloured by its own value — stroke: "R"

0.6.18 stops splitting a line into one-point pieces when the colour channel repeats the y channel. Under 0.6.17 it breaks into dots, and Plot shows its warning sign.
