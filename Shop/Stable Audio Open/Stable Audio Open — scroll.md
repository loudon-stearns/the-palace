### 2026-11-14 — Arc or texture? A blind test of what the models hold over time

The Daily Page put the old forward vector to the test: can Stable Audio 3's small models render an arc, a sound that changes over time because the prompt says so, or only a texture? Four matched pairs, each an arc prompt and a steady twin on the same model, seed and length (20 s), plus a labelled practice pair and one 60 s render. Loudon heard them blind and called each arc or steady. Every clip was also measured: a loudness envelope, and brightness in the first and last quarter.

Four of the eight counted prompts were honored. small-sfx dropped its arcs: the rain asked to build into a downpour stayed flat within 0.5 dB, and the pulses asked to lock into one rhythm stayed flat within about 3 dB. small-music gave every clip an ending: the "static" pad and drone faded 35–40 dB, and its arc prompts came back as a swell and a fade. Loudon's ear matched the actual sound on all eight; his misses were the model's. The 60 s small-music render held together past the page's old ~47 s ceiling and, in his words, "even added a nice change in harmony".

What it changed: the page's vector (keep trying new kinds of audio; know which shapes it can hold and which must be built around it), its Charter's best-for and not-yet-shown, two gotchas under Choosing a model, the stale SAO 1.0 limits, replaced by SA3's, and a plan to try more kinds of audio.
