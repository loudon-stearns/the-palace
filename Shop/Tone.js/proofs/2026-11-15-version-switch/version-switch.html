The phrase from the Transport proof: four chords on a triangle8 PolySynth through a delay, reverb and limiter. Pick a version, then play its two renders. Each version renders the phrase first at 44.1 kHz, then at 48 kHz.

Play 15.0.4's first render, then its second. Without the numbers, would you have heard that the second one is out of tune?

Measured headless (check.mjs beside this page), and again here each time you play a render: the pitch of the first chord's A3, which should be 220 Hz. In 15.0.4 and 15.1.22 the 48 kHz render plays at about 240 Hz, 1.5 semitones sharp. In 15.5.72 both renders are at 220 Hz. Rendered at the same rate, with the reverb's noise seeded alike, the three versions make the same sound: they differ by -140 dB, rounding error. The table fills in as you play.
