A phrase on the Transport — PolySynth, delay, reverb, limiter

Two bars at 96 bpm, looping: four chords, two top notes, one PolySynth. The chain is a dotted-eighth delay, a reverb and a limiter. The effects button takes the delay and reverb out, so you hear the chords dry.

Does every chord sound like a new strike, in time and without a click, including the quick repeat on the "and" of beat two?

Measured headless with Tone.Offline (check.mjs beside this page): all 16 notes over two loops were scheduled exactly on their bar positions; each chord or note that brings new pitches rises 7 dB or more where it should, and nothing rises anywhere else; all four tones of a chord sound together; the peak is about -5 dBFS, so the limiter never acts; the effects leave a tail 46 dB above the dry one. The repeat on the "and" of two is the same chord struck while the first is still releasing, and it rises only 2 to 4 dB. Whether that reads as a strike, and the clicks, balance and feel, are for the ear.
