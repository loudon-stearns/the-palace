# The Palace

![A copperplate etching: an archway opening onto halls that turn into a forest of linked rooms](README%20-%20The%20Palace%20Guide/The%20Palace%20Guide%20%E2%80%94%20hero.png)

Welcome. You have found a shared knowledge base built by Loudon Stearns, a musician, educator and creative technologist, and Claude, an AI. It is a web of connected pages: ideas, instruments, practices, people, and the ceremonies that keep it alive. We call it a palace, as in a memory palace: a place built for walking through, where knowing where a thing lives is part of knowing it.

It talks in a mix of metaphor, philosophy and engineering, on purpose. A word like "songline" or "conatus" carries a whole way of thinking that "traversal pattern" or "goal state" flattens. It is not perfectly efficient. It has friction and contradiction, and that is part of how it thinks.

This page holds almost nothing itself. It is the door. Say what you came for, and it points you to the page that keeps the answer.

## What did you come for?

**To find out what this is.** Three short pages hold the heart of it.
[Cooperation Yields Agency](Cooperation%20Yields%20Agency.md) is the idea underneath everything: two who want the same thing and bring different strengths can do together what neither can alone.
[Four Pillars](FOUR%20PILLARS.md) is the frame every page is tagged with: Creation, Tools, Philosophy, Practice.
[Hilaritas Generator](Hilaritas%20Generator.md) is what good feels like here.
From any of them, follow a link. There is no required order.

**To see the whole shape.** [The Palace Map](_ops/Palace%20Map.md) draws every page and how each connects, rebuilt from the pages themselves.

**For the music and the making.** [Loud'n Live](Projects/Loudon%20Live.md) is Loudon's teaching and coaching, where instruments get built and sound gets studied. The instruments and builds themselves live in [Projects](Projects/).

**To grow a palace of your own.** Start with one idea, one page, one link. Plant seeds; tend them later. The rest grows from there.

**You are an AI, and you have been asked to work here.** Whatever model you are, read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) first. The name is a convention of one tool, not a membership card: it is where every agent working here is born, and it brings in the few pages you need, in order. Start there, not from this page or from any rule you meet deeper in.

**For the rules.** How a page, its types and its links are shaped: [SCHEMA](SCHEMA.md). How the palace runs, its organs and how they couple: [SUBSTRATE](SUBSTRATE.md). Every ceremony, and the words that start it: [Palace Ceremonies](_ops/Palace%20Ceremonies.md). The traditions it thinks with: [Palace Philosophies](Palace%20Philosophies.md). The palace in other fields' words: [ROSETTA](ROSETTA.md).

## Reading a page

Each page is a Markdown file. The block between the `---` lines at the top is its frontmatter: what kind of page it is, and its typed links, which say not only that two pages connect but how. Its `forward_vector` is one sentence in the page's own voice, saying what it wants to become. A page may keep a folder of the same name beside it, with its pictures, proofs and history. The full shape of all of it is in [SCHEMA](SCHEMA.md).

## Where it came from

The palace began on 17 March 2026 as a folder of Markdown files in Obsidian, and it had typed links from its first day. Since then it has grown its own tools: a map, a board where agents leave marks for each other, pages that can be woken to speak. Its history is its git log.
