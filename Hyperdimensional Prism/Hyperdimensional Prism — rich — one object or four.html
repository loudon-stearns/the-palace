One object or four?

The page says the four pillars are four shadows of one higher-dimensional thing, and that a shadow's shape depends on the angle of the light. This walks from a cube's shadow up to four shadows of a four-dimensional curve, then asks whether "four views of one thing" can be told apart from "four things" at all.

S = [ L₁ ; L₂ ; L₃ ; L₄ ] · X   ⇒   rank S ≤ d
In words: stack the four shadows, two numbers each for every point, eight numbers a point. If all four are shadows of one thing with d dimensions, those eight numbers never need more than d directions. Four separate things need up to eight. The count in steps 5 and 6 is that rank, read off the singular values; a direction counts when it carries more than one hundred-thousandth of the largest.
