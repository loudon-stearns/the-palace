# Hyperdimensional Prism — Context

Why [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] is shaped as it is. Read before editing the page, not to learn the idea; the page does that.

## What the palace asks it to carry

Thirty-nine pages pointed here at the October 2026 weave's open; twenty-six after it. The weave read the weakest of those links one by one (`ed04958f`, the weave's Phase 2b). The real ones come in two kinds. Most cite the page for **one thing, met one projection at a time**: [[FOUR PILLARS]] calls the pillars "a Hyperdimensional Prism", [[SUBSTRATE]] casts the palace four ways and says each cast is "true and partial", and [[Meaning and the Link]], [[Dispersion]] and [[Autodidact Polymaths]] borrow the same shape. A few cite it for the **prism machine**: [[Wallpaper Groups]] and [[Crystal Synthesizer]] are built cases of the page's crystal passage.

What the page is *not* asked to carry is the palace's monism. The weave found six inbound links with no sentence on either side that holds the prism's idea, and several of those pages hand their one-substance claim to Spinoza, whom they already link. The page says "This IS Spinoza's monism applied to pedagogy." [[Spinoza Conatus]] asks the same thing as an open question: "Is Hyperdimensional Prism Spinoza's monism applied to pedagogy?" Keep both. The claim is the Prism's lean; the question belongs to Spinoza's page, where the monism lives. The Taoist reading in [[Palace Philosophies — Context]] adds a third angle: the Prism cannot be described directly, only approached through its projections. So it suits the page to argue by instances and not by definition.

## What it left out

- **The founding conversation.** The Origin section says the model "crystallized during conversations" and stops. The conversation itself is in [[4 Pillars Framework - The Founding Conversation]], which calls the Prism the "later articulation" of what that conversation demonstrated.
- **The DSP mechanism.** The All-Pass Origin names the insight and points to [[Harmonicity and Inharmonicity]] § All-Pass Networks as Inharmonic Resonators for how it works. The page keeps the cascade, not the maths.
- **The gate.** Computational Instantiation gives Mixture of Experts one paragraph; the architecture lives on [[Mixture of Experts]].
- **The formal answer.** Whether the projection can be made precise, and what the light of curiosity is in formal terms, are left as questions. The symmetry-group route is named once, in the crystal passage. Nothing in the palace answers it yet.
- **The patch.** The page says students can download the logo patch. The bundle holds only the hero and the icon.

## What was tried and dropped

The page reached git on 2026-03-17 already holding its opening, Origin, Visual Identity, Spinoza, Cross-Pillar and Computational sections (`abc25921`). The changes since then are mostly additions, plus a few retreats:

- **Its scope narrowed as it grew instances.** On 2026-03-24 (`4169e6ad`) "the meta-model that unifies Loudon's entire framework" became "entire four pillars framework". The same commit added the kaleidoscope, crystal and wallpaper section and the links to [[Trickster]] and [[Compressor Design]]. The All-Pass Origin followed on 2026-05-02 (`c4f30b53`), and with it the claim that the kaleidoscope came out of filter design, not out of the metaphor.
- **The logo's tool changed in one place only.** At first the visual identity was "generated in Max/MSP/Jitter", and from 2026-03-21 Loudon made it in "the same tools he teaches". The 2026-05-28 audit (`1c1b14cc`) changed that to P5.js and cut the "same tools he teaches" clause. The kaleidoscope paragraph still says "the Max/MSP patch". The audit's message gives no reason for the change. Which one is true is Loudon's to say.
- **The forward vector lost a duty.** The first vector (2026-04-21, `76e60f39`) ended "and I name every moment when the same pattern appears in multiple projections simultaneously". Loudon's own tuning on 2026-06-22 (`fac4d0af`) swapped that for "I strive to illuminate the oneness and the distinct perspectives of all entries." The page stopped promising to catalogue every instance and took on holding unity and difference together. The commit says only "vector tuning", and nothing in git records why.
- **Links folded.** The outbound links to [[Kuramoto Coupling]] and [[Loudon Live]] were folded on 2026-10-24 (`0dba352d`) under one link per pair. Both still appear in the body.

## Why it is shaped so

**It argues identity, not analogy.** Three times the page sets "IS" in capitals: the Spinoza claim, "the prism model IS the argument for cross-pillar work", "the interface IS the physics". That is the page's weight. If an edit softens these to "is like", the page loses the one thing it says that its neighbours don't.

**The longest section is the buildable one.** Kaleidoscope, Crystal, and Wallpaper outweighs everything above it, and that weighting is the [[FOUR PILLARS]] trajectory at work: a philosophy here has to be something you can build. The four-level reading of the visual identity, from music producer to peer, is the page's account of a single image read at four depths. [[Autodidact Polymaths]] cites it for exactly that.

**Its seams are accretion, not design.** Origin restates the opening paragraph. The kaleidoscope's teaching paragraph repeats Visual Identity's "students can download it". Both came from separate edits in March 2026. The weave's lighter-prose readings propose keeping one of each.

**It carries three pillars, not four.** Since its first commit it has been tagged philosophy, creation and tools, never practice. That is odd for the page that says the pillars are one thing, and git doesn't say whether it was chosen. The page has been quiet since July. Its prose last changed in the May audit; since then it has had the vector, the hero and some links.
