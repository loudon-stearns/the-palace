The room that settles short

A room on a cold day, 5 °C outside, a heater, a thermometer, and a target of 20 °C. At minute 60 a window opens and stays open. Six steps, one new thing in each: the room alone, then P, then the lag every real room has, then I, then D, and last the page’s own sentence about P put against the room. The page’s example cools; this one heats. The sums are the same with the sign turned.

Two tones: the target, and the room. When they beat, the room is off; the slower the beat, the closer. 24 seconds, four hours of the room.
