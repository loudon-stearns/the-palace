Curvature is timbre

Read the decay curve once per cycle and it is an oscillator. The phasor supplies time; the curve shapes it; curvature becomes the colour of the tone. Four steps: the phasor alone, the phasor through the curve, what the sign of curvature changes, and then the test of the section’s last claim, that steep curves alias and PolyBLEP or oversampling is the fix. The slow side of this, where the same curve is an envelope and the reading speeds up into a pitch, is the Dissolutions page’s piece, the seam. Nothing plays until you press Play.
