Exponential Decay Curvature · the Moog contour · four steps

A capacitor, a comparator, and the curve

The Minimoog’s contour is a capacitor charging through a resistor, with a comparator that says when to stop. Four steps: one capacitor charging, the comparator that ends the stage, the three stages of a note, and then the test: is each stage this entry’s curve, and which member? The voice is the one Synth Templates sat its time tables with: a sawtooth at C3 through a 2500 Hz lowpass. Nothing plays until you press the key.
