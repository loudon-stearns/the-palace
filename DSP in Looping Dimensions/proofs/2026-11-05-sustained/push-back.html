The face found that two plucked loops slide: couple them and their pitches drift toward each other smoothly, with no snap. Plucked loops only ring down, though. These two loops keep themselves going, like a bowed string or a feedback loop, and they hold their own loudness. Turn the knob up slowly and listen for the beating to stop. Then turn it back down and notice where it starts again.
