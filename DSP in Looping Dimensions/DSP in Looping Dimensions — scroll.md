### 2026-11-05 — Push back: two loops that keep themselves going snap, and remember

Two self-sustaining tanh loops, coupled, with the coupling ramped up and then down. Near unison they pull together and stop slipping all at once. At the golden ratio the pair jumps to a new mode on the way up and jumps back at a different place on the way down. Measured on the ramp: up at c ≈ 0.28 (locked by 0.289), down at c ≈ 0.19 (unlocked between 0.193 and 0.187). Loudon heard it at 0.27 and 0.20. Above the jump both loops share one tone at 1077 Hz, near the 5th partial of one and the 8th of the other: they settle on 8 : 5, a Fibonacci ratio close to the golden one. The gap between up and down is the memory only a nonlinear system has. Loops that ring down, by contrast, only slide (the face's snap-or-slide piece).
