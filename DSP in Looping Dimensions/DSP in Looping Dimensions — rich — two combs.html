Where do two combs put their lines?

Frame 2 says two coupled combs at an irrational ratio place their peaks on a 2D frequency lattice, the same spectral signature as scanning a torus. The lattice is every m·f₁ + n·f₂. Four steps, one idea each. The last one measures where the lines fall.

two in a row: H(f) = H₁(f) · H₂(f)
(the pair's response) = (the first comb's) × (the second's). A product of two responses peaks where either one peaks: at k ⁄ T₁ and at l ⁄ T₂. A linear filter only reshapes the frequencies it is given, so nothing appears at a sum or difference of the two.

(what one click becomes) = an echo at every (j × the first delay + k × the second), each weaker by a for every pass through the first comb and b through the second: a 2D lattice, laid out in time.
a = b = 0.95, T₁ = 1 ⁄ 220 s, T₂ = φ ⁄ 220 s.
