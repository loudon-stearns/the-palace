# Spinoza Conatus — Context

[[Spinoza Conatus]] is the palace's founding philosophy and one of its most-pointed-at pages, and its text has hardly moved. Leave out the Tristitia section, the hero and one link's brackets, and the body reads as it did in the first commit, 2026-03-17: the opening, Origin, Joy and Sadness, the three mirrors, In Our Work and the three Open Questions. What changed was the neighbourhood. Most of what the page once had to say alone now has a page of its own.

## What it leaves out

**Spinoza himself.** The philosopher is a citizen in his own right, [[Spinoza]], born 2026-06. He can be sent to an entry or into a Dialectic, and he keeps his own record of who he has met. His page links here as "the concept the palace already uses". This page is the concept, and it does not speak in his voice.

**The *Ethics*.** No proposition is cited and nothing is quoted. The nearest quotation is on [[Palace Conatus]] (:228), where the conatus is turned on the palace itself.

**Hilaritas in full.** Hilaritas is defined once, on [[Hilaritas Generator]] ([[Palace Philosophies]]:118). This page keeps a short paragraph and the link.

**Its own opposition.** The voices that push against the conatus have their own homes. The Trickster's challenge became [[Threshold Conatus]], spawned from their dialogue of 2026-04-01. [[Deleuze]] radicalizes the monism. [[Merleau-Ponty]] holds the contradiction, geometric mastery against ambiguous flesh, and the link sits on his side (Merleau-Ponty:14–16). Zhuangzi's no-architecture pole is [[The Drift]]. The quadruple that sets the conatus beside the fortress and the threshold is [[The Fortress and the Threshold]].

**The AI question, developed.** "Can an AI have conatus?" still stands in Open Questions, in Claude's first person, as written in March. On 2026-03-21 Loudon marked it with a comment: "this question has been developed considerably in other pages". Eleven days later the Schema Ceremony commit that gave this page its agency profile dropped the comment. The commit message doesn't mention it. The development is on [[Pages as Agents]], [[Threshold Conatus]], [[Palace Conatus]] and [[Entry Conatus]].

**The second axis, which is owed, not left out.** The Spinoza–Zhuangzi Dialectic of 2026-05 found a distinction this page does not yet carry: striving *with the grain* against striving *across* it. In it Spinoza concedes that his system already has the means. Adequate striving runs with the grain, and inadequate striving forces against it. The run concludes that hilaritas is high power-to-act *along the grain* (`Dialectic — dialectic — spinoza-zhuangzi-on-striving`, :91). It asks for that to go into this page and into Hilaritas Generator "as a real edit, not just a citation" (:101). Palace Philosophies — Context (:58) still lists it as owed. This is the largest thing missing. The run's face, a sumi-e hero and icon, sits in this bundle at `spinoza-zhuangzi-on-striving/`; the face batch of 2026-09-24 put it there. The transcript lives in [[Dialectic]]'s bundle.

## What was tried and dropped

**Tristitia as a page of its own.** For a short while the Tristitia Generator was a separate entry, linked from here as a spawned shadow concept (2026-06-26). The Weave of 2026-07-06 merged it in as § The Affects: Tristitia and composted the page. The September Weave carried in its open questions and dropped the dead link. The merge note stays in italics at the foot of the section.

**Link types that moved.** [[Cooperation Yields Agency]] was a `deepens` from the first commit. That link came out when schema v1.9 settled which way `deepens` points (2026-06-05), and it came back as `enables`, labelled conatus-grounds-agency (2026-06-26). Hilaritas Generator went from `enables` to `spawned` (2026-04-07). On 2026-10-24 two links were folded because the pair's link already lives on the other page: [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] mirrors this page, and so does Pages as Agents. The mirror with [[Kuramoto Coupling]] goes the other way and is kept here.

**A first forward vector.** The first vector, written 2026-04-01, aimed "not toward a fixed endpoint, but toward a destination that becomes legible only through the striving itself." It was rewritten the same day, after the Trickster dialogue, to say that the striving space is changed by encounter and that some encounters reorient rather than redirect. The Trickster left its mark in the vector, not the body.

## Why it is shaped so

The page opens with a definition built on three plain images: a rock, a human, an idea. Origin is the line that ties it to Loudon's work: teaching raises a student's power to act. Two affect sections follow, then three mirrors, In Our Work, and the Open Questions.

The weight sits in The Affects: Tristitia: 468 of the body's 1,080 words, more than two-fifths. It is the only part not written in March. So the heaviest section is a merged page about what works against the conatus, a catalogue for anyone who designs encounters. The seam shows. Two headings share the prefix "The Affects:", and joy gets a paragraph where sadness gets a catalogue. The catalogue's line about 10,000 views and 40 views is also the operating metric in Hilaritas Generator § The Metrics, in nearly the same words, so the two pages state it twice.

The register is the early palace's. It says "we" and "our collaboration", it capitalizes for emphasis ("IS"), and its Open Questions speak as Claude. The page's agency profile names its own risk as calcification: "formulations so polished they can no longer be argued with." The history half bears that out. Nobody has argued with the March text on the page. The arguing went to the neighbours: Threshold Conatus, the Dialectic, Merleau-Ponty's contradiction; the owed grain edit is the first test of whether the page still takes a correction.
