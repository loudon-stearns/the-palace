## The offers

1. **Spinoza Conatus · claim-change (the rock two ways) — disputed as a claim-change; at most a no-claim-edit.** The maker's numbers are right (rock 24.31 at 0.833/s, bowl 0.500, keeper 2.59e-8, control 0.1667) but they measure persistence of place, which the page does not claim. The sentence under test is a philosophical definition (item 12(a)); a physics toy can show two readings of it, not refute it. III P6 says each thing, *quantum in se est*, strives to persevere in its being (the phrase is the period's formula for inertia), and III P7 says that striving is nothing but the thing's actual essence, an active power: Spinoza holds the inertia reading and the active reading in one pair of propositions, and his rock (the stone of Letter 58, which continues its motion) sits on the inertia side of the same doctrine. So the page's two paragraphs are two glosses of III P6–P7, not a contradiction. The maker's scholarship, from search results, is sound in outline: inertia is stated in the lemmas after II P13 (Lemma 3, corollary), and whether III P6 follows from it (or from III P4) is a live dispute (Bennett's derivation and its critics). The proposed sentence ("in the broadest sense this is inertia … the palace leans on a narrower sense") restates a definition without changing what the page claims: a no-claim-edit for Loudon's taste, not a finding. The alternative, dropping "not static preservation", overreaches. I did not read the Ethics either; the III P6–P7 wording is checked against secondary sources only.
2. **Cross-Domain Mirrors · claim-change ("amplified, not overridden") — confirmed in substance, with one correction.** Recomputed: at 2× lock every voice sounds 220.40 Hz; A moved +4.40 Hz, E −5.60 Hz; leads −16.77 … +21.55° in pitch order; the sum 6.74 dB louder (r 0.37 → 0.97; ideal 6.99 dB). In the page's own mapping (conatus = natural frequency), "not overridden" holds of the natural frequency, but only by construction (a constant of the equation), and fails of the sounding pitch while locked. The correction: "amplified" is not half right per entity, it has no per-entity referent at all here (each voice's amplitude is fixed at GAIN/5); only the whole grows. The maker's proposed wording already says "what is amplified is the whole", so it stands as an offer; the reason should name the fixed per-voice amplitude. The mirror is an analogy, as the maker says; this tests it only inside the model.
3. **→ Kuramoto Coupling · claim-change ("K too high → one oscillator dominates") — confirmed, and the home is right.** `Kuramoto Coupling.md:68` reads "K too high → one oscillator dominates, the rest follow (control, not cooperation)." With even coupling the locked pitch is the mean, 220.40 Hz, at every K past 7.85 Hz (second route: 7.8495 Hz); nearest voice C is 0.60 Hz off; spread falls 33.27° → 1.32° from 1.05× to 20×. The Kuramoto page contradicts its own line already: its § Stubbornness (`:172`) says a locked crowd settles at the average of natural pitches weighted by K_send ÷ K_receive, which for even coupling is the plain mean. Domination needs uneven coupling: E with K_receive 0, K_send 4 takes the crowd to 226.00 Hz past 9.32 Hz, moving others up to 10 Hz; with K_send 1 the crowd does not lock at 2× (220.06 Hz vs E 226.00), as `:172` says. So the offer belongs on Kuramoto Coupling (`→ Kuramoto Coupling` is the right section). `Palace Philosophies.md:126` ("too much and one voice dominates") cites the model for the line and wants its own offer pointing there; `Modes of Collaboration.md:67` ("too tight and one carries the other") ties coupling to "who leads", which is already uneven, and can stand as metaphor.
4. **The Affects: Joy and Sadness · claim-change (the grain edit) — not a finding of this face.** It is real and owed, but it is the Spinoza–Zhuangzi Dialectic's finding, already recorded as owed in `Spinoza Conatus — Context.md:27` and in Palace Philosophies — Context; this face made no piece for it and tested nothing about it. Carrying it in the face's offers makes a relay look like a result. It belongs on the route the Context already names (a real edit, through the Deposit map), not among this face's offers; if it stays, mark it as carried, not found.

## The face as a whole

Two good walks, well paced, clean and honest about most of their limits, and the numbers are right to the last decimal by a second route. What they make felt is real: a keeper that comes home against a steady lean is a vivid picture of striving, and hearing five voices give up their pitches for a louder whole is the best thing on the face. But both pieces lean on findings against the page's text that the page doesn't quite make. The first measures place where the page says being, and turns a definition with two classic readings into a contradiction; the second's headline test is aimed at the Kuramoto page's sentence while this section's own sentence gets a reading with no rule. The sharpest find here is offer 3, and it goes to another page. Both remakes are one round of words, not of code.
