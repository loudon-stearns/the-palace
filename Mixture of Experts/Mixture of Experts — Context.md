# Mixture of Experts — Context

The page was in the palace's first commit, 2026-03-17, as a 68-line concept (`abc2592`). Its shape then was a two-part analogy: neural MoE, and the palace as an MoE system. Everything that makes it a three-substrate page came from one note Loudon left in the margin.

## The margin note

On 2026-03-25 an HTML comment appeared under the title, in Loudon's voice (`46d904f`): he wanted the page related to the [[Excellent Adventure]] and the [[Dialectic]], because "we have a pool of experts we bring to human situations ourselves," by embodying an important person and seeing through their eyes, and by putting such people in dialogue with each other. A typo in it was fixed on 2026-04-21 (`76e60f3`), and it sat there unanswered for five weeks.

The enrichment sweep of 2026-05-02 answered it and deleted it (`c4f30b5`). That one commit is most of the page's present shape. It renamed "The Key Insight from 2024" to Substrate Indifference and pushed it from a question (can an expert be procedural code?) to a claim (MoE is a pattern, not an architecture). It split the body into three substrates and wrote the embodied one, the deep-reading-adds-experts paragraph, and the three open questions that carry embodied load balancing, cross-substrate routing and cross-talk. So the embodied substrate is not a later extension of the analogy. It is Loudon's ask, written out by an agent.

## What it let go

That rewrite dropped three things (`c4f30b5`).

**"Learned" from the gate.** The opening once said the gating mechanism was learned. It became just a gating mechanism, which the curated palace gate and the human gate both need.

**The hybrid frontier as a claim.** The 2024 section went on to say that production MoE still used uniformly neural experts, that the hybrid neural and symbolic MoE was unrealized at scale, and that this was "where the most interesting territory lies": AI reasoning, human judgment, formal specification (TLA+) and code, all experts routed by shared intent, tied to [[Symbiotic Skills]]. The claim went. The TLA+ open question that came with it stayed. If the hybrid vision is still live, that open question is its only trace.

**The prism as the headline.** The second paragraph once called MoE "a precise architectural mirror of both the [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] and the palace itself." It now names substrate indifference instead, and the prism is down to one cross-pillar line.

## The ladder

The Routing Ladder came on 2026-07-06, a second-tier spawn of that day's Multi-Lens Weave, gated by Loudon (`fad4b68`). The commit records one correction to the digest it came from: the middle rung had been put loosely as "a few in series", and it was corrected because [[Philosopher Visits the Entry]] defines itself as single-expert routing at a node. The same commit added the link to Philosopher Visits.

## Links that moved

**Harvest became Weave.** From birth the page mirrored the Harvest Ceremony, while its prose has always named the Weave as the load-balancing audit (`abc2592`). The 2026-05-28 audit retargeted the link to the [[Weave Ceremony]] and labelled it load-balancing-audit (`1c1b14c`). The link and the text had disagreed for ten weeks.

**Folded to the other side.** The 2026-10-24 Weave took out links the other page already states. The first round folded Hyperdimensional Prism, [[Kuramoto Coupling]] and [[Swarm Weave]] (`769c93a`). The second folded Excellent Adventure, Dialectic and Philosopher Visits the Entry (`050af49`), the three links the page had added for its embodied substrate. Each of those six pages now mirrors this one. The page still names all six in its prose, but its frontmatter no longer links out to them.

**Cut outright.** [[Embeddings as Relational Meaning]] had been a bare connects-to since the first commit. The prose never named it, and the skeptic pass of 2026-10-24 cut it (`17e99b3`). Nothing links the pair now.

## What has not moved

The Current State section, the list of frontier models and the claim that "the paradigm won", is word for word what the first commit wrote on 2026-03-17 (`abc2592`), apart from its heading and one tightened phrase (`c4f30b5`). It is the most dated thing on the page, and no commit has revised it since. The forward vector has been unchanged since it was added on 2026-04-21 (`76e60f3`). It still speaks of ceremonies and collaboration structures, and the embodied substrate it now also maps is not in it.
