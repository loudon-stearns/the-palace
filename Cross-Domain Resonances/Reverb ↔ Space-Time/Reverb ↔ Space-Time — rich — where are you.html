Where are you?

The page's demonstration: the same voice, dry, then in a small room, a hall and a cathedral. Each room is a modelled box, drawn to one scale; the voice stands 2.5 to 3 m from you in every room but the dry one, so mostly only the room changes. Last, the four again in a shuffled order, and the question.

RT60 = 0.161 · V ⁄ A
(seconds for the sound to fall 60 dB) = 0.161 × (the room's volume) ÷ (its absorption). The cathedral is not only bigger; its stone soaks up less of each bounce.
The voice is synthetic (Kokoro, af_heart), speaking a paraphrase of the page's own last lines. The rooms are the image-source shoebox of the clap piece, with a noise tail past about 600,000 reflections, darkened with time as a sound choice, and cut at 8 s. Real halls and cathedrals are not boxes; these are three sizes of one idea.
