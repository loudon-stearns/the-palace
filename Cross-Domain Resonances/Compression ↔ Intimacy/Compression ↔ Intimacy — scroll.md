### 2026-11-08 — The first principle, disproved by the page's own face

**What was tested:** the sentence the page's whole bridge stands on, in The Technical Side: "Our ears interpret small dynamic range as proximity — a whispered voice in your ear has minimal range; a shout across a field has enormous range."

**The work:** nothing new was made this morning. The evidence is the rich face's first round, three pieces on one synthetic voice, made, challenged and remade in the proposal round of 2026-11 (`85775bad`, `17d238d8`, `2c9bc223`) and still at proposal.

**What it showed:**
- In open air, distance is one gain on the whole voice, so it cannot change the range at all.
- Over a quiet noise floor, distance only narrows the range: 27.9 dB at 4 m, 12.2 dB at 32 m. The far voice is the narrow one.
- Loudon's objection in the sit, from the physics: up close, the inverse-square law makes a whisper the widest voice there is. A 2 cm lean at 2 cm is a 6 dB swing; at 50 m it is nothing. Breath bursts and plosives reach the eardrum. Far away, the room and the noise fill in the quiet.

So "small range reads as near" runs backwards. Near is where the range is widest.

**What replaces it (Loudon's reading, 2026-11-08):** "I believe that intimacy is mostly hearing the quiet bits, and the DANGER of hearing the quiet bits is key to intimacy, because intimacy requires trust." A compressor lifts the quiet bits, which brings the source near, and caps the loud ones, which promises the near thing will not shout. So the closeness is borrowed, not consented to: "it is a forced intimacy, and perhaps the gentle loving term 'intimacy' isn't even quite right." The hiss of a close-miked guitar cab, brought near by compression, can bring dread. What compression makes may be nearness, which reads as intimacy when the near thing is trusted and as dread when it is not.

**What is still open:**
- Whether compression is heard as closer at all. The blind test, "where is the singer", has had no listener. Its brightness leak is unclosed: raising the whispers also brightens them (+7.2 dB above 3 kHz at 4:1), so a "closer" answer could not yet tell range from detail.
- The four knobs' seam (the hold read as the guard in three lines, as the closeness in the release line) may be the page's real contradiction, held on purpose: the guard is what makes the closeness bearable.
- Whether the page should be about nearness, with intimacy and dread as its two faces.
