# Dovetail Joint ↔ Counterpoint — Context

The page arrived on 2026-03-25 as one of the Bridges in the 4 Pillars Framework artifacts, part of a palace-wide restructure (`46d904f`). It came in finished: 360 lines, stage mature, hook quality 10, and two links only, to [[Cross-Domain Resonances]] and [[FOUR PILLARS]]. Everything since has been subtraction and wiring. The body has gained no section and no claim, only a hero image, one wikilink, and the sentences that stand in for what was cut.

## What it left out

Two editing passes cut it to its present length, and neither moved what it cut anywhere. The cuts live only in git.

**The worked examples (2026-05-28, `b41c7c5`).** The audit's shortening pass cut about 85 lines. Its rule for the ten Bridges was to cut the Technical Side taxonomies, repeated "why it works" triads and teaching scripts, and keep the cross-domain metaphor, the structural identity and the philosophy. Here that took out four things. ASCII sketches of tail board, pin board and assembled joint, with the usual slopes (1:8 or 1:6). A four-note two-voice example, C–E–D–C over E–C–B–C, with its Roman numerals. A pair of force diagrams. And a section called The Physics and Music Theory: an idealized wedge formula claiming the joint multiplies resistance about seven times at 8°, and a voice-leading resolution from D–F–A to C–E–G. Each survived as one sentence in the Technical Side or the Deeper Principle.

If anyone brings the examples back, check them first. The music example called D–F–A unstable because it "includes a tritone". It doesn't: D–F–A is a minor triad. It also marked A→E as a voice that "stays". The 8° figure doesn't match the 1:8 slope given a section earlier, which is about 7°. The wedge number came with no source.

**The why and the measure (2026-10-24, `a75350e`).** A Weave writer cut Why This Resonance Works from three subsections to one sentence, keeping the page's own line, "Shape one to receive the other" (writer log, `_ops/swarm/sessions/weave-2026-10-24/writers/log-set-4.md:49`). The sentence that stayed is the first subsection, concrete to abstract. The other two went. One was the aesthetic parallel: exposed dovetails and clear counterpoint as marks of quality, beautiful because they show a problem well solved. The other was the transferable-thinking prompts for music, code and collaboration. The same pass cut Success Metrics from a Not/But list to its closing sentence. The forward vector still hopes people will see the pattern "in their code". Since that cut, the body never mentions code.

## Links: came, moved, went

Lao Tzu went the other way: the frontmatter link stays on this side, and the Lao Tzu heading in the body has never been a wikilink.

## The stage, down and up

The page was born mature. The 2026-06-26 Weave, which also gave it its first forward vector, demoted it to growing (`b67fa03`). That pass demoted thirteen pages whose bodies fit growing, not mature. Ten days later the multi-lens Weave promoted it back to mature, with no reason recorded beyond the stage list (`44339ae`). The body did not change in between. So nothing in history says what earned the promotion. The next stage call should be made on the page as it stands.
