The four tracks

Part 1 of the assignment, done here: one groove played freely, quantized at 0, 25, 75 and 100 %. Then a strength for each part, as a student discovers they need. Then every part at every strength, laid out as a matrix to play from and to copy. Last, four mixes, blind: you say which grooves and which is robotic before you learn which was which.

(where a hit ends up) = (its grid line) + (what that part's strength leaves) × (how far it was played from the line). One strength for the whole groove is the four tracks; one per part is the student's discovery.
The groove is the one in The Technical Side's piece, with a percussion part added (a short 820 Hz blip), played loosest of all; synthesised here, nothing sampled.
