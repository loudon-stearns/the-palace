Mistake or feel?

The section asks one question of every deviation from the grid: was it a mistake to correct, or expression to keep? And it says quantization assumes mistake. Four steps, one idea each, at 120 BPM, where a 16th note lasts 0.125 s. The last step asks the quantizer to answer the question for you.

one beat, close up · grid lines every 16th note (0.125 s)
two bars, played freely · each mark where the hit lands now · hollow ring = its grid line
the stumble · bar 1, beat 3kick against the hi-hat
the lean · bar 2, beat 4snare fill into the downbeat
g = 0.125 s · round( t ⁄ 0.125 s )
(the grid line a note belongs to) = the nearest multiple of one 16th note, which at 120 BPM is 0.125 s.
t′ = g + (1 − s) · (t − g)
(where the note ends up) = (its grid line) + (what the strength leaves, 1 − s) × (how far it was played from that line). At s = 0 nothing moves; at s = 1 every note sits on its line. The same (1 − s) is applied to every note: the rule has no input for why a note was off.
The groove is made here, from a sine kick, a noise snare and a noise hi-hat; nothing is sampled. Its timing is written, not recorded: small seeded wobbles on every hit, plus two plants, a kick 37 ms late in bar 1 and a snare fill leaning up to 37 ms early in bar 2. Checked offline on this page's own engine (the script marked engine): every deviation keeps the same share (1 − s) at every strength; the stumble falls under 5 ms only above s = 87 %, where the lean keeps 13 % of itself.
