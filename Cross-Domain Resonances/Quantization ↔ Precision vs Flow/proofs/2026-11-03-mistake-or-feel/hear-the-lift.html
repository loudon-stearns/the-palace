Hear the Lift

Four bars, perfectly in time, except for one gesture through bars 3 and 4. Two ways a drummer can make it.

Lean against a click: the clock never moves. The drummer leans ahead of it, a little more each beat, then lands back on the clock's bar 1. Speed up, no click: there is no clock to return to. The pulse itself quickens, then eases back to the old tempo, and the loop comes round early. No swing, no stumbles, so the gesture is the only thing moving.
