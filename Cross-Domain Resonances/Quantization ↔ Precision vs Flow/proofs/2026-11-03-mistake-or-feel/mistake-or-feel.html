Mistake or Feel?

The page says quantization can't tell a mistake from feel, and only you can. Here is one drummer, four bars, and both kinds of deviation. Your ear first, then the picture.

The drummer has a feel: swung sixteenths on the hats, a backbeat that sits behind, ghost notes that lean early. It comes back every bar. The drummer also makes mistakes: a little jitter everywhere and three clear flubs. Those don't come back.

The picture

Four bars on one grid

Each dot is one hit, placed where it landed. All four bars are laid on top of each other. Feel shows as a tight cluster sitting off the gridline. A mistake shows as a dot that wanders from its cluster.

Rows, top to bottom: hat, ghost, snare, kick. Thin lines are the sixteenth grid. Short amber ticks are the take's own groove: the median of the four bars for that slot.
