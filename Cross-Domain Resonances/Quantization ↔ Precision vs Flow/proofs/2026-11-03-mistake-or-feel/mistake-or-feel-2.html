Mistake or Feel, Phrased

Feel speaks on many levels at once. A measure is not a copy of the last one; it sets up the next.

Same drummer, now eight bars. The feel inside each bar is the same as before: swung hats, a laid-back backbeat, early ghosts. On top of it the drummer plays the phrase: settles back into bar 1, holds steady, then leans ahead through bars 7 and 8 into the turnaround, getting louder. The stumbles are still there too.

Only timing changes between versions. The dynamics, the crescendo included, are the same in all five, so the ear is judging time alone.
