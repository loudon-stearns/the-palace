Under the kick

The page says kick and bass share 60 to 120 Hz, and that hearing can’t tell apart two sounds in the same band at the same moment. This walk puts a bass note under a kick and asks whether you can still name it. Five steps, one thing in each; the last is blind. Every sound is made here from sines and noise. The test needs headphones or speakers that reach 60 Hz. Nothing plays until you press Play.
