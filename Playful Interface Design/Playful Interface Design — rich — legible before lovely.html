Legible before lovely

One compressor on a heartbeat, and one question: how hard is it squeezing? First a bare meter answers it. Then the same gain is drawn as a vessel whose opening is the gain. Then a juice slider adds reactions, first ones that mean gain, then ones that only follow the beat. Last, a blind test of whether you can still read the squeeze. Four steps; nothing plays until you press Sound on, and the test is silent.
