The leak on a jack

One neuron's membrane, charging to a threshold and starting again. Its leak time constant τ begins as a knob and ends as a jack you can patch anything into. Nothing sounds until you press play. The last step reads the same jack two ways, once a sample and once a block, and lets you hear what the block costs.
