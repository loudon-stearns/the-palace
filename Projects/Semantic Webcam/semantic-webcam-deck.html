Semantic Webcam — a deck in seven facets

Serve this folder over http (06 and 07 load a face mesh from the web; from a file they fall back and say so). A camera that renders you as typeset language. The project is its build history — seven facets that could not stop iterating each other. Each card below opens the live cam. They are the answer the entry gave to its own carried question: is legibility just another band of light?

01

Live STIGMERGY Cam terminal

Your motion as cells lit on an amber CP437 grid. Camera as blackboard: the screen is the trace, not the picture. The homage that started it.

Open the cam → facet · terminal grid
02

Word Flock Cam guesses

Words as boids, flocking to what it guesses are eyes and fingertips — motion and dark blobs. Set aside on the pivot to multiply; revived as 06, where the guessing stops.

Open the cam → facet · flocking
03

Character Multiply Cam anchor

Luminance picks the glyph; periods in shadow, dashes above, whole words through the mid-tones, heavy shade, then white. A convolution of two character layers — the multiply that made the rest of the deck possible.

Open the cam → facet · multiply blend
04

Grammar-Fit Cam composes

Mid-tone runs filled by chained words that follow each other grammatically, not just by length. The blob becomes a phrase that almost reads aloud — the picture trying to speak.

Open the cam → facet · syntax
05

Tonal Word Cam final

The carried question rendered as the answer: light is typeset. Spawned the concept entry it could not stop pointing at — Language as a Tonal Medium.

Open the cam → facet · concept-spawning
06

True Features Cam knows

Card 02 revived with a face and hand mesh. The real irises, mouth and fingertips each pull their own vocabulary — seeing words orbit your eyes, speech words your mouth, touch words your fingers.

Open the cam → facet · face + hand mesh
07

Anatomy Cam joins

The tonal picture of 05 and the true features of 06, joined. Light still picks each word; now the part of you a line sits on picks its text. Your eyes are typeset in seeing, your mouth in speech, your hands in touch — each a grammar line, not a word pile.

Open the cam → facet · body as syntax
08

Tonal Bench measures

No camera: the question asked with numbers. One still, three fits — grammar alone, tonal, and a control aimed at random light. Tonal fit makes a line's ink track its brightness (r ≈ 0.49 against 0.02); cell by cell the effect is faint (0.17). Language carries tone, but with a wide brush.

Open the bench → facet · the carried question
