One ring, two clocks

The detector’s ring and Retrospective Delay’s buffer are the same thing: slots that fill in order, the oldest leaving as the newest enters. The section says that at 11 ms the ring sits below perception, and stretched to a second it holds a phrase you hear. One ring here, read two ways at once: the sum of what it holds is a level, and what leaves it is a delay. Three steps, and the last asks your ear where the ring turns into time.
