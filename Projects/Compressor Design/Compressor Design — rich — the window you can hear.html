The window you can hear

The compressor here measures loudness with a ring of slots and a running sum, and nothing else; that is this piece’s model, and the Gen~ build may smooth more than this. The section's claim is that the ring's length stops being a number and becomes a time you can hear: 100 samples twitchy, 1000 sluggish, 500 in between. Five steps, one thing in each, and the last is a blind test. The drum loop is made here from sine and noise. Nothing plays until you press Play.
