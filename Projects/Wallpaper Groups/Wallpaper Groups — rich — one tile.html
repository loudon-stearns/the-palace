One tile, and only the operations change

The section's prism machine, run: one tile, then one operation at a time, until every wallpaper group stands beside the others. The tile never changes. Silent; nothing here makes sound.

amber = as drawn · blue = reflected
Each group is drawn from its operations as the International Tables list them, on a lattice of the shape it needs; the tile sits at the point of the cell farthest from its own copies. The orbifold names (Conway's) count what is left when the pattern is folded up by its symmetry.
