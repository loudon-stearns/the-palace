The turn and its mirror

Write a cell in steps 1 to 4, turn it twice as the Method says, hear it beside the four steps alone and played backwards, and last, count the mirrors on every loop this recipe can cook. Sound starts only when you press play; changing step stops it.

n → n + 4  ·  n → c − n
(a third of a turn on the clock) is (a slide of four steps in time); (a mirror) sends step n to step c − n, and a loop has one when it lands on itself that way. Twelve values of c, twelve mirrors to try.
100 BPM, sixteenth-note steps, numbered 1 to 12. One wooden tone at one level for every hit. A quiet tick marks step 1 of every twelve only, so no grouping of three or four is imposed; the tick button turns it off.
