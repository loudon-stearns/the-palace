The mirror, heard blind

Write half a phrase, mirror it as the Method says, set the same half beside it played twice, and last, find out blind which one sounds stiller to you. Sound starts only when you press play; changing step stops it.

(a hit at step n) gets (a partner at step 17 − n): the Method's mirror, a reflection in time about the seam between 8 and 9. On a loop the same mirror has a second end, the seam between 16 and 1. recipe-p2's rule, n → n + 8, sends the same half on unreversed: the half played twice.
100 BPM, sixteenth-note steps, numbered 1 to 16. One wooden tone at one level for every hit; both loops in any comparison have the same hits in their first half and the same number of hits. A quiet tick marks step 1 of each bar only; the tick button turns it off.
