## the mirror, heard blind (What you should hear): remake

- **Test or decoration?** A test, and the right kind. "pm sounds still" (:23) and "heavier, more settled, than p2" (:25) are claims about hearing, and a blind two-way choice is how you test hearing. The piece compares the same half under the Method's mirror and under recipe-p2's rule. It could come out against the page: 0–1 mirror picks, or 2–6.
- **Faithful?** Yes, as far as it goes. The comparison loop is the loop recipe-p2 actually cooks (n + 8), which is what "than p2" names.
- **Walks up?** Yes. The half, then the mirror, then the half again beside it, then the test.
- **Rule and arms.** The rule is set before trial 1. Chance is 9 in 256 (3.5%) each way, never summed. Both directions are scored, and so is first-pick habit. Recomputed: 0.0352.
- **Matched.** Same tone, same peak, no compressor. The same first half and the same hit count in all 120 test halves. Both loops play two bars, with 6 steps of silence between them. Nothing in the DOM gives the order away: the pictures are hidden (`display: none`) and the trial panel shows only "first … then second". Answering "first" every time scores at chance.
- **The leak, and why naming it isn't enough.** Step 3's own text says "The page says pm sounds heavier and more settled than p2", and step 3 also teaches which loop is which: the half played twice is easy to hear. So anyone who walks steps 1–3 can score 8 of 8 by recognising the repeat and answering from the words, with no stillness heard at all. The maker's fix, "hand it to someone who hasn't read the recipe", doesn't hold either. That listener walks the same steps and reads the same sentence, unless they happen to jump straight to step 4. This is tuning item 12(b), the surrounding text as a leak surface, only here the text is inside the piece.
- **Not a confound, but worth saying.** A mirror about a seam pairs 8 with 9 and 1 with 16. So the mirror loop has more adjacent hits than the repeat in 70 of the 120 halves, and fewer in none. That is part of what a mirror is, not a mismatch. But a doubled stroke at the seam may be what a listener hears as "settled", and a reader could also use it as a cue.
- **Runs clean.** No AudioContext before the click. Play gives `running`. Hiding gives `suspended`. One trial played to its end and enabled its answers. Console empty.

**Remake, exactly:**
1. Take "The page says pm sounds heavier and more settled than p2" out of step 3's text. Keep the switch and the listening.
2. On step 1, add "a fresh listener: straight to the test". It opens step 4 without walking steps 2–3.
3. Record whether steps 2–3 were seen and print it with the score. When they were, the 7–8 verdict should read "the page's direction, or the page's words: this run can't tell them apart".
4. Optional: after the eight stillness trials, add eight "which one ran backwards?" trials. If the listener names the mirror 7–8 of 8 and has read the page, the stillness count is recall, not feel.
