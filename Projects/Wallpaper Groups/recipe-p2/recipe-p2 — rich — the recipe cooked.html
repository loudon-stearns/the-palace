The recipe, cooked

The Method's steps, done exactly as written, then pm's beside them, then a 180° turn drawn two ways. Then a repair the note doesn't make, a second voice, offered; and last, a test of the note's own claims on its one voice. Sound starts only when you press play; changing step stops it.

the proposed repair: (n, voice) → (17 − n, the other voice)
(turning the drawn strip 180° with a second voice) plays the half backwards and swaps low and high: a half-turn that is not a mirror. The note names no second voice; this is an offer.
100 BPM, sixteenth-note steps, numbered 1 to 16 as the recipe numbers them. One wooden tone; with two voices, the same tone at two pitches a fifth apart (587 and 880 Hz), same peak, same envelope. A quiet tick marks each beat; the tick button turns it off.
