Track VI — Elemental Motion

subtle, dramatic motion on an individual inked frame — without losing the hand-drawn black-and-white look.

The finding: stylize once with AI, then move the ink with pure geometry. The drawing is inked a single time; a deterministic displacement field animates it into a seamless loop (frame N ≡ frame 0 by construction). The result is temporally rock-solid — zero boil — where a per-frame AI restyle flickers. "Manipulate the existing ink, slowed down," made literal.

The decision map

Is the element already drawn, and does it keep its topology (just displaces)? ├─ YES → WARP THE INK (Tier A). Free, loop-perfect, native. │ [water · sky · wispy smoke · flame · gentle dust] └─ NO → billows / scatters / not-drawn-yet? └─ SIMULATE (Tier B) → STYLIZE NPR (Tier C) → RECOMPOSITE (multiply). [billowing smoke · heavy dust · a medium the drawing lacks] Physically-grounded motion on drawn ink? → bake velocity → drive the warp (fields.from_flow). Never: per-frame AI restyle for the motion — it boils. AI makes the still; geometry makes the motion.
