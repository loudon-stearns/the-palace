Slime Mold Delay · Study · the flow dish · realtime

Feed the dish, play into it, and listen to the delay grow its own routing.

Every gold tube is a delay line. Its length sets the echo time, and its thickness sets how much sound it carries. The mold thickens a tube by how much flow it carries: loud food pushes, the red listener drains, and pressure settles across the network. Feeler tips reach toward food that is sounding but not yet connected. A tube that stops carrying anything thins out and pulls back. You don't patch anything. You decide where the food is and what plays.

Click bare agar: place an oat flake (food, up to 6). Click a flake: it starts or stops playing, and it becomes the selected flake for the mic. Shift-click a flake: take it away (its tubes pull back). Keys 1–6 pluck a flake by hand. Flakes swell while they sound. A tip can bud from partway along a tube (a ring marks the bud); if it finds something, the tube forks there (a gold dot). Moving dots are the flow, travelling the way the cytoplasm goes. Faint threads are feeler tips; brown threads are tips that gave up.
