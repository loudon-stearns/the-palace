Floquet Kernel — a wavetable surface played as a filter

The surface's fast axis is the filter, and its slow axis is scanned at the scan rate. Here it runs as a bank of modes: each mode is one band-pass followed by one frequency shift. Slow scan: a filter you hear move. Fast scan: the input is split up and moved off its own pitch.

Scan follows the pitch: the scan rate becomes the input's pitch times a ratio. For the saw that is the pitch slider; for the melody, each note; for the phrase, a pitch tracked from the voice beforehand. A whole-number ratio (or, on Knot Shadow, a multiple of 1/3) lands every moved partial back on a harmonic, so the result stays in tune as the pitch moves. A ratio of a few percent gives a shimmer whose speed rises with the pitch. Hann on the taps fades the filter's 512 taps in and out instead of cutting them off: each band gets a little wider and leaks far less into its neighbours.
