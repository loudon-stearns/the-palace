Study · Generative Preset Development · Ableton Wavetable

Nine Wavetable presets, described the way a synthesist would

Six of Ableton's own factory presets and the three pads made this week from "light through deep water". Each one starts with a single sentence verdict, then where the sound comes from, how it is filtered, how it is shaped, what moves it, and what your playing changes. The old parameter list sits under each card for comparison. A † marks a feel-word borrowed from how Ableton's factory presets set that knob; once your ear sitting lands, those marks disappear on their own.

Aqueous Pad

Ableton factory · Synth Pad · Huston Singletary
A slow-swelling pad built on Rust and Ruby (Modern), through a warm† filter, whose life is LFO 2 folding Osc 1.

SourceOsc 1 reads in the middle of the Rust table through Modern mode (warp 22%, fold 2%), panned left; Osc 2 reads in the middle of the Ruby table through Modern mode (warp 12%, fold 19%), panned right; the two are detuned against each other, so they beat slowly; a sub oscillator fills in underneath; shimmer unison stacks 2 voices, held tight.

FilterThe filters are split, each taking its own share of the oscillators: a warm† clean 12 dB low-pass at 714 Hz with singing† resonance, and a warm† clean 12 dB low-pass at 1.4 kHz with singing† resonance.

ShapeThe amp envelope is slow-swelling: swell† attack (634 ms), settles to 53% over 15.4 s, tail† release (1.8 s).

MotionWhat moves it: LFO 2 (a tempo-synced sine) folds Osc 1 hard; LFO 2 sweeps Osc 2's wave position firmly; LFO 2 warps Osc 2 firmly; LFO 1 (a tempo-synced sine) warps Osc 1 firmly. Another 28 smaller routings keep Filter 1 cutoff, Filter 2 cutoff, LFO 1 depth, Osc 1 fold and more alive underneath. Some modulators modulate other modulators, so the motion itself drifts over time.

PlayingAn unnamed controller (slot 9) moves LFO 1 depth, LFO 2 depth. Higher notes move Osc 1 fold, Osc 2 warp, Osc 2 pan and more. Playing harder moves Osc 2 pan, Osc 1 pan, sub level and more. The mod wheel moves Filter 2 cutoff, Filter 1 cutoff.

How sure I am: which hidden slot is Env 2 and which is Env 3 is the census's guess until the slot decoder is read; synced LFO divisions are not decoded yet; † marks a feel-word borrowed from how Ableton's factory presets set that knob, not yet set by ear; filter circuit names follow Wavetable's menu order and are unconfirmed in Live.
