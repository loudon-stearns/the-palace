Where the fences sit

The page says a knob's numbers hide what it sounds like, and that named regions (dark, warm, open, bright; punchy, soft, pad) are what keep a generated preset from wearing the same blank face. For Vital the project has drawn those regions twice, before anyone listened: its Vital generator places bands by hand, and the rule it uses for Wavetable computes a second set. These are the project's own renders, one knob changed at a time on Vital's init saw. The last two steps test, blind, whether either set of fences sits where your ear hears the sound change. Both answer keys stay shut until you have placed your own. Nothing plays until you press a rung.

Step 1 of 4 · one knob, numbered

Filter cutoff, fourteen settings, nothing else changed

This is the project's cutoff ladder, rendered by vital_measure.py: Vital's init saw holding C3 through its analog 12 dB low-pass, with the cutoff set to fourteen values. The knob counts in semitones on the MIDI note scale, so an equal step on the knob is an equal step in pitch, and the cutoff in hertz doubles every twelve. Press a rung. These play as rendered.

Step 2 of 4 · one switch

Match the rungs for loudness, so most of the fade is gone

Asked where the sound changes, an ear will put a fence at the fade, and the fade is not what a region names: a preset sets its level somewhere else. So each rung was measured for loudness and turned to meet the loudest. The measure is the K-weighting of ITU-R BS.1770, over the same one second of sustain, 0.6 to 1.6 s into the note, where the project measures brightness. The bottom rung comes up 29.1 dB, and by that measure the rungs now sit within 0.04 dB of each other. That is not the same as sounding equally loud. A model that adds loudness band by band (ISO 532-1, run by this face's challenger) still hears the bottom rung about 16.6 phon under the top. Matched, the bottom rungs will still sound somewhat quieter. Switch between the two.

All of this is one note with key tracking off; on another note the same knob numbers fall in a different place against its partials.

Step 4 of 4 · the same test, another knob

Amp attack: where does the start turn punchy, soft, swelling?

The same test on envelope 1's attack. The page's words are punchy, soft and pad; the Vital generator's are punchy, soft and swell, and those are the three you'll place. This knob is not in seconds: the time is its number to the fourth power, so 0.5 on the knob is 62 ms and 1.0 is a whole second. The rungs run from 0 to 1.3, short to long, and each holds the note five seconds. Every rung's sustain already measures the same loudness to within 0.01 dB, so nothing is turned.

in ladder order, short to long · each rung 5.6 s
