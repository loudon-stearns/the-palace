# Phoneme Choir — Context

The page has a short history in git: one birth, one trimmed sentence, a handful of link moves. Most of what is worth knowing is about its forward vector, and about a plan in its bundle that the build overtook.

## The vector came first

The page entered git on 2026-05-05 (`5c9149b`), two days after the build log dates the bank (`_ops/sample-libraries/phoneme-choir/build-log.md:3`, added in `a5ca537`). It arrived in an Enrichment round of the old card kind. The card, a Wikipedia stub written from 2050, recorded the vector the page had before that day: "I will remain a percussive vocal sample bank…" (`dc35e62`). Reading the stub, Loudon said that "I will remain" is not a forward vector — "it is death, nothing, staying" — and that an entry has a will to act. The vector was rewritten before the page was ever committed, so git holds only the new form. That commit names this page as the first test of the swap from *I will remain X* to *I will keep X-ing*, and it drafted the principle in [[Palace Graffiti]] for review (`5c9149b`). A second round deepened the stub with the oral-percussion lineage, konnakol, beatbox and throat games among it (`9964944`).

So the vector's promise to name those ancestors in the body comes from the stub. No later commit to the page has added them.

## Tried and dropped

**The story of the principle.** Until 2026-10-24 the Future Echo callout went on to say that the stub had surfaced the conatus principle and that the vector above was its first test. The October weave cut that sentence as the story of how a rule was found. The rule lives in the Seed Jewel and [[SCHEMA]], and the stub keeps its own file (`a75350e`). The same weave's room flagged the vector itself as stasis-verbed ("I keep teaching"), against the principle the page once announced. No change was made.

**Links folded or retyped.** A `spawned` link to [[Generative Sample Libraries]] sat beside the `emerged-from` link to the same page. It was dropped on 2026-06-05 as one of fifteen reciprocal contradictions (`8379795`). The `mirrors` sister-build link to [[Talking Keyboard]] left this page on 2026-10-24 under one link per pair. It now lives on Talking Keyboard's side (`769c93a`). The link to [[The Metaphor Stretch]] came in on 2026-05-14 (`cb13eac`).

**A merge, proposed.** The October weave put "Talking Keyboard → Phoneme Choir (tentative)" to Loudon as a merge (`70982aa`, `checkpoint-2-for-loudon.md` § E). Both pages still stand, and nothing in git records his answer.

## The plan the build overtook

The bundle's proof `Phoneme Choir — proof — claude-code-handoff-prompt.md` came into git on 2026-05-09 (`a5ca537`) and is dated 2026-05-04. It briefs a forty-file Stage 0 audition of hard stops, aspirated stops and sibilants, and it asks for a "Stage 0 Audition" section on this page. That section never appears in any committed version. The build log dates the full 352-file bank 2026-05-03, a day before the prompt's own date. History can't say which date is wrong. Read the prompt as an earlier plan, not as what was built.
