How Frustration's gates were set, 2026-10-20. Outputs as measured, verbatim.

Each script takes the plugin folder and a scratch folder:
  python3 chaos_floor.py   "plugins/Frustration" ride-breathe /tmp/work
  python3 exact_catches.py "plugins/Frustration" /tmp/work
  python3 wrap_test.py     "plugins/Frustration" /tmp/work
chaos_floor.py and exact_catches.py ran against the sketch before it wrapped its phases;
wrap_test.py needs that unwrapped sketch's cached render (it compares the two), so it
reproduces only from a checkout of commit cafe611c or earlier.

== 3. ride-string-bar drifted to 3e-6 of peak after 19.8 s. Precision or a bug? (first 21 s)

engine vs unwrapped sketch: 1.4e-06 of peak
engine vs wrapped sketch:   5.0e-08 of peak
wrapped vs unwrapped sketch: 1.4e-06 of peak

Reading: precision, in the sketch. With partial 1 pinned and B near 30 the top
partials race at about 176 kHz and their unwrapped phases reach millions of
cycles. The C++, which wraps, matched a wrapped copy of the sketch to the
float32 floor. The sketch now wraps its phases too, and string-bar is exact for
all 40 s. The knobs path, which had parted at 4.85 s, became exact for all 6 s
with the same change: that parting was precision too, not the edge.
