How many grains make a color?

One neuron fires a grain of sound each time it comes round. Walk from that one grain to a handful, grow the crowd rung by rung to 512, and then test the section's claim: does the population make the sound steady at any size? A meter measures what you hear. Nothing sounds until you press play.
