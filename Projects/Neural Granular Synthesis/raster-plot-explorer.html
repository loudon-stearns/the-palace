The Raster Plot

Neural Granular Synthesis · population coherence, made visible

Each row is one neuron. Each dot is a spike at the instant it fired. You are not looking at a waveform — you are looking at a crowd. Turn the coupling up and the scatter snaps into vertical stripes: the population has agreed. The number that measures that agreement is r, the synchrony index, printed live below. The neurons sit on a ring: change who listens to whom (the kernel) and how late they listen (the lag α), and two more shapes appear — a traveling wave, and a chimera, where half the same crowd locks while the other half drifts.

Vertical stripes — synchrony, the crowd is phase-locked (r → 1).   Random scatter — incoherence, no coupling (r → 0).   Diagonal bands — a traveling wave, the mean phase ψ marching across the population. Chimera — stripes in one band of rows, scatter in the rest: one crowd, two states. The teal trace along the bottom is global r over time; the teal strip on the right is local r down the ring — full all the way is lock, empty is drift, half-and-half is a chimera.
