The scanner's tables as an instrument. Plug in a MIDI keyboard, or play the computer keys (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z and X shift the octave). The mod wheel moves Position, so the timbre travels through the table under your hand. Drop a folder of single cycles and it packs them first, in the family-chain order.
