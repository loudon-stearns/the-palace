Portamento and Physical Pitch Modeling · the page's first open question: what does a glide sound like when its damping changes while it moves?

Every glide here goes up a fifth, C3 to G3, on the same spring. Only the damping (ζ) changes — and in five of the seven, it changes during the glide. Blue is the pitch, the dashed line is the note, orange is ζ. Listen to all seven, then try the exercise at the bottom.

Exercise — what happened to the damping?

The one to sit with: Loosened after arrival sounds exactly like Clean. Damping acts on motion; a pitch at rest has none to lose. That is why ζ(t) is a gesture of the glide, not of the note.
