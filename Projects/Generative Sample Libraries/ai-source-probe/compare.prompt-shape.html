Each render is asked for one note on one instrument. The grader tracks its pitch frame by frame and asks two things: did it land where we asked, and does it hold still. A sampler can fix a wrong note with pitch_keycenter and tune. It cannot fix a wandering one.

stable = voiced ≥ 60% ∧ spread ≤ 30¢ ∧ dominance ≥ 80%
stable = (share of frames that have a pitch) ≥ 60% ∧ (width of the middle half of the pitch readings) ≤ 30¢ ∧ (share of frames within ±50¢ of their median) ≥ 80%
on_target = stable ∧ |cents_err| ≤ 20¢  ·  retunable = stable ∧ |cents_err| > 20¢  ·  texture = ¬stable
on_target = stable ∧ |distance from the note we asked for| ≤ 20¢  ·  retunable = stable ∧ |distance from the note we asked for| > 20¢, any octave  ·  texture = ¬stable, not stable

An instrument passes on an arm when 75% of its cells are on target (ON TARGET) or at least retunable (RETUNABLE). Below that it is TEXTURE ONLY: fine for beds, not for a keyboard.
