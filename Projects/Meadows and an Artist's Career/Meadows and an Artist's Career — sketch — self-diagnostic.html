How to read the verdict

Inverted (the common case). The bulk sits at levels 10–12. You are spending your attention where Meadows says the leverage is lowest — parameters, buffers, stock-and-flow ratios. Real changes feel out of reach because the moves you make do not reach high enough to make them.

Balanced. Meaningful weight at levels 3–7 — goals, rules, feedback loops, information flows. The career is reachable from where you stand.

Top-heavy. Most of the weight at 1–4. Rare. Either you are mid-paradigm-shift, or you are avoiding execution.
