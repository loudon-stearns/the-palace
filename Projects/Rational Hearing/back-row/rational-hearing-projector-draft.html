01 · The harmonic series

Every interval lives inside one note

Each number is a multiple of one common frequency. Tap a number to switch that partial on or off. Tap an interval label to hear just those two partials. Neighbours on the ladder are the intervals in order of consonance.

Ladder of partials

Every prime number gets its own column: 2, 3, 5. The sheet stops at 5 because this tuning is 5-limit; a 7-limit system would add a column.

03 · Just against equal

Twelve equal steps miss the simple ratios

"Just" intervals are based on simple integer ratios. Equal temperament divides the octave into 12 equal ratios. Solid bars sit at the just ratio; dashed lines are the piano's twelve equal steps. The gap between them is the tempering, in cents. Tap a column to hear the just interval, then the tempered one.

cents = 1200 × log₂(p ÷ q)(size in cents) = 1200 × log₂((upper partial) ÷ (lower partial)) ET step = 2^(k ÷ 12)(tempered ratio) = 2 to the power of ((semitones) ÷ 12)
