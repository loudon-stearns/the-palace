### 2026-10-30 — cycle 1 — I measured every label on my page as the back row would see it. The ladder's edge labels, the ones you point at, are the first thing to fail.
> shipped · page untouched, your review still open · steward leans enlarge the ladder in Projector mode

**Open this:** [back-row-report.html](open:Projects/Rational Hearing/back-row/back-row-report.html). It is one page with everything inline, and it ends in a six-item checklist for your walk to the back.

**How I measured.** I loaded the page in a headless browser at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1024×768 and read the rendered size of every one of its ~225 text labels and every drawn just-vs-equal gap. Then I converted each size into the angle it makes for a student sitting 8 screen-heights back, which is the usual far limit (a small room is nearer 6). I compared those angles to thresholds I recall but did not look up: a letter 5 arcminutes tall is the 20/20 limit, about 10 reads, and 16 or more is comfortable.

**Three findings.**
1. *The anchors you point at are the smallest things on the page.* On a 1280×720 projector in Projector mode, the ladder's edge labels come out at 5.9′, right at the 20/20 limit. The bracket column (PERFECT, M6, −3…) is 5.3′ and the letter names are 4.7′. Meanwhile the big 3:2 readout is 67′, four times what it needs.
2. *Browser zoom won't save the ladder.* The ladder shrinks to fit its column, so at 125% and at 200% the edge labels stay at about 6′. Fixing it takes a page change, not a room setting.
3. *At ×1 the fifth's and fourth's 2¢ miss is invisible from any seat.* The gap is 1.4 px, hidden under the 10-px bar. ×5 makes it 5′, just visible. The thirds and sixths show at ×1 from the back. So ×5 really is the back-row mode. A 1920×1080 projector makes everything a third smaller again, because the page stops widening at about 1280.

The strips show the ladder and the ×5 strip three ways: as the front row sees them, as the back row sees them with 20/20 eyes, and with 20/40 eyes.
