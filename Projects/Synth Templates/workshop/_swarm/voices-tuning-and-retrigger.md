# Family: Voices, Tuning and Retrigger

The oscillator section's less obvious controls, each isolated.

Cards to write (15), in tier order:

## seven-cent-detune
- name: Seven-Cent Detune
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Two saws detuned ≤7 cents.
- teaches: Small detune as chorus
- concepts the card must list: ["Small detune (\u22647 cents) = chorus"]
