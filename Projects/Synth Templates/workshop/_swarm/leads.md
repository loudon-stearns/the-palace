# Family: Leads

A single voice meant to be heard as a line. Mono with glide, or poly for stacks.

Cards to write (22), in tier order:

## saw-lead
- name: Saw Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono legato
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Saw, LPF mostly open, modest filter env, vibrato by mod wheel, glide short.
- teaches: The default lead; mod-wheel vibrato
- concepts the card must list: ["Subtractive: rich wave then filter"]

## square-lead
- name: Square Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: chiptune / Jan Hammer
- essentials (catalog): Square, no or light LPF, vibrato LFO delayed by ~300 ms.
- teaches: Delayed vibrato; hollow odd-harmonic timbre
- concepts the card must list: []

## supersaw-lead
- name: Supersaw Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: JP-8000 trance
- essentials (catalog): 7 saws detuned ±20–40 cents, LPF, mild filter env, big reverb.
- teaches: Unison and detune spread
- concepts the card must list: ["Unison / unisono"]

## trance-pluck
- name: Trance Pluck
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Saw, LPF, filter env fast decay, delay + reverb; sequenced.
- teaches: Pluck + delay as rhythm
- concepts the card must list: []

## sine-lead
- name: Sine Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono legato
- classics: theremin / 'Flim'
- essentials (catalog): Sine with vibrato and glide; theremin-like.
- teaches: Expression with no timbre: pitch and amp only
- concepts the card must list: ["Sine: no response to filtering; FM/additive building block"]

## brass-lead
- name: Brass Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: Prophet / Oberheim
- essentials (catalog): Saw, LPF, filter env with attack ~50 ms (the 'blat'), slight detune.
- teaches: Filter attack as brass
- concepts the card must list: ["Envelope \u2192 filter cutoff (bite; auto-wah with long attack)"]

## acid-lead
- name: Acid Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono legato
- classics: TB-303
- essentials (catalog): The acid bass played high: saw, high res, env mod, slide.
- teaches: Same patch, different register
- concepts the card must list: []

## whistle

- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics:

## dub-siren
- name: Dub Siren
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Square or saw, LFO on pitch (fast-ish), spring reverb and delay.
- teaches: LFO → pitch at audio-adjacent rate
- concepts the card must list: []

## chip-arp-lead
- name: Chip Arp Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: C64 SID
- essentials (catalog): Pulse 25%, arpeggiator at 1/32 across a chord, no filter.
- teaches: Arpeggiators; pulse widths
- concepts the card must list: []

## soft-lead
- name: Soft Lead
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Triangle + sine, LPF, slow attack, chorus.
- teaches: Gentle timbres; triangle as a soft saw
- concepts the card must list: ["Triangle as a filtered square"]

## pwm-lead
- name: PWM Lead
- tier: 2
- mode: mono
- classics: SH-101 / Juno
- essentials (catalog): Pulse with slow LFO on width, LPF, chorus-like thickness from one osc.
- teaches: PWM
- concepts the card must list: ["Pulse width, static and modulated"]

## sync-lead
- name: Sync Lead
- tier: 2

- classics: Cars 'Let's Go', Prophet-5
- essentials (catalog): Hard sync, osc2 pitch swept by env or wheel; aggressive, formant-like.
- teaches: Hard sync as a timbral control
- concepts the card must list: []

## distorted-lead

- tier: 2
- mode: mono

## screech-lead
