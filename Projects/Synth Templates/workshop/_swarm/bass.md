# Family: Bass

The low end. Mono almost always; the filter envelope and the decay shape are the whole character.

Cards to write (20), in tier order:

## sub-bass
- name: Sub Bass
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: any 808 sub
- essentials (catalog): Sine (or triangle), no filter, instant attack, held sustain, short release. One octave down.
- teaches: Pure fundamental; why sub needs no harmonics
- concepts the card must list: ["Sine: no response to filtering; FM/additive building block"]

## 808-bass
- name: 808 Bass
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: TR-808 kick-as-bass
- essentials (catalog): Sine, pitch envelope drops ~1 octave in 20–60 ms, long amp decay (1–3 s), light saturation for upper harmonics.
- teaches: Pitch envelope as punch; decay as length
- concepts the card must list: []

## moog-bass
- name: Moog Bass
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: Minimoog
- essentials (catalog): Saw + square an octave apart, 24 dB ladder LPF, cutoff lowish, filter env short decay, slight drive.
- teaches: Two-osc stacking; ladder filter slope; drive
- concepts the card must list: ["Combining waveforms: square + saw octave up (zipper)", "Filter models and their distortion (sine test)"]

## reese-bass
- name: Reese Bass
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: Reese Project / jungle, DnB
- essentials (catalog): Two saws detuned 10–30 cents, little or no filter movement, the beat frequency is the sound. Often chorus or phaser later.
- teaches: Detune as motion; beating
- concepts the card must list: ["Small detune (\u22647 cents) = chorus"]

## wobble-bass
- name: Wobble Bass
- tier: 2
- mode: mono
- classics: dubstep
- essentials (catalog): Saw or square, LPF with LFO on cutoff synced to tempo (1/4–1/16), high resonance, distortion after.
- teaches: LFO as rhythmic modulation; tempo sync
- concepts the card must list: ["Tempo sync; triplets"]
