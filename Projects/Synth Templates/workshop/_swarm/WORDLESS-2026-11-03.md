# Wordless words — 2026-11-03

The sanity pass flagged thirteen cards whose words have no number in the table the voice reads. Two could say the same thing with a word the table has, and were changed (serial-extreme-48-db and serial-notch-vs-band-pass-tug: filter2 cutoff `same as filter1` → `mid`, the slaving kept in the why). The eleven below keep their word: it is the right word, and the voice reads the wrong table or has no word small enough. No words were added to vocabulary.yaml. Each case says what the voice needs.

## An LFO to pitch wants a pitch_amount word

Cards: **alarm** (`a fifth`), **arpeggiated-chord** (`a fifth`), **granular-cloud** (`a fifth`), **glitch-stutter** (`about an octave`), **sample-and-hold-melody** (`about an octave`), **siren** (`about an octave`).

Each routes an LFO to `osc1.pitch` with an interval word. The words are right: `pitch_amount` holds them (a fifth 700, about an octave 1200, two octaves 2400). The voice's LFO branch (voice.js, `/^lfo/` in noteOn) reads every LFO depth from the `amount` table — `word('amount', depthWord, 0.5) / 5`, then `depth * 100` cents for an oscillator target — so the widest word it has, `full`, reaches one semitone. No amount word can say a fifth; the fallback 0.5 gives these renders a ten-cent wobble where the card wants a two-tone alarm, a siren, a stair.

**Fix the voice needs:** when an LFO's target is `osc*.pitch`, read the amount from `pitch_amount` and set the gain in cents directly: `g.gain.value = word('pitch_amount', amt, 700) / 2` (the named interval as the full swing, bottom note to top; `/ 1` if Loudon hears the interval as the excursion from centre). Keep the `amount` read for filter, amp and pan targets. The depthWord lookup should also prefer the route's amount over the LFO's taste `depth` default when the target is pitch, since the taste row on these cards is `amount`, not `depth`.

## A pitch envelope wants a word smaller than a fifth

Cards: **marimba** (`little`), **seinfeld-slap-bass** (`little`).

Both route `menv → osc1.pitch` with `little`: the mallet's thump, a slapped string's blip, under a semitone. The voice reads the right table (`pitch_amount`) and the table's smallest word is `a fifth`. `little` is not there, and the fallback is 1200 — an octave of thump, which is a kick, not a marimba. Changing the card to `a fifth` would change the sound. Both cards already say so in their menv notes.

**Fix the voice needs:** a `pitch_amount` word below a fifth. This is a vocabulary matter (vocabulary.yaml, Loudon's table), not voice.js: something like `little: 50` and `a semitone: 100` (cents), sat by ear. Until then the voice could at least fall back to a small number, not an octave, when a `pitch_amount` word is missing — `word('pitch_amount', amt, 100)` instead of 1200 would wrong these two cards less.

## A comb filter tuned to the note

Cards: **karplus-strong-string**, **sitar**.

Both have `filter1: {type: comb}` with essential `cutoff: keytracked` and a `key → filter1.cutoff, amount: full` route. `keytracked` is not a Hz; it says the delay is one period of the played note, which is what makes it a string. No cutoff word (dark/mid/bright/open) means that, and both cards say "Voice: drawn only" on the block.

**Fix the voice needs:** a comb type. Today `{lowpass, highpass, bandpass, notch}[f.type] || 'lowpass'` turns the comb into a lowpass at the fallback 1200 Hz with half keytracking. A comb is a DelayNode of `1 / hz` seconds with a feedback gain (resonance word → feedback 0.7–0.98) and the `key → cutoff, amount: full` route read as full tracking: delay time from the note, not from a cutoff word. With that in place `keytracked` can be the cutoff word that means "base at the note's frequency, tracking 1.0", resolved in noteOn before `word('cutoff', …)` is asked.

## A modulator oscillator switched off

Card: **sid-lead** (`osc2.level: "off"`).

osc2 is a ring-mod option drawn as a jack (`[osc2, osc1, ring]`), approximated by the voice as FM. For a modulator the voice reads the level word from `fm_index` (`word('fm_index', B[s].level || 'half', 3)`); `off` is a level word, not an fm_index word, and the fallback is 3 — half index. So the default render has the bell's FM on when the card's whole point is a plain pulse lead with the ring mod off.

**Fix the voice needs:** resolve a modulator's level word through `level` first when it is zero: `const lv = VOCAB.level?.[B[s].level]; const idx = lv === 0 ? 0 : word('fm_index', B[s].level || 'half', 3)`. The same read should honour an essential or taste `level` default for the modulator slot, as it does for carriers (`levelWord(s)`), since the card's taste row carries the `off` too.

## Note on the two that were changed

`same as filter1` is a sheet idiom like the time table's `same as decay`, which the voice resolves. The cards now say `mid` with the slaving in the why, so no voice change is required; if the idiom is wanted back on the sheet, the voice could resolve `same as filter1` on filter2's cutoff and resonance the way envTimes resolves `same as decay`.
