# Family: Brass and Winds

The filter envelope's attack is the instrument.

Cards to write (10), in tier order:

## synth-brass
- name: Synth Brass
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: Prophet / Oberheim / Jupiter
- essentials (catalog): Saws detuned, LPF, filter env attack 50–100 ms then decay, vibrato delayed.
- teaches: The brass blat
- concepts the card must list: []

## horn-section
- name: Horn Section
- tier: 1
- mode: poly
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Synth brass with wider detune and octave doubling.
- teaches: Doubling
- concepts the card must list: []

## flute
- name: Flute
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Sine/triangle + breath noise, soft attack, vibrato.
- teaches: Breath noise
- concepts the card must list: ["Noise at the attack; noise oscillator with its own envelope"]

## clarinet
- name: Clarinet
- tier: 1

## trumpet
- name: Trumpet
- tier: 2
- mode: mono
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Saw, LPF, filter env attack, slight pitch env up at the start, vibrato.
