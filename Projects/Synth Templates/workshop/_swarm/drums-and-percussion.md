# Family: Drums and Percussion

Mostly one-shots: noise, sine, pitch envelopes. Teaches transient design.

Cards to write (19), in tier order:

## 909-kick
- name: 909 Kick
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: TR-909
- essentials (catalog): Sine with faster pitch env + click (noise burst, 5 ms), shorter decay, drive.
- teaches: Click + body layering
- concepts the card must list: []

## snare
- name: Snare
- tier: 1
- mode: mono
- classics: 
- essentials (catalog): Noise (HPF/BPF) + tuned sine ~180 Hz, both short decay, noise longer.
- teaches: Noise + tone
- concepts the card must list: ["Noise at the attack; noise oscillator with its own envelope"]
