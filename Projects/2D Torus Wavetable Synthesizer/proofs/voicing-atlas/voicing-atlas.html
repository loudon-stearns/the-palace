Which partial does the note actually land on?

On this instrument a note is not baseHz. Each surface, parked on each lock, puts its energy on some harmonics and not others, and longitude can re-voice it. Every cell below is measured from the surface itself, and the numbers match the Hopf codebox's own output to the percent. Click a cell to hear its partial mix and get the knob settings.
