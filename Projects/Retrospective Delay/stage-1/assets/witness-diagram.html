The Witness — always-recording circular buffer

The write head (indigo) sweeps the loop continuously, overwriting the oldest cell every sample. The read head (amber) trails behind by a fixed lag — it is playing back what was written lag ago, in present time. Nothing arms it. The buffer is the constant witness; the gain knob is your only choice about whether to listen.
