Where does the knob sit?

The page draws one measure of tape, a write head, a read head and one knob that "multiplies the write amplitude." Walk it up a head at a time, then put the knob in both places and see what it can summon. The phrases are synthesized plucks at 120 BPM, so one measure is two seconds. Silent until you press something.

The write head

One measure of tape, drawn as a ring. The amber line is the write head. It goes round once a measure and writes whatever comes in into the slot under it, every sample. Nothing to arm, no gate: the tape is always recording.

Play a phrase and watch it go down on the ring. Then wait. A measure later the write head comes round again and writes your silence over it. The tape only ever holds the last measure.

tape[w] ← x
(the slot under the write head) takes (what you play), every sample; w goes round the measure and starts again

The read head

A second head, the pale line, trails the first by a lag, half a measure here. It plays back what the tape holds where it is: the past, in present time. Play a phrase and it comes back half a measure late, all of it, once.

Change the lag and the ghost moves; the tape doesn't. At a whole measure the read head sits on the write head and hears the slot just before it is written over.

ghost = tape[(w − L) mod M]  ·  y = x + 0.8 · ghost
(the ghost) = (what the tape holds L samples behind the write head, wrapping round the measure of M samples); (what you hear) = (what you play) + 0.8 × (the ghost)

The knob, and where it sits

Now the one knob. The page says it multiplies the write amplitude: it sits on the way in, between you and the tape. The séance the steward built later puts it on the way out, between the tape and your ears. Turn it while you play, in each place.

Then try the gesture: the knob down, a phrase, and the knob raised halfway through it. On the way out, the ghost brings back the half you played while the knob was down. On the way in, that half was written as silence.

on the way in: tape[w] ← g · x,   y = x + ghost
(the slot) takes (the knob) × (what you play); you hear (what you play) + (the ghost) at full level
on the way out: tape[w] ← x,   y = x + g · ghost
(the slot) takes (what you play) as it is; you hear (what you play) + (the knob) × (the ghost)

Silence keeps

The page opens with a promise this tape can't keep yet: when you play silence, the last phrase repeats. A tape that always writes writes your silence over the phrase. So let it listen. While you play, it writes; while you're silent, it writes down what it already holds. Stop, and the last measure keeps going round.

Then the séance's gesture: play with the knob down, stop, and raise it a measure later. Try it with the knob in each place.

tape[w] ← tape[w] + listen · (x − tape[w])
(the slot) moves toward (what you play) only while (you are playing): listen is 1 while the input is above a whisper, 0 in silence, with a 5 ms crossfade between. In silence the slot keeps what it holds.

The test

Three gestures, each run on this engine with the knob in each place and the tape in each mode: twelve runs. Each number is how much of the phrase comes back where it should, as a share of what the ghost would be with the knob up the whole time (0.85). Press ▸ play it to hear a run.
