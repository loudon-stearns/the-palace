Where is the harmonic series?

The page says the oscillator's energy levels are the harmonic series. But a level can be heard two ways. The clock hears its phase turning: the real part of ψ at one point. The detector hears what a measurement could register: |ψ|², the chance of finding the particle there. Five steps: one rung alone, the ladder, two rungs, a swinging packet, and then the test, which solves the equation and measures both ears. Each step plays. You can jump to any of them.

5 · The test

The page, on the oscillator:

“These are quantized energy levels, equally spaced in frequency. They are identical to the harmonic series: fundamental frequency f₀, second harmonic 2f₀, third harmonic 3f₀, and so on.”

And the next section, on the box: “Schrödinger's particle in a box … sounds 1 · 4 · 9 · 16.” Measure both, with both ears, and with the rule the page's own proofs use: each level played at its gap from the lowest. Each row computes ψ(x₀, t) in time from the solver's levels and shapes, 65,536 samples at 44.1 kHz, and reads its partials off an FFT: every peak within 40 dB of the tallest.

Press measure.

The packet is the one from step 4 at its starting place: in the oscillator a coherent state, α = 2.2, the ladder proof's; in the box a bump of width 0.35 let go at x = 0.72. The probe is x₀ = 1.0 in the oscillator and 1.1 in the box. The window is Blackman-Harris, so a peak's own sidelobes sit 92 dB down and can't pass for partials. The clock is not something a microphone can hear: a phase shared by the whole state is not physical. Its pitches assume energy counted from the well's floor. Raise the floor and every clock pitch moves, while the detector's stay put: raised by half a quantum, the oscillator's clock reads 0 · 2 · 4 · 6 × 110 Hz, its lowest level silent and the rest 1 · 2 · 3 on 220 Hz. It is the reading the page uses for the box, and in a stiff bar it is real, since Re ψ there moves the way a bar moves (One equation, two spacings). The steward's ladder proof, a frame further down this page, plays the same two ears move by move.
