Quantum Synthesizer · Study · the quantum pendulum

Drag the well. Hear the squares fold into a string.

A particle on a ring, with one smooth bump on it. With no bump the energy levels go 1, 4, 9, 16: Schrödinger's squares. Deepen the bump and the bottom of the ladder relaxes toward 1, 2, 3, 4, because a small swing in any well is a harmonic oscillator. Each level gap is played as a partial, and the lowest one stays pinned at A3, so the only thing that moves is the spacing above it.

i·∂ψ/∂t = −½·∂²ψ/∂x² + V₀·cos(2πx)
i · (rate of change of the wave) = −½ · (how sharply the wave bends) + (bump height) · cos(2π · position round the ring)
fₙ = 220 Hz × (Eₙ − E₀) / (E₁ − E₀)
(nth partial) = A3 × (nth level's height above the floor) ÷ (first level's height above the floor)

What to listen for. Hold the tone and drag slowly from left to right. At zero the partials sit on the squares; it sounds hollow and bell-like, with a doubled fundamental because the ring lets the particle spin either way. Around q ≈ 6 the low partials land near 2 and 3. Past 20 they read about 1, 1.97, 2.89, 3.78: close to a string, but sagging, because a bigger swing of a real pendulum is a slower one.

What is physics and what is my choice. The levels are solved here, live, from the equation above (an 81-term plane-wave basis, the same solve as pendulum.py). The loudness of each partial comes from a pluck at 0.40 round the ring and a pickup at 0.46; moving those changes the colour, not the spacing. The decay times are a synth choice.
