# Frequency-Time Duality — the rich face, by design

The text walks from the flip to three doorways. The face puts one playable piece beside each stop, so a class can walk the axis by ear. Loudon means to teach from it in STIGMERGY as it grows.

**One axis, every piece.** Every made piece draws the same horizontal log axis, 0.5 Hz to 2 kHz, with the same rows under it (rate, period, tempo) and a readout of pitch and of the studio's name for that spot. It is the seed proof's axis. A reader moving from piece to piece is moving along one instrument, which is the page's claim made visible.

**Mark a zone, never a point.** Wherever a piece asks where something changes, it asks for the bottom and the top, Loudon's correction of 2026-10-13.

### Every piece

- Starts silent, sounds only on a click, stops when the tab is hidden or loses focus, and stops on its own after a set time unless held. Nothing in the bundle does all of this yet: the seed's ride sweeps for 24 s and then keeps playing at 2 Hz, nothing stops on a hidden tab, and the rich face hushes a piece only when another one starts. So the gathered pieces get a stop on hidden and a time cap, in copies made for the face, before the first face lays them.
- One self-contained HTML file in the Graphite skin and the house type, every equation twice (the symbolic line, the worded line under it, the same operators in both), radii no larger than 4 px. The seed uses 8 and 6 px; the copies fix that.
- The three axes take the house's categorical colours `--cat-2` to `--cat-4` (green, blue, violet); `--cat-1` is the accent amber, which the active control already wears. The seed used the semantic info, success and warn colours, which the house keeps for signals.
- Each piece declares what it needs in its own head (`piece:needs storage` for the marks), and a piece made for the face leaves the review kit out: the face carries its own review dock.
- Every sound is synthesized in the page, so the face names no audio file and nothing needs tracking for the public face. The one take worth choosing to track: a recording of Loudon's own Reaktor patch doing the ride, copied into `rich-face/`, under 25 MB.
- First round at Sketch tier, from the seed and the gathered pieces, so Loudon can teach from it soon. The made pieces come at Study tier, and the ride's drums are tuned with him by ear.

### Where Loudon's design-system rework may move this

- **Storage, settled this morning.** Since `b6e90ecc`, a piece declares what it needs in its own head and the palace keeps its storage apart. The face wants storage: a piece that remembered a listener's marks would let them compare their own zones across axes and days, on the face inside STIGMERGY; the public face forgets at the end of a visit. Comparing one listener with the next needs a way to gather marks, which a face doesn't have.
- **The room.** Graphite is the default, but this face will be projected in a classroom. Whether a projected teaching face wants a lighter skin, Drafting or Amber Lab, is his call.
