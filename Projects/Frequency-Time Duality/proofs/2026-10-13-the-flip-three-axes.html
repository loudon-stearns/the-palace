The Flip on three axes

The page claims one threshold rules every axis: cross about 20 Hz on the rate of a click and rhythm becomes pitch; cross it on the rate of a delay's wobble and wobble becomes timbre; cross it on a polyrhythm and the polyrhythm becomes an interval. Drag the playhead, listen, mark the bottom and top of your crossing zone on each, and see whether the zones agree, and whether they sit where the page says.
