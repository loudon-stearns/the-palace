What's Happening:

  • • As you bend the string, tension increases and the fundamental pitch rises
  • • Stiffness stays constant, so B decreases proportionally to 1/tension
  • • Upper harmonics become less stretched - moving closer to ideal harmonic positions
  • • The tone becomes purer and less piano-like when bent
  • • This is why bent guitar notes sound subtly different from unbent ones!
