Where the feel is computed

Pick the slow wire, choose frets or the string, then let go of the knob. A stiff feel computed far from the motor starts to buzz on its own: the delay feeds energy in faster than friction takes it out. Grip it and the hand's own damping calms it. That is the sweep below, run live.

fine = stable with no hand on it · only while held = the hand's damping is what keeps it stable · buzzes = unstable even gripped

The feel map · which brake each feel wants, and where it can live

Driven by voltage, the motor brakes itself for free. That brake is what lets stiff feels cross a slow cable, and it is also what kills the ring a struck spring wants. The firmware can cancel it per feel. Try the struck spring with the brake cancelled, then on. Try frets with the raw bumps and let go near the ridge between two notes.
