The struck spring

Pull the knob, let it go, and a virtual spring wobbles it down into pitch, like a whammy bar flicked. The page says a spring and a pendulum would sound different: a spring wobbles at one rate all the way down, and a pendulum quickens as it settles. This walk strikes one, then the other, puts a finger on it, and ends by measuring both from the same pull. It runs the steward's feel engine. Sound starts only when you press sound.
