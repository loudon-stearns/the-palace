Two parts or one

A knob with a light beside it is two parts: one to move, one to read. The handwheel's motor is one part that does both, and pushes back besides. This walk builds the two-part knob first, then the one-part knob, and ends on a test: land a named note four ways, one of them a control that lands in tune without a motor. It runs the steward's feel engine, the same code as the feel lab. Drag the knob around its centre. Sound starts only when you press sound.
