A stiff spring down a wire

A feel that answers the hand is a loop: sense the angle, compute a push, push. The page says the stiffer the feel, the closer to the motor that loop has to run, because delay makes a stiff spring unstable, and that soft feels can cross a cable. This walk puts one spring on the motor, sends it down a wire, adds a hand, keeps some damping on the device, and ends on a map the engine draws of where every feel on the page sits. It runs the steward's feel engine and the steward's own stability test. Nothing here makes a sound.
