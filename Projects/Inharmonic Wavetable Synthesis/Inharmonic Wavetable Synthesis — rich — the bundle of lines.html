The bundle of lines

Unison voices, each one a line in Wavetable B. Spread them along τB, the position in Wavetable B’s frames, and each voice reads a slightly different moment of the bend. The left panel draws the bundle in cents. The right one shows how fast the voices beat against each other at each partial, in hertz: that beating is the shimmer the page is after.

Every voice plays a saw-like wall, 32 partials, at 110 Hz, all summed to mono. The trajectories relax to the flat line as τB runs from 0 to 1, the way the page’s settling frames do. The measured string is a Grand Piano A2 from the project’s trajectory library, its 16 frames laid along τB.
Δn(τ) = (1 − τ) · Sn ⟹ Δn(τ + δ) − Δn(τ) = − δ · Sn
(when the frames relax a shape S toward the flat line) then (two voices δ apart along τB differ at partial n) by (δ times the shape’s height there), not by its steepness.
beatn ≈ n · f0 · (ln 2 ⁄ 1200) · widthn
(how fast partial n beats between two voices, in hertz) is about (n times the fundamental) times (how far apart the voices sit there, in cents). A plain detune keeps the width the same at every partial, so its beating grows as n.
