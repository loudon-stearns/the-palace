Bayesian Granular Synthesizer · from the prior to belief

A chord the cloud comes to believe

The page says held notes are evidence: the cloud's belief about pitch sharpens around them, and grains near a believed note grow long, so the clicks condense into tone. Five steps build that up, one idea each, and the last one tests it. Every step plays. You can jump to any of them.

1 · The prior alone

Nothing is held. Every grain picks its pitch from the prior, which is flat: any pitch from A1 to C7 is as likely as any other. Every grain is four cycles of its own pitch, with a one-cycle fade at each end: about 23 ms at middle C, 3 ms at C7. Nothing is believed yet, so nothing sustains. This is the raw, clicky cloud.

p(pitch) = 1 ⁄ 63 semitones, from A1 to C7 · N = 4 cycles
(how likely each pitch is) = the same everywhere in the range · (every grain's length) = four cycles of its own pitch

2 · One note of evidence

Hold a note. It adds a Gaussian bump to the belief, centred on its pitch in log-frequency. Now each grain first chooses where to come from: the prior, in proportion to doubt, or the note, in proportion to its weight. With one note at full weight and doubt at 0.25, one grain in five still comes from the prior. Lengths stay short in this step on purpose, so you hear only where the grains land.

posterior = (doubt · prior + Σ wᵢ · 𝒩(mᵢ, σᵢ)) ⁄ (doubt + Σ wᵢ)
(the belief grains draw from) = (the prior, weighted by doubt, plus a bump at every held note, weighted by how strongly it is held), divided by the total weight

3 · Belief takes time

A held note is not believed at once. Its weight rises fast (80 ms), and its bump narrows slowly, from 120 cents wide toward a 5-cent floor, with a 1.6 s time constant in log-cents. Let go and the weight falls over 1.4 s while the bump blurs back out. The trace below follows the last note you touched. Lengths are still short.

(how strongly the note is held) rises quickly to full · (how wide its bump is) shrinks toward five cents, the same fraction of the way in each equal stretch of time · (how sure the cloud is) runs from 0 at 120 cents to 1 at 5 cents

From 120 cents, σ comes within a cent of the 5-cent floor after about 4.6 s of holding. The floor is approached, never reached.

4 · Length follows belief

Now switch on the page's design move. A grain's length depends on how close it lands to a held note and how surely that note is believed. Near a believed note it grows toward 128 cycles, long and pure; far from every note it stays at 4, a click. The gate is the same evidence envelope as step 3, so the coupling fades in as belief grows instead of jumping at the press.

grains of the last 2 s · across: cents from the nearest held note · up: length in cycles, 4 to 128
N = 4 · 32^(closeness · gate) · closeness = e^(−½ (d ⁄ 35¢)²) · gate = w · conviction
(a grain's length in cycles) = 4, raised toward 128 by how near it lands (d, its distance from the note, against 35 cents) times how much that note is believed

5 · The test: does the cloud resolve in character?

The page claims the coupling makes the cloud resolve in character, not just pull toward the pitches. So render the same ten seconds three ways: two seconds of the prior alone, then C E G held for eight. The same seed draws the same grains at the same pitches every time. Only their lengths differ: coupled to belief, every grain short, every grain long.
