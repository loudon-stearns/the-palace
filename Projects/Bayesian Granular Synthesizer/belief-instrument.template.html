The believing cloud

Bayesian Granular Synthesizer · held notes are evidence · grains are draws from the belief

With nothing held, every grain draws its pitch from the prior, the indigo curve, and comes out short and clicky. Hold notes and each one becomes evidence: a bump that starts wide and narrows to ±5 cents while you hold it. Grains that land near a believed note grow long and pure. A chord is a hypothesis the cloud slowly comes to believe. Drag on the picture to paint the prior.

Top: the belief over pitch (amber, log scale) and the prior you paint (indigo). Dots are recent grains, bigger = longer. Keys: click, play the computer keyboard (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = C to C, Z / X shift octave), or plug in MIDI.
