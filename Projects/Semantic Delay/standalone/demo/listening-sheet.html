Semantic Delay — listening sheet

Each echo of this delay comes back sung by another voice. The real model now runs behind the helper, and nobody has heard it yet. Two questions, one phrase. Everything below is the same 4.5 seconds of SoulX's example singer, re-sung by its example reference voice on the M1 Max.

A · How far should each echo stray from your melody?

One knob per echo. Here the first repeat is locked to your melody, the second half-follows it, the third wanders free onto the scale: the dub feedback train, letting go a little more each time.

Locked (knob 1)
should sing your melody, in another voice. Measured: 0.81 semitones from your melody, 2.1 s of compute per second sung.
Half (knob 0.5)
should keep the shape and round off the leaps. Measured: 2.44 semitones from your melody, 2.0 s of compute per second sung.
Free (knob 0)
should keep your words and rhythm, and sing its own line on the scale. Measured: 4.32 semitones from your melody, 2.0 s of compute per second sung.
Listen for: does Free sound like singing, or like a robot stepping on scale notes? Does Locked sound like the same tune?

B · How fast can an echo be and still sound good?

Faster settings let the delay be shorter. We planned around an 8-second delay; today's settings need about 17.5 seconds for an 8-second phrase.
