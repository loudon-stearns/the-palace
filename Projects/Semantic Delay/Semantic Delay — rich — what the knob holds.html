What does the coupling knob hold?

The section says the coupling strength is the constraint, and calls it Kuramoto: speech rhythm and musical meter as two oscillators phase-locking. That stage, Stage B, isn't built yet. What Phase 1 built is a knob on the model's pitch input, and its code says "the knob is Kuramoto's". Here is one sung phrase through the real model at three settings of it, and the two things the takes can answer about that built knob: what it holds, and whether it locks.

One phrase

A sung phrase, 6.4 seconds of SoulX's own example vocal. It carries two things at once. Its melody is the pitch line on top. Its rhythm is the loudness underneath: where the syllables land, where the breaths fall, and where a consonant cuts the pitch line off. Listen for both.

One echo, held

The same phrase, re-sung by the model in another singer's voice with the knob all the way up, K = 1: the echo is asked to sing your melody exactly. The model also moves every echo 2 semitones up into its singer's range; the drawing takes that back out, so the two lines can be laid on each other.

Loosen the knob

Turn the knob down. At K = 0.5 the echo hears your melody as a suggestion; at K = 0 it sings the spirit's own line, a slow wander that snaps onto the scale your phrase already sits on. These are the three real takes from 14 October; there are no others in between.

p[n+1] = p[n] + a · ( K · (m[n] − p[n]) + (1 − K) · (s[n] − p[n]) )
Every 10 ms, the pitch the echo is asked to sing (p) takes a step toward a blend: K parts your melody (m), 1 − K parts the spirit's own line (s). Below K = 1 the result is also pulled onto the scale, by 1 − K. The step size a grows with K, so a tight knob follows without lag; where the pitch settles doesn't depend on it. That is the knob's whole rule, from coupling.py. Nothing in it touches timing.

The test: what does K hold, and does it lock?

Two questions the section's claim makes, put to the takes and to the knob's own rule.

does it lock?— the knob · — Kuramoto, two oscillators

Up the side: how far each is from locked, as a share of where it starts. The dots are the three takes, as the model sang them.

dφ/dt = Δω − K sin φ    locks when K ≥ |Δω|
Two oscillators: the gap between their phases (φ) grows at their difference in speed (Δω), and the coupling pulls it back by the sine of the gap. Below |Δω| they slide past each other at √(Δω² − K²); at |Δω| the slide stops dead and stays stopped. That knee is the lock.
p* = K · m + (1 − K) · s
Left alone, the knob's echo settles K of the way from the spirit's line to your melody. A blend, so its distance from you shrinks in proportion, with no point where it snaps.
