How I listen

err(tube) = Σₖ wₖ · ( Hₖ − [ Sₖ + Tₖ(tube) ] − g − s·log₂fₖ )² / Σₖ wₖ + 0.02 · Σ (Δ² log A)²
error = Σ over harmonics weightₖ · ( heard_dBₖ − [ my_pulse_dBₖ + tube_dBₖ ] − gain − slope·octaveₖ )² / Σ weightₖ + 0.02 · Σ (bend of log area)²

wₖ = 1/√k leans on the formant region. T is the tube exactly as it sings, lip lowpass on. S is a glottal pulse; for a real voice I first try sixteen throats (how long the pulse stays open, how sharply it shuts) and keep the one that fits best. In a self-test I know the throat, and the numbers match what singalong.py measured. On a throat that isn't mine (tested on my own tubes, sung through a different pulse with breath), I get F2 within about 1% and F1 within 5% on average, 10% at worst, but the mouth shape only loosely. On a real voice nobody has checked me yet. That is what this page is for.
