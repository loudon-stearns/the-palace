# Synth Templates

A catalog of base default presets, called templates, that teach basic synthesis and give learners one language that holds across synthesizers. "House Bass: mono, square, percussive filter envelope." "Super Saw: poly, unison detuned saws, low-pass." Each is a card, and a card is three things.

**The settings, split in two.** The *essential* settings are what make the sound that sound: the waveform, whether it is mono or poly, that the filter envelope has no sustain. The *taste* settings are what a player moves: cutoff, resonance amount, release length. The split is the lesson. A learner who can say which knobs are structural and which are theirs has understood the patch, not copied it. The words for ranges come from the perceptual regions [[Generative Audio Devices]] and [[Generative Preset Development]] already use, *snappy*, *dark*, *open*, *sub*, so the catalog speaks the vocabulary the palace's synth profiles will be labelled in.

**A signal-flow sheet.** Oscillator, filter, amplifier, the envelopes that move them, drawn under the [[Technical Diagram Standard]] on the [[Drafting Table Design System]]'s paper ground. One diagram grammar for every card, so a learner reads the second sheet faster than the first.

**A reference render.** Each template is rendered from a neutral, fully controlled subtractive voice the project owns, with a feature sidecar (level, brightness, rise, tail) and a spectrogram. The learner programs the template on a real softsynth, Vital, Serum, Ableton Wavetable, whatever is in [[Loudon's Toolkit]], then compares by feature and by ear. Not by null test: two synths never cancel, and the point is to hear whether the sound is *that* sound.

The cards climb as [[Progressive Staging]] and Loudon's ruling on the Dissolutions proofs ask: the house bass alone first, every later card adding one idea. Each card is held against [[Hilaritas Generator]]'s four primitives, and the sound leaves only through the [[Audition Gate]].

## Neighbours

[[Synthesis Topologies]] names subtractive as the ground the palace's generators stand on and says the palace has no subtractive instrument of its own; this catalog is that ground, taught. [[Generative Audio Devices]] holds the first house-bass recipe, `house_bass.pdl`, and an archetype registry (kick, warm_pad, pluck…) whose rule, identity pinned and expression seeded, is the essential-and-taste split in other words. [[Generative Preset Development]] builds a Synth Profile per fixed-architecture synth; its perceptual bands are the vocabulary here, and its Wavetable ear-sitting is the same sitting this catalog's words will need. [[Resonance and Damping]], [[Exponential Decay Curvature]], [[Wavetable Oscillator]] and [[FM Synthesis]] are the concept pages a card points at rather than restates. [[Portamento and Physical Pitch Modeling]]'s curated ear set is the render-manifest-quiz shape a comparison sheet can borrow. [[VST Template]]'s SimpleSynth is one candidate for the reference voice.
