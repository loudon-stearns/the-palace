# Era-map interview — the questions, drafted from the sketch

*A draft for the morning of 2026-11-04, about an hour. Not an entry. Open the sketch (`The Gallery — sketch — timeline.html`) beside it: the questions are the sketch's amber bars and red holds, turned round to face Loudon.*

## Before any date: the eras (about 20 minutes)

1. If your making life were an album, what are the tracks? Name each era in your own words, with rough years, and stop when it feels whole.
2. Which era has nothing in the sketch? The drive's earliest dated file is 2005, so school and anything before it is blank. Where would those things be, if they still exist: tapes, a drive, a parent's house, a Google account?
3. Where does each era end? A move, a job, an instrument, a person?

## The ten anchor works (about 20 minutes)

4. Ten things you made that you would hang first. For each: the year, who it was with, and whether any copy survives.
5. Which of them seeded something in the palace? (The 2014 paper seeded the house; the Synthesis Notes seeded Synth Templates. What else?)

## The sketch's guesses, fast (about 15 minutes; a word each is enough)

6. Teaching. What are "261" and "361"? What does ALT name: a school, a program? What is CPEM? Was the Coursera folder your course, or art for someone else's? The FXpansion videos: made for the company?
7. Berklee: which years, which roles, and is the Berklee NYC / LMPD line true as it stands?
8. Emerson: the MFA's years.
9. The Synthesis Notes: when were they written, and for which class?
10. Collaborators. Valen, Shae, Kyle, Lee, Dan Mc, Altenderfer: one line each on who they are and what you made together. Who might be asked for an interview later?
11. Eternal, Drum Dynamics, Filter Proof, Fall 2016 demos, Dufforama 2016: works, exercises or something else?
12. When did the name Loud'n Live begin?

## Close (5 minutes)

13. How do you feel looking at the line? What's missing that hurts to see missing?
