The stressed body

The page’s sympathetic knob “globally raises threshold + reduces release time, simulating a stressed body that compresses more aggressively.” Two moves and a promise about what they add up to. This takes each move alone, on the plugin’s own compressor and a drum loop made here from sine and noise, and then puts the two together, as written and as the plugin built them. Four steps; the last is the test. Nothing plays until you press Play.
