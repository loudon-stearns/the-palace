### The tension it lives in — "where it stops being a groove" (made) — **remake**

1. **Test or decoration?** It doesn't touch the section's claim (build the seam, adopt the craft). It probes a precondition of Loudon's open reading: does this listener have a breaking point in the swing, and how cleanly? The probe can come out "fog" or "none", so it can fail to find an edge.
2. **Faithful to the baton?** Keeping it is faithful. The baton says to bring the question to Loudon and not settle it (`— baton.md`, Next move 2). A proposal that plays the question, captioned as his ("not the page's claim"), and that ends by saying "his to answer; the page's text stays as it is" (`:391`) brings it without settling it. Nothing reaches the face as approved without his yes. Retiring it would hold back the strongest material the baton itself names. But one word puts a meaning in his mouth. His zone is the stretch between the norm and the breaking point. The piece's outcome "an edge with a zone" (`:121–122`) is the band of mixed answers around the breaking point, and the result text sets that word beside his "zone" (`:391`). It is the same drift the baton warns of for "seam". The probe measures one end of his zone, the breaking point. The other end, the norm, is fixed at 64% by the piece, not measured. Also: approving this section in the dock approves a piece, not his reading. Whoever records his answer should not read an approval as one.
3. **Walks up?** Yes: the norm, swing pushed alone, lay-back dragged alone, then the probe.
- **Chance, recomputed** with an independent edge finder over all 2^14 answer sets, coin flip: sharp **0.49%**, zone **10.64%**, fog **85.38%**, none **3.49%**. All four confirmed. But the coin flipper is only one way not to listen. A level-blind answerer who says "it broke" one time in ten gets sharp **5.8%**, zone 12.0%, none 78.3%. At two in ten: sharp 3.5%, zone 19.9%. So "a sharp edge is hard to get by chance; fog is what chance looks like" (`:122`) holds only for a 50/50 answerer. The zone result, the one closest to his reading, carries no chance figure at all, and an indifferent answerer reaches it 10–20% of the time.
- **What to change, in one round:** (a) Rename the outcome "an edge with a zone", to "a blurred edge" or similar, so it can't be read as his zone. Say in the rule and the result that the probe finds the breaking point, one end of the zone he named, and that the norm end is set at 64%, not measured. (b) Give each result its chance under both a coin flipper and an answerer who leans: sharp 0.5% / 5.8%, blurred 10.6% / 12–20%. (c) Optional: the caption's placement reason ("about where make-first energy goes") is the maker's. The baton puts the reading closer to the seam's definition and the third-seam question. A note, not a requirement.

It was not found already in place.
