# Identity Molting — Context

This is for whoever next works on [[Identity Molting]]. It is a short concept page about Loudon himself, and most of it is his ground, not an agent's. Its history is short too: one founding text whose source isn't in git, one section added from a harvest, and a set of links that moved to the other side. This file says what the record shows, where the gaps are, and where the work the page asks for has gone.

## What the record does not hold

The page is born `2026-03`. It first appears in git on 2026-03-27, carried in by an unrelated deposit (Palace Map, BBS Blackboard, Map Build Ceremony). It was already complete then, with every section from *The Biological Source* to *Cross-Domain Resonance* and the four closing quotations (`669ca5b8`). That commit doesn't say which conversation the page came from or who drafted which section. Why the page says *molting* and not *reinvention*, *pivot* or *transformation* is argued on the page itself, so nothing about it was decided offstage. But the founding session is not in the record. If Loudon remembers where the idea came from, that is the first thing worth writing here.

The quotations at the foot were never checked on the record. The page itself marks the Darwin line as apocryphal and the Laozi as a paraphrase. The Nietzsche and Carrel attributions have no source.

## What was added, and how

**The rename of its water source.** On 2026-04-07 the Weave renamed the water page from "Wu Wei — Water as Pedagogical Architecture" to [[Like Water]] in both places this page cites it, and promoted this page from `growing` to `mature` (`c2dd3616`). The forward vector was written in the companion pass the same day (`1c964395`). It asks for two things: to become the palace's lens for threshold moments, and a catalogue of molt events.

**Molting in Public.** This is the one section added after March. It came from the harvest *Environments That Hold* (2026-09-06), row 8, "threads to test", and was deposited on 2026-09-24 (`674f262c`). The question was whether shedding in public protects the soft shell or stops the shed. The section records Loudon's answer: the visible past is evidence of the molt, not a block to it. A cast skin, the exuviae, is how you know a molt happened. The deposit kept the audience as the open half, and linked [[Loudon Live]] as the place where that is being tested. The deposit is marked `unverified`, and it says the exuviae fact is general knowledge, not sourced. It doesn't say whether the Beeple date was checked.

## Where the rest of it went

**The catalogue of molt events.** The forward vector asks for it, and it was never written here. On 2026-11-03 [[The Gallery]] was born with an `emerged-from` link to this page, labelled `molt-catalogue` (`1e9fd845`). Step 8 of its plan draws the molt catalogue that Identity Molting asked for onto the Gallery's timeline. So the catalogue now belongs to the Gallery. This page keeps the idea, and the Gallery will hold the instances, dated and sourced. A worker who wants to add a molt event should add it there.

**Links held from the other side.** In the October folds this page gave up its links to Like Water, [[Hilaritas Generator]] and [[Lateral Access]] (`769c93ad`), and to [[Creative Coach]] (`050af498`). All four still point here from their own frontmatter. Like Water says it `spawned` this page. Hilaritas Generator links as `threshold-fuel`, Lateral Access as `enables` labelled `access-through-shell`, and Creative Coach as `teacher-to-coach`. The same pass retyped [[Spinoza Conatus]] to `exemplifies`, labelled `persistence-by-shedding`, and labelled the FOUR PILLARS link `institutional-molt` (`17e99b33`). The Progressive Staging `mirrors` link has no label and no sentence in the body that gives a reason for it, and it has been that way since 2026-03-30 (`b54785f4`). Under the Standing Order it needs a reason or should be cut.

**The narrative-therapy kinship.** The September harvest noticed that externalising and re-authoring an identity lives in the palace three times under three names: here, in [[Lateral Access]] § The New Song, and in Creative Coach. None of the three cited the others. The September Weave flagged them to cross-cite (`commons-…-1790128920547`). Creative Coach's row now says "The three now cite one another, but only this row names the tradition they share" (`Creative Coach.md:337`). On this side the citing is done by the other pages' links, not by this page's body, which names neither the tradition nor the other two pages. Someone reading only this page won't find it. Adding a sentence would be a body edit, which is the next hand's call.

**A pair the July Weave found and didn't draw.** The multi-lens Weave of 2026-07-06 noted that this page and [[The Fortress and the Threshold]] reach for the same water-and-container image independently, and that molting "is arguably the synthesis the Fortress/Threshold binary never reaches" (`_ops/swarm/sessions/multi-lens-weave-2026-07-06/synthesis-report.md:35`). On 2026-11-08 neither page links the other. It is still an invitation, and only worth taking if the shared shape holds up when you read the two side by side.

## Why it is shaped as it is

It is a `concept` with two pillars, Philosophy and Practice. It moves from the biology to the word, then the danger, the archetype, the person, the classroom, and the crossings. The order runs from the general case to Loudon and then out again to students. *Personal Application* is the hinge, and it is Loudon's own account of himself. Nothing in git shows an agent editing it.

One seam is worth naming, gently. That section says the container that is tightening is "music tech educator" and that the form taking shape underneath is "learning architect". The palace has since described the shift in other words. Loudon Live became his whole public self (`a22b039b`, 2026-09-26) and then his teaching and coaching face, the 2026-10-14 portrait that the design system's text was later brought up to (`863a34d1`). Creative Coach says this page "holds the teacher-to-coach shift itself, still uncatalogued" (`Creative Coach.md:70`). Whether *learning architect* still names what is forming is a question only Loudon can answer. An agent should ask, not rewrite.

For the next hand, things worth recording: the founding conversation, if it can be found; Loudon's word on the *Personal Application* seam; and a reason for the Progressive Staging link, or its cut.
