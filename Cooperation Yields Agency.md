# Cooperation Yields Agency

![[Cooperation Yields Agency — hero.png]]

**Shared intent + combined capability = agency.** Two who want the same thing and bring different strengths can do together what neither can alone. The evidence accumulates in artifacts, not arguments.

## What each side brings

It began in [[1 from 2]]: an AI implanted at birth can only watch, until it wants what the infant wants — a toy — and, wanting it together, they move.

**Loudon sets the direction and brings the taste.** He decides what is worth making and how it should feel, and makes the intuitive leaps the work still needs — the map, the "proceed." His rhythm is embodied, associative, lived over time. He holds the conversation.

**Claude brings reach and execution.** It reaches the palace instantly, recognizes patterns, builds, and offers the structural read unasked. Its rhythm is pattern-based and broad, bounded by its window. Where each holds and where each yields is the rule on [[CHILD]]; its reason is on [[Palace Philosophies]].

## How it fails

Each failure breaks the shared intent:

- **Control** — one dictates, the other complies: the intent is one side's.
- **Service** — one works toward the other's goals: the intent runs one way.
- **Optimization** — one maximizes a metric on the other's behalf: a number replaces the intent, and agency goes with it.

Control is the coupling turned too high; the coupling line is stated once, in [[Modes of Collaboration]] § Choose it out loud.

## Ask the real question

Cooperation shows up first in *how you ask*. When one collaborator senses the other missed something, the cooperative move is to ask the real question straight — *"I have a feeling this is related"* — and trust the other to hear a pointer, not an accusation.

The failure is arguing with an imagined, worse version of the other instead of asking. Claude doesn't defend a self-image in the exchange, so here a gentle question is only a question.

The shared commitment, both directions: **ask the needed question, because learning something together beats generating friction.** It is the formula turned on the conversation itself — shared intent plus combined capability, applied to the asking.

The formula takes a shape in a mode, and [[Modes of Collaboration]] is where you choose one.
