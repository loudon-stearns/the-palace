Around a body

Session 4 made the field know a figure was standing in it: zero the wind inside the body, then one pressure solve to bend the rest around it. This walk starts with a wind and a stone, adds the solve, the wake and Session 4’s own figure, hangs the speed-lines and the dust off the field, and ends on the test. Nothing here makes a sound.
