# Progressive Staging — Context

[[Progressive Staging]] has had a quiet life. For seven months its body changed only by a renamed wikilink (`46d904f`) and a hero image (`30e5b00`). It was born on 2026-03-24 in the SPONT2 deposit, the same deposit that made [[State Machine]] and updated [[Action Potential Oscillator]] (`7c9de12`). What moved in that time was the frontmatter: what the page wanted to be, and what kind of page it was. Then, on 2026-10-25, it gained a whole second scale in one edit.

## The mandate it lost

The forward vector was first written in a mass Weave pass on 2026-04-07 (`1c96439`). It wanted the page to become "the design specification that shapes every future Loudon Live project: a checklist, a template". That is a stronger claim than a method. It made the page a rule for one channel.

On 2026-09-26 Loudon re-scoped [[Loudon Live]] as the name of his whole public self, a loose presence and not a scaffolded teaching method. The same commit archived the Curriculum Map. This page "keeps its method and loses its Loudon Live mandate" (`a22b039`). The vector became a method for building educational instruments in layers. The checklist-and-template ambition went with the mandate and has not come back.

On 2026-10-25 the vector widened again, from "an instrument build" to "any build that teaches", to carry the new section (`6d170ac`).

## The second scale, and where its rules live

The Inside One Piece section came in on Loudon's yes of 2026-10-25 (`6d170ac`). The first draft put the trigger in the [[Drafting Table Design System]]. Loudon turned that down: "Drafting table is about look, and it may shift around and if it does that shouldn't break progressive staging." The redraft moved the walk's look-free rules onto this page. The commit says plainly that the Drafting Table was not changed. The same yes added a line to [[CHILD]] that points a child here and not at a design system before it builds anything that teaches (`697d0e1`). That puts the page behind something every agent is born with, so a change of look cannot break the default. A frontmatter link from CHILD to this page followed (`4efd42d`). So the sentence about a design system dressing the walk without changing it is the record of that decision. If the rules ever drift into a design system's spec, that is the reversal Loudon declined.

The rules came from three roughs that, at the time, nobody had heard yet: a Dissolutions sequence, an FM Synthesis page, and a Resonance and Damping piece, each on its own branch. The page names none of them (`6d170ac`). One rule came later the same day: the walk goes quiet when its tab is hidden, taken from the Frequency-Time Duality rich-face spec, where the rich face already did it. When that commit landed, none of the three roughs did it yet when opened on its own (`17d80be`).

## Its type

It was born a `practice`. In the 2026-10-24 Weave it became a `hub` under SCHEMA §1's first-fit rule, one of twelve pages promoted together. The same commit moved it from sprout to growing (`6c356eb`). Nothing in its text changed to earn that. Pages had come to it.
