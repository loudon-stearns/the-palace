Flat and in depth

One of BLUELINE's synthesised ink marks (the flow-field proof's synth_001), rendered in Blender 5.1.2 every way this walk needs. The drawn mark every step holds to is a metaball fitted to synth_001, seen from the front, and fed back as the card's picture, so both makes start from the same image (against synth_001 itself, scaled and centred, it agrees about 0.94, with 6 pieces of ink to its 7). Five steps: the mark on paper, the mark given a depth, a card that faces the camera, the same mark built as a metaball, and last the test of the page's choice between them.

1 · The mark

An ink blob: a body, a few satellite drops, a thrown tail of specks. It is flat, and it belongs to the paper. Move the camera and it moves with the frame, like a smudge on the lens.

the mark · as drawn

Every step after this one keeps this exact picture as its reference: the drawn mark, seen straight on. The question the page answers is how to give this mark a place in the world without turning it into something else.

2 · Give it a depth

Put the mark on a card in the scene, twice: one card 5 units from the camera, one 15 units away and three times bigger, so at rest they look the same. A far line stands at 30. Now slide the camera sideways.

Δu = f · Δx ⁄ z
How far a mark slides across the frame is the camera's sideways move, times the lens in pixels, divided by the mark's depth: near marks sweep, far ones barely drift.

3 · Face the camera

Now swing the camera round the mark instead. A card that stays put turns away and thins to an edge. A card with a COPY_ROTATION constraint to the camera, as blob_swarm.py makes every billboard, turns with it.

How much of what you see is still the drawn mark: the ink the two share, over all the ink either has. One means the same picture.

4 · The same mark as a metaball

The other way to give ink a place is to build it in 3D. Here the mark is a metaball: 147 elements fitted to the drawing, each centred on the mark's own plane, as thick as it is wide. From the front it is the mark. Swing round it.
