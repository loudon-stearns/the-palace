1 · The lines

The street as Freestyle draws it: building edges in black ink on paper. Nothing else in the scene. Every later step is held against this picture.

the street · no billboards

The page's warning starts here: always check a mark sitting over a line, never on white. On blank paper the square is invisible, because what goes missing is line.

2 · A billboard over the line

Add the two billboards in the same view layer as the buildings. They are kept out of Freestyle's line set by collection, so they get no outline. But Freestyle still asks them which edges they hide.

3 · DITHERED alone

The first fix the page names: make the billboard's material a DITHERED alpha clip, which throws the empty fragments away. Still one view layer. Switch between BLENDED and DITHERED.

4 · Two view layers alone

The other fix: render the buildings in one view layer, where Freestyle sees nothing else, and the billboards in another, then lay the billboards over the street only where they are nearer than it. Keep the material BLENDED, so this is the layers alone.

show(p) = [ z_street(p) − z_mark(p) > 0 ]
At each pixel the mark shows only where the street behind it is farther away than the mark: the factor blob_swarm.py feeds to its Alpha Over.

5 · The test: is either fix enough alone?

The page: "Neither fix alone is enough." All four corners of the two switches, and three more with the billboard material's own edge guards undone. Choose a render; the close view is billboard A, with the moved edge pixels in amber.

billboard A, close · amber = edge pixel moved
moved(p) = [ |r(p) − s(p)| > 4 ], p in the quad's edge ring
An edge pixel counts as moved when it differs from the plain street by more than 4 of 255, within 3 pixels of the billboard's square edge, away from its ink and the lines.
