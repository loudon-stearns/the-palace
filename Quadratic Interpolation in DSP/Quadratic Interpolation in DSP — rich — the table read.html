The table read

A wavetable is one cycle of a wave stored as a short list of numbers. To play it at any pitch, a reading head steps through the list by a fraction of a sample each time, and almost never lands on a stored number. The page calls quadratic "the sweet spot" between linear and cubic. Four ways to read between the numbers, one at a time, and then the test.
