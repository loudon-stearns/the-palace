# Wavetable Synthesis — Deep Research & Higher-Dimensional Design — Context

[[Wavetable Synthesis — Deep Research & Higher-Dimensional Design]] came into git whole. It was not written in the repository. A pre-Weave commit on 2026-04-27 captured it, 341 lines long, with an older `spawned: "2026-04-21"` date in its frontmatter (`e58548d`). Since then its prose has been reworked once, by being cut in half; later body edits were link repairs (`1861dd6`). Its role has changed once, by being handed to another page.

## What it left out

On 2026-06-16 a Weave shrank it from about 4,940 words to 2,400, a 51% cut. The pass kept the frontmatter, the equations, the forward vector and the open questions (`cc01c30`). The commit names no destination for the rest, and none of it was moved to another page. Git is the only place it lives:

- **Most of the history.** Palm's first machine, the Wavecomputer 360, and its 1,920 timbres; the PPG Wave 2's price and sound; the Korg Wavestation's wave sequencing, which the old text called the arrival of a temporal axis; the Waldorf line from the Microwave to the Q; Massive, Vital, Phase Plant and Ableton Wavetable.
- **Three of the seven isomorphisms.** Precomputed lookup tables in aerodynamics, climate and combustion; the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram, with stellar evolution as a scan through the table; Turing's reaction-diffusion patterns as a pattern wavetable. The Shepard tone stayed. From the higher-dimensional part, 3D Perlin noise as a three-dimensional table and a Shepard–Risset paragraph went too.
- **Small design claims.** That wavetable synthesis is a subset of additive synthesis. That a good design needs two oscillators with sub-cent detuning, for the chorus that wavetables do well. That a timbre trajectory is like keyframe animation in 3D graphics.
- **A line spoken to the asker.** The inharmonicity section opened "Your intuition is exactly right". That was left over from the research conversation the page came from.

The cut also moved some prose. The `CLAUDE → LOUDON` comment at the foot of the page, there since birth, ends on the claim that the wavetable plays deviations from the harmonic ideal, and that this is its link to productive contradiction. The shrink wrote that claim into the body's closing paragraph of Part IV (`cc01c30`). So the page now makes it twice, once in the text and once in a comment.

## The job it wanted, and gave away

Its first forward vector, written by a Weave on 2026-04-27, wanted it to be "the master research index that holds every speculative wavetable direction" and the test every wavetable project would face before it could call itself complete (`03faf97`). On 2026-06-05 it was promoted to mature, at 357 lines with 11 typed links and "actively spawning child entries" (`b445ac0`).

The index job went to [[Wavetable Oscillator]], minted on 2026-10-12 as the wavetable entries' front door. That deposit turned down the idea of folding the ground into this page (`8d3315d`). The same day this page's vector was rewritten to call it the April research record, its unbuilt branches to be claimed one at a time. Loudon approved the role on 2026-10-06 and the words on 2026-10-12 (`1861dd6`). It now deepens Wavetable Oscillator as its long form (`e4c76f5`). The stage stayed mature, and that commit left whether mature still fits as a question for the Weave (`1861dd6`).

One claim here is held against another page. The Wavetable Oscillator deposit records that this page says FM at an irrational ratio leaves the harmonic series, while [[Three Kinds of Warp]] calls FM type-1 in disguise. That tension is kept in Wavetable Oscillator's body, not here (`8d3315d`).

## Its name

From birth until 2026-09-26 the file was `Wavetable Synthesis -- Research & Higher-Dimensional Design.md`. That is a double hyphen with no "Deep", while the title always had both. The mismatch was an accident. It came to light because the face tools filed faces by title and STIGMERGY looked them up by filename. The file took its title as its name, and seven links and two steward manifests followed (`9abd813`).
