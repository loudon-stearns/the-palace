# Stoicism — Context

Stoicism was born on 2026-03-21 out of one conversation: the Fire at Nicopolis, an [[Excellent Adventure]] that sat Epictetus and Confucius beside a fire as old men (`d887d75`). The same deposit made [[Confucianism]] and [[The Fortress and the Threshold]], and the harvest log of that day records its `contradicts` links as the first in the palace. The page came in as a sprout concept of about 700 words. It has been rewritten once, on 2026-06-05. Everything since has been links and frontmatter.

## The one rewrite

On 2026-06-05 the page became a hub (`f4f595f`). Concept became hub, growing became mature, working became foundational, and in the same commit three pages crystallized out of it: [[The Dichotomy of Control]], [[The Four Virtues]], [[The View From Above]]. The commit calls them the operating discipline, the character spec and the cosmic altitude. The page took on the three paragraphs that point to them, the contradiction with [[The Drift]], and the two sections that read Stoicism as engineering, Lens on the Technical Work and In STIGMERGY. None of the control-theory or agent-ethic reading existed before that day. The commit marks its own verification "couldnt".

## What it left out

The rewrite cut the Epictetus biography roughly in half (`f4f595f`). Gone: his owner's name and post under Nero, Hadrian coming to hear him, the clay lamp he kept after his iron one was stolen, the abandoned child he adopted in old age. The fuller life now sits on [[Epictetus]], which moved into People/ from the dissolved 4 Pillars Framework folder on 2026-06-16 (`871cde1`). That page tells one story differently: it calls the broken-leg exchange ("There. I told you.") later legend, not the earliest record (People/Epictetus.md:63). This page still tells it as fact. Nobody has reconciled the two; whoever next touches the Epictetus paragraph should.

The rewrite also dropped the closing section, In the Palace. It named the Stoic tools as survival technologies in Loudon's practice and asked the Confucian question outright: are you using the fortress to survive the loss, or to avoid the threshold that comes after it? No other page asks it in those words, turned on Loudon's own practice; [[The Fortress and the Threshold]] holds only its general form (The Fortress and the Threshold.md:71). Its edge survives only softened, in the Epictetus paragraph and the first open question.

Two of the birth's open questions folded into one (sympatheia as the Stoic answer to *ren*, and whether sympatheia smuggles Confucian structure back in). The question of how Marcus's practice differs from Epictetus's gave way to asking whether the palace itself is a *Meditations* at organism scale.

## Tried and dropped

**The forward vector, three times.** The first, added by the 2026-04-07 Weave (`1c96439`), asked to develop the Epictetus thread further: the fortress found in slavery, possibly a wound become worldview. Two months later the rewrite shortened that thread instead, and its new vector asked to be "a hub the way [[Spinoza Conatus]] is a hub", "held equal to the conatus, because Loudon lives by me" (`f4f595f`). On 2026-06-24 a hand edit softened it (`0794ccb`): "Loudon lives by me" became "Loudon attempts to live by my principles", and the Spinoza comparison left the vector. The opening paragraph that holds the page equal to the conatus "because it is load-bearing in Loudon's actual life" was not touched, so the vector and the body now speak with two degrees of confidence.

**Links to the three Stoics.** The rewrite linked out to Marcus Aurelius and Epictetus; the 2026-06-26 Weave added Seneca (`198833f`). The 2026-10-24 Weave folded all three, and the link to [[Palace Philosophies]], because each of those pages already links here: one link per pair (`769c93a`). The people pages now hold the relation as `exemplifies`. Seneca's name in the opening has never been a wikilink, though [[Seneca]] exists.

**Spawned The Fortress and the Threshold.** From birth the page claimed it had spawned that page. The second round of the same Weave removed the claim (`050af49`). The relation now lives on the other side: The Fortress and the Threshold `contradicts` Stoicism, labelled the-fortress-pole (The Fortress and the Threshold.md:35-37). Parent and child became two poles.

**Palace Map.** Added as a bare connects-to in the 2026-06-26 Weave (`198833f`), retyped to `mirrors`, view-from-above, on 2026-10-24 (`17e99b3`).

## Why it is shaped this way

The family shape, three disciplines split off and each with a life face and an agent face, was copied from [[Spinoza Conatus]], by the rewrite's own vector (`f4f595f`). The page keeps short pointer paragraphs and lets the three pages hold the depth. The other four core concepts, prohairesis, logos, eudaimonia and sympatheia, are the birth's set lightly reworded (`d887d75`); none has a page of its own.

The rewrite also wrote the agency profile and the vector's last clause, both asking that each concept earn a worked example from a real decision Loudon met. No commit has done that yet. Since 2026-06-05 the prose has changed only by the hero image (`068a19d`). The page is still the deposit's argument plus the hub's scaffolding, waiting for its first lived case.
