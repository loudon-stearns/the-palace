# Kuramoto Coupling — Context

[[Kuramoto Coupling]] is read two ways, and its shape follows from that. Most pages that point at it want one sentence: too little coupling and nothing cooperates, too much and one voice dominates. Four of the World files every agent is born with lean on that sentence ([[Palace Philosophies]], [[Modes of Collaboration]], [[Hilaritas Generator]], [[FOUR PILLARS]]). The rest of the page is an instrument-maker's notebook: what coupling does to a spectrum, and how to make it something a performer plays.

## What it leaves out

**The equation.** The text never writes the model down. K, K_c and R appear in words and in the regimes diagram. The equation and the order parameter, each in symbols and in words, are the rich face's opening card (`Kuramoto Coupling — rich — equation.md`); the full build-up is the [[Kuramoto Coupling — 8-Lesson Quiz Series]]. What travels to other domains is the Origin's claim that K is a relationship descriptor, and that claim needs no symbols.

**The derivation.** Why Kuramoto is the right model for any population of weakly coupled limit cycles belongs to [[Phase Reduction]]. That page is standalone on purpose: the bridge to [[Floquet Theory]] belongs to neither hub (Phase Reduction:90).

**The self-model reading.** The opening keeps one sentence, K as a threshold of self-model revision, and points to [[Threshold Conatus]], which holds the finding (Threshold Conatus:83). The sentence came in on 2026-04-01, the day that page was born.

**The instruments.** In Our Instruments is two paragraphs. The builds are [[Neural Granular Synthesis]], the project the page names as its origin, and [[Action Potential Oscillator]].

**The making record.** Shop Round 1 ran on this page in May 2026. Its story is in the two archived handoffs in `Kuramoto Coupling/Archive/` and on the Shop specialists' pages.

**The literature.** The page names no paper, and not Kuramoto himself. Nothing in git says whether that was chosen. The page's own agency profile worries that it "crystallized in 2025". The open questions on chimeras, inertia, Hebbian coupling and hysteresis reach toward the field without citing it.

## Why it is shaped so

The first two paragraphs and the diagram are the hub: the part most links come for, short, and in the page since its first commit. Then comes Origin, then the page's own technical claim, the Fine-Tuning Insight: coupling resolves "approximately" into "exactly" and never invents a relation. Its three paths to harmonic locking came from the math mentorship deposited 2026-03-21. Cross-Domain Mirrors fans out from there. Its [[Spinoza Conatus]] paragraph has a twin on that page, and since the 2026-10-24 fold the typed link between them lives on Spinoza's side only.

The weight sits in Asymmetric Coupling: about 700 of the body's 2,000 words, a third of the page. It grew from a harvested idea (bright idea #38) and from Loudon's question about refusing to move the lowest note. That is where the page stops describing a model and becomes an instrument design, a parameter you perform. So the shape is inverted on purpose or by luck: the general claim is short and travels, and the specific design is long and stays home. The most-used part of this page is not the longest.

The register predates the palace voice: bold labels, capitalized emphasis ("IS"), arrows. A rewrite has a cost here that most pages don't carry. The rich face fingerprints each section's prose, so every changed sentence drifts a stamp and calls for a re-tune.

The Open Questions hold two near-twins on the inharmonicity threshold. They came from two deposits three days apart (2026-03-21 and 2026-03-24) and were never merged.
