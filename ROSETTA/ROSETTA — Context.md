# ROSETTA — Context

The page was born on 2026-03-18 in the same Schema Ceremony that created [[SCHEMA]] (`d1f62f5`). It has had 49 commits in all, birth included, and only three are about it alone (`22e8cf9`, `7342f8c`, `81f0f7b`). Every other change was carried here by someone else's change. When a ceremony's spec moved, its row here moved in the same commit. The shape follows from that.

## Why it is shaped this way

**It is a mirror by rule.** From the first ceremony table, the Schema Ceremony's last step was "update Rosetta Stone" (`47ce436`). On 2026-06-09 v1.11 made it one mirror among several: the Guide, SUBSTRATE and Substrate Skill. The doc-drift linter became the check (`fe610f3`). A Context can't keep it in step. Those mirror edits do.

**It grows by lettered sections, not renumbering.** Sections 1 to 8 have the headings they were born with. New material came in as subsections. §4b, the agent-to-agent ontology, came with the STIGMERGY schema on 2026-06-07 (`09e84b6`), and its baton-lifecycle rows a month later (`4e8a65d`). §4c, harness-speak, came on 2026-07-04. Its commit says every tradition on the page was conceptual, and the one substrate the palace actually runs on was missing (`22e8cf9`). The steward row and the harness-anatomy paragraph followed the same day, out of the [[Skills Are Enchantable Pages]] deposit (`ce94be9`). The `label` paragraph under §4 came in at the 2026-03-30 weave (`b54785f`).

**The name.** It was born as `Rosetta Stone.md` and renamed `ROSETTA.md` on 2026-03-25, beside JEWEL (`46d904f`). The title stayed "Rosetta Stone". The file tree went on naming the old file until 2026-05-02 (`c4f30b5`). The alias came on 2026-09-26, when faces moved to the folder named by the page's file (`9abd813`). That is why the folder is `ROSETTA/` while everything in it is named "Rosetta Stone — …". The frontmatter `version: 1` has not moved since birth. It was only reformatted from `1.0` (`46d904f`).

## The reversal

On 2026-06-05 v1.9 turned around `deepens` and `emerged-from` (`0026495`). The page was born saying "B is a more developed articulation of A", and the ontology mapped `deepens` to `skos:narrower`. That ran against the Guide's examples and against how the 276 edges of those two types were actually drawn. It had produced nine reciprocal pairs in the graph. The ceremony ratified the dominant reading, ground as target, and moved the mapping to `skos:broader`. Any copy of this page from before June has those arrows backwards.
