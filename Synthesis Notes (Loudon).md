# Synthesis Notes (Loudon)

Loudon's teaching notes on synthesis, written for students and undated. "Think of the oscillator like marble and the filter a sculptor's chisel": that is the register throughout, plain words with a diagram beside them, the manual a working synthesist would have wanted. The PDF and its extracted text live in this entry's bundle: `Synthesis Notes (Loudon) — source — notes.pdf` and `— notes-text.txt`.

Seven sections. **Oscillators**: saw, sine, square, triangle, noise, sync, pulse width, detune and sub. **Voices**: mono and poly, tuning and retrigger, portamento. **Filters**: the types, slope and resonance, and the routings (serial, parallel, split) that most synths hide behind one knob. **Amplifier and envelopes**: ADSR and its shapes, the filter envelope against the amp envelope. **LFO**: rate, shape, retrigger, fade-in, and what happens past audio rate. **Delay effects**: chorus, flanger, phaser, echo and reverb as one family of delays. **Modulation strategies**: the tables of source against destination that turn a patch into an instrument.

[[Synth Templates]] grew from these pages. Its catalog maps every concept here, 93 of them, to at least one template that teaches it, so the notes are not only read but programmed. [[Synthesis Topologies]] names the same ground more formally; [[Resonance and Damping]] holds the mathematics the filter chapter leaves out.
