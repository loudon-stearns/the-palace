# Outside the computer

Everything in [[Loudon's Toolkit]] you can pick up. Software is in [[Loudon's Toolkit — catalog — inside the computer|Inside the computer]]. A tool that reaches across the line is listed here once, and its line says what it drives inside.

## Synthesizers

### Central — performance and teaching

**ASM Hydrasynth Explorer**
Wavemorphing digital polysynth. Primary teaching instrument for certain clients. Very digital in character — pairs intentionally with the Prophet as an analog/digital contrast pair. Getting fluent; still much to learn. Will be part of the live performance setup.

**Sequential Prophet-6**
Analog polysynth. Well-known, deep relationship, long-term companion. Covers the full range: bass, lead, pad. Not ideal for drum sounds. Has not yet built a comprehensive custom preset library — recognized as an important ongoing project. "We will be friends forever and keep getting to know each other."

**Nord Drum 3P**
Electronic drum pad synthesizer. Most recently acquired. Stage purchase: chosen specifically for visual dynamism in live performance. Just starting to learn it. Preset-building work needed. Central to the planned performance configuration.

### Deep personal — less deployed

**DSI/Sequential Evolver Desktop**
Knows this synth inside and out. Edits very fast. Has audio input and output, making it useful as an effects unit as well as a synth. Keeps discovering new things even after deep familiarity. Love relationship. Would upgrade to the keyboard version given space.

### Shelf — occasional or mostly replaceable

**Waldorf Blofeld (desktop module)**
Digital polysynth, clean and polished. Great factory presets. Loves the sound but rarely comes off the shelf — much of its capability is replicable in software. Would eventually upgrade to a more advanced Waldorf keyboard form factor when the time is right.

**Blip Blox (old version)**
Audio/visual synthesizer toy. Peripheral — occasional use. Not part of teaching or primary production workflow.

## Drum machines

**Erica Synths drum machine**
Fully synthesizer-based drum machine. Knows it well. Central to the planned live performance setup.

The Nord Drum 3P, above, covers the drum pad role.

## Controllers

**Ableton Push**
Performance controller and standalone instrument. Acts as the center of the hardware-only configuration. *Inside, it drives Ableton Live.*

**Ableton Move**
Compact standalone instrument/controller. Can also serve as center of the hardware-only configuration. *Inside, it drives Ableton Live.*

**Expressive E Touché**
Controller. Loudon owns it (confirmed 2026-10-12). Its companion plugin, Lié, maps synth parameters to it; see the Expressive E row in [[Loudon's Toolkit — catalog — inside the computer|Inside the computer]]. How he uses it has not been gathered.
