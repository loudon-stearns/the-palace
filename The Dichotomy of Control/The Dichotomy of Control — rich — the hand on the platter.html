The hand on the platter

A turntable: a platter, a motor that turns it through a belt, a sensor that reads its speed, and a hand that is not the motor’s to command. Seven steps, one new thing in each, and last the page’s own sentence about actuation authority put against the platter. The platter is a model with round numbers; its percentages are its own, not a real turntable’s.

    Two tones: 440 Hz for 33⅓, and the platter at 440 times its speed. When they beat, the platter is off. The window shown, in real time.
