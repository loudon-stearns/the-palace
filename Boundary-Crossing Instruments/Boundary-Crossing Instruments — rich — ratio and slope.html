One compressor, two knobs

A drum loop, made here from noise and sine, goes through one compressor. The compressor is a single curve that maps how loud a sound comes in to how loud it goes out. Two knobs can set that curve: the ratio everyone labels it with, and the slope underneath. They set the same curve, but they are not the same knob. Six steps, one thing to notice in each, and the last is a blind test. Nothing plays until you press Play.
