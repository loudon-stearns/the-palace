### 2026-10-29 — One Strip, Six Stories: the same frames, color as the only variable

**What it tested:** whether color alone can change what a fixed strip means. The same six frames, drawing and clock throughout. Four takes set a mood (anger, nostalgia, wistfulness, depression). Two set an arc by moving the palette across the panels: warming toward the end opens a story, draining toward the end closes one. Underneath is one wordless story: a man in the rain loses a glove and does not notice.

**What it showed** (one viewer, one sitting):
- The arcs carried far more than the moods. The ending, warm color draining to grey, was the clearest story and "by far" the most emotional.
- A mood can become a plot. The anger take read as someone getting rid of the last evidence of a crime.
- Nostalgia read true. One of the cool takes read only as neutral.
- The beginning failed, because the drawing (a glove thrown or dropped) contradicted the warming. The fixes: stay desaturated until the close-up, or make the fall an accident, the mishap that starts the plot. A tool cannot carry a meaning the other tools contradict.

His answers were lost once. He filled them in inside the browser pane, and when the session read the page every field was empty, so he retyped one overall comment.

### 2026-10-28 — The Color Hold: where one red arrives

**What it tested:** the page's claim that one color held back hits harder than a full palette. Five blind takes of one six-panel ink strip on an 84 bpm clock, differing only in where the color is and on which beat it arrives.

**What it showed** (one viewer, one sitting; scored for punch out of 5):
- Red withheld, revealed on the close-up's downbeat: 5
- All ink, no color: 4
- Red arriving two beats late: 4
- Red shown from the moment the glove falls (panel 3 of 6) and held: 3
- Full palette: 2

A color became an event only once the strip had taught its absence, and the timing of the reveal did as much work as the color. Loudon took the result further than the page had: color is one tool among several, and its rank among them is untested.
