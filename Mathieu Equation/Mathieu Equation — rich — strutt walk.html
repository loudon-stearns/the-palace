The Strutt diagram, walked

The diagram is a map of two numbers, a and q, and every point on it is a sound. One unit of √a is 110 Hz here, so the pump, at twice that, is 220 Hz. Silent until you press or click.

The bare line, q = 0

With no modulation the equation is a plain oscillator, and a only sets its pitch: √a × 110 Hz. Click anywhere on the line. Every point rings and holds its level. There are no tongues yet, only the places they will grow from.

Lift q, at a point outside the tongues

Now the stiffness breathes, with depth q, at a fixed a = 2.2, between the first two roots. Start from a whisper of noise, or pluck it. The pitch wobbles; the level does not climb. Outside the tongues every solution stays bounded.

ẍ + (a − 2q cos 2t) x = 0
(how the mass speeds up) + (a stiffness that swings by 2q around a, at the pump's rate) × (the stretch) = 0. The Mathieu equation, whole.

q at 0 puts you back on the bare line.

Lift q, at a = 1

The same depth, at the first root. Nothing plucks it, and the whisper starts far below hearing. Any q above zero puts this point inside the first tongue, and the whisper grows until the engine's catch holds it: a soft cubic spring and a loss that wakes only when the swing is large, the "something nonlinear" the section names. Below that size the engine is the linear equation exactly.

The whole plane

Every point at once. Amber is growth, brighter for faster; the blue ground stays bounded. The lines are each tongue's edges, measured, so the thin ones show where the colour cannot. Click anywhere to hear that point from a whisper, or switch to pluck.

Measure the tongues

The section makes three claims: the tongues root at a = n², they widen with q, and the first is the strongest, the rest weakening fast. Choose a depth. The table measures each tongue at it, and finds each root by measuring again at half the depth and running the centre back to q = 0.
