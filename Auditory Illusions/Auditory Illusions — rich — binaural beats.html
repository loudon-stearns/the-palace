Δφ(t) = 2π · (f_R − f_L) · t  ·  Δt = Δφ ⁄ (2π f_L)
The phase between the ears turns one whole circle for every cycle of the difference; read as a time, it is the interaural delay the brainstem measures, here sweeping across ± half a period.
the two tones
air: x(t) = sin(2π f_L t) + sin(2π f_R t) = 2 cos(π Δf t) · sin(2π f̄ t)
In the air the two waves add, and their sum swells and fades Δf times a second: the beat is in the waveform. Between the ears nothing adds; each ear’s level is flat.
