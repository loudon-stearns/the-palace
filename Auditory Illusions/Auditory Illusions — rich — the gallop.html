The gallop

One rhythm, two tones. Pull on it.

then drag the dots: up and down pulls the two pitches apart, left and right squeezes the time
flo = 1000 · 2−g/24 Hz  ·  fhi = 1000 · 2+g/24 Hz  ·  slot k starts at tk = k · T  ·  pattern: lo hi lo —
In words: the two tones sit either side of 1000 Hz, g semitones apart. A slot starts every T seconds; three slots sound, the fourth rests, and the four repeat. That is the whole signal: no echo, no filter, nothing else.
for each sounding slot: a 40 ms tone rendered here (a sine, 5 ms raised-cosine ends) → AudioBufferSourceNode → gain → DynamicsCompressor (threshold −3 dBFS, above every tone’s peak, so it never acts) → speakers. A clock schedules each tone 150 ms ahead, so a drag changes the next tone, not the one sounding.
