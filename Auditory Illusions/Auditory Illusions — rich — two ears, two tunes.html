f = 440 · 2(m − 69)/12 Hz  ·  step i: rising note m = C‑major[i], falling note m = C‑major[7 − i]  ·  rising note → left ear when i is even, right ear when i is odd
In words: two C‑major scales at once, one climbing C4 to C5 and one falling C5 to C4, four notes a second. At every step the two notes trade ears, so each ear gets every other note of each scale.
