R(f) = L(f) · e^(i·φ(f))  ·  |e^(iφ)| = 1  ·  φ(f₀) = −180°
The right ear’s noise is the left ear’s with its phase turned, and the size of every frequency untouched; the turn sweeps a full circle across a narrow band, half a circle at its centre.

D(f) = L(f) − R(f) = L(f) · (1 − e^(iφ(f)))  ·  |1 − e^(iφ)| = 2 at f₀, ≈ 0 far from it
Subtract the ears and everything identical cancels; what is left is a narrow band of noise at f₀, which you can hear as a whistle. The eye’s version: subtract the two images and everything identical cancels, leaving the shape.
