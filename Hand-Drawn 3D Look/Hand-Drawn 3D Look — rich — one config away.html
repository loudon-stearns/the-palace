1 · One line

Freestyle finds the edges of the 3D scene (silhouettes, creases, borders) and draws each one once, in one weight. No noise, no pen, nothing chained twice. This is where the true edge is; the next steps measure every other line against it.

3 · The confident line

The catalogue's #9: PLAIN single strokes, a Bézier fit, a wider calligraphy pen, a trace of wobble. "A decisive hand." Drag the wipe: left is tentative, right is confident, the same scene.

4 · Four knobs

"One config away" is one line-style block, and inside it ten values move. They group into four knobs. Turn them one at a time and walk from one corner to the other; each of the sixteen settings is its own render.

5 · Which knob carries it

The page names the causes: SKETCHY chaining and heavy noise for tentative; PLAIN, Bézier smoothing, the calligraphy pen and a trace of wobble for confident. The meters can't read a feeling; they can say which knob moves the marks the page names. You are the instrument for the rest: click any row to see it.

Each knob's effect is the change when that knob alone flips from the tentative side to the confident side, averaged over the eight settings of the other three.
