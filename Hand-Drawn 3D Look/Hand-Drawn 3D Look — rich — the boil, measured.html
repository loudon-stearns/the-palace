The boil, measured

BLUELINE's smoke plume from 03_motion.py: displaced spheres through a toon ramp, sketchy Freestyle ink, and a handler that re-seeds the line's Perlin noise "on twos". Rendered again here, each job in a fresh Blender 5.1.2. Five steps: one frame, twelve frames, the boil the page names, the boil nobody set, and last whether a frame is a function of the playhead.

1 · One frame

An ink plume: six churning blobs, two tones of value, a sketchy line around them. No fluid simulation and no bake, so the scene itself is the same every time you ask for frame one.

smoke · frame 1 of 12

This is the element the page means: atmosphere, staged behind the figures. The line here is Freestyle's SKETCHY chaining with two noises on it, spatial noise (amplitude 3.5) and Perlin noise (2.5). Keep the second in mind; the boil the page names lives there.

2 · Twelve frames

The plume rises and sways at 12 frames a second, and the ink wavers as it goes, which is the hand-drawn feel. Now turn off the page's boil (the handler that re-seeds the Perlin noise) and play it again.

3 · The boil the page names: on twos

Hold the plume still at frame one and do only what the handler does: set the Perlin seed to frame ÷ 2, rounded down, plus one. Frames 2 and 3 share a seed, 4 and 5 share the next. That is "on twos": the line redrawn every other frame.

seed(f) = ⌊f ⁄ 2⌋ + 1
The Perlin noise's seed for frame f: the frame number halved and rounded down, plus one, so it changes on every even frame and holds on every odd one.
