4 · A composition seed, not an appearance

The page's claim, as a test: at 0.95 with loose Canny the model should redraw the medium (many new marks) while the plate's composition still guides it (its edges still held, well above what chance would hold). Both numbers, every rung, at a tolerance you choose.

held = |{ p ∈ Eplate : d(p, Eredraw) ≤ r }| ⁄ |Eplate|  ·  new = |{ q ∈ Eredraw : d(q, Eplate) > r }| ⁄ |Eredraw|
Held is the share of the plate's edges that still find an edge in the redraw within r pixels; new is the share of the redraw's edges with no plate edge within r. Chance is held again, with the plate's edges slid about 35 pixels four ways: what a drawing this busy would hold by accident.

Each rung moved more than denoise: Canny went 0.85 → 0.55 → 0.42 and stopped earlier, and the 0.95 rung has its own seed. The proofs never moved denoise alone, so the ladder shows the balance the page names ("the denoise/ControlNet balance decides everything"), not denoise by itself.

5 · Eight seeds, one plate

The same plate, the same 0.95 recipe, eight seeds. The page says the inking swings "from light line-sketch to splatter-bomb while the structure stays locked". The swing is plain to see. Does the structure stay locked? Pick a seed; violet is plate edge it lost.

above chance = (held − chance) ⁄ (1 − chance)
How much of the way from an accident to a perfect hold the seed got: zero is no better than a slid plate, one is every plate edge kept.
