The drive

A waveshaper is a table: a sound goes in as a place on the table, and comes out as the height it finds there. Here the sound going in is one cosine, and the tables are Chebyshev's. Five steps, one thing to hear in each. The last pushes the cosine past the ends of the table. Nothing plays until you press Play.
