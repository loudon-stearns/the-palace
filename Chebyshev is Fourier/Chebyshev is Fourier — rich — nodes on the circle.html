Nodes on the circle

The section says Chebyshev’s points, the ones that beat Runge’s phenomenon, are just equal steps round a circle, seen from below. Four steps: the shadow, the steps, a curve that goes wrong through evenly spaced points, and then the same curve through the shadows’ points, measured. Nothing here makes a sound.

Top · the circleA circle, squashed to fit, and the line across it. Dots on it are equal steps of angle; dashed lines drop each one to its shadow on the line.
Below · the curveGold: the curve to rebuild, 1 ⁄ (1 + 25x²). Blue: a polynomial through evenly spaced points. Violet: one through the shadows’ points. Grey: Taylor’s series at x = 0, the one-point reading.
