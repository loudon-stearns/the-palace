Chebyshev is Fourier · wallpaper groups on the torus · four steps

Selection rules

The section says a wallpaper group decides which cosine modes cos(mφ₁)·cos(nφ₂) a torus surface may carry, and zeroes out the rest. Four steps: one mode, many modes, one symmetry move, and then the test, where you ask for a group and count what it actually does. Every surface is heard as a scan across the torus. Nothing plays until you press Play.
