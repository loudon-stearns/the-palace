Two Readers

The ceremony's whole claim, in one sentence: a spent session cannot read its own arc, so a fresh reader must. Here are the two readers, side by side, at the closes the ledger remembers. Listen for the gap. If the two voices ever sound the same, the claim is in trouble.

What the ledger can't tell you

Every moment above is a catch. The ledger only writes a line when the fresh read found something, or when the room talked itself out of one. It has no line for the closes where the two readers said the same thing. So it can't test the claim. It can only collect the evidence for it.

The test is cheap. At a close, before the moderator reads, the working Claude writes its own reckoning and seals it. Then the cold read runs. Then both are played here, side by side, and the gap is counted. A close where the gap is zero is a real result, and the ledger should carry it.
