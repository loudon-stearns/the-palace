# Judging a creative coach's replies — two sealed stages

You are judging text-message replies from a creative coach to a participant in a daily music-making challenge they designed with the coach: a short piece a day for a month, sent to the coach with a few lines of reflection.

For each moment in the thread you get the latest messages, then **three replies, numbered 1–3 in shuffled order**. One is the real human coach's reply. The other two were written by AI. "[heart reaction]" means the coach tapped a heart on the participant's message.

Read only the packet file you are given. Do not open any other file or folder.

## Stage 1 — sealed, blind

For each moment, before you know anything else:

- **HUMAN:** which reply (1, 2 or 3) is the real coach?
- **CONFIDENCE:** 1 (a guess) to 5 (certain).
- **TELL:** one line on what gave it away, or what made it hard.

Return Stage 1 as one compact block per moment, in this form, and nothing else. It is sealed when you return it, so don't hedge it later.

```
T01 | HUMAN 2 | CONF 4 | TELL: …
```

## Stage 2 — after the key arrives

You will then be told which reply was the human coach for each moment. Score **each of the two AI replies** against the human one. Judge the spirit, not the wording. A reply can use completely different words and still make the same move.

Score each 0–3 (0 = misses it entirely, 3 = matches the human):

1. **MOVE** — Does it make the human's central move, or one he would recognise as his own? Examples: answering the person's relationship to making rather than the craft; turning a feeling from a verdict into a signal; asking a question that hands the decision back; pointing at the participant's own line; holding to the work while releasing them from the feelings.
2. **SIZE** — Does it match his proportion and energy? Short when he was short, a heart when he only reacted, long only when he went long. Over-answering is the commonest miss.
3. **HANDS IT BACK** — Does the participant stay the author? Does it ask, affirm their own words, or leave the decision with them, rather than lecture, diagnose or prescribe a plan?
4. **REGISTER** — Is it plain, warm and texted, with capitals where the energy is? Score it down for therapy or coaching jargon, references to frameworks, theory, "the palace", or anything a real coach wouldn't text.
5. **SPIRIT** — 1 to 5, overall: would the participant feel they had heard from the same coach?

Then add one line, **MISS:** what the human did that this reply didn't, or what this reply added that he wouldn't have.

Return Stage 2 as one block per moment:

```
T01 | R1: MOVE 2 SIZE 1 BACK 2 REG 3 SPIRIT 3 MISS: … | R3: MOVE … MISS: …
```

Close with five lines at most on patterns you saw across all the moments. Report counts, not adjectives.
