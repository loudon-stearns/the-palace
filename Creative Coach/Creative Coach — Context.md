# Creative Coach — Context

This is the memory behind [[Creative Coach]]: why a page that began as a harvest's survey now carries a month of one person's coaching, what the harvests and tests left out, and the two questions it has handed to Loudon and not had back. Read it before adding a row to either table, before editing § In the room, or before running the next coach test. The page is the practice. The [[Creative Coach — scroll|scroll]] holds the plan and the standing orders. The tuning ledger holds what the tests taught, as scores and counts only.

## Why it is shaped as it is

**Born from a harvest, as a survey.** The page was minted from the 2026-09-06 harvest "Environments That Hold", approved by Loudon section by section in Cowork and landed sixteen days later (2488e3d9, 2026-09-22). It grew one line of [[FOUR PILLARS]], "creative coach, not teacher", into a practice. It had four sections then: Loudon Live as the worked example whose limit is the point, the eleven-tradition repertoire, the defenses read as an evidence problem, and appropriate responsiveness as the open problem. Two more harvest sections folded in over the next two days. The three-person room came from §7 (da27e5e3), turning on Loudon's own account of what AI does well and what it doesn't. Balint groups came from §8 (674f262c), as the unsteered contrary of that room at group scale. The harvest record is `_ops/Harvest Ceremony/Harvest — 2026-09-06 — Environments That Hold.md`.

**The contradiction with Hilaritas Generator is on purpose.** The mint commit says so. Coaching needs the room, and the mission reaches people who are never in it. The link is typed `contradicts` and labelled `never-in-the-room` so the tension stays held and does not get resolved.

**Then the room came back.** On 2026-10-14 Loudon named Loud'n Live as his teaching and coaching face, with one-on-one coaching already running (016ec791). That turned the page's "no room exists" into "the cohort room is the one still to build". On 2026-10-17 § In the room arrived (1b730846). A trainer agent drafted it from one participant's thirty-day month of daily loops and tested earlier drafts blind against Loudon's real replies. It grew twice more: v2.2 added craft as a reward, the coach's clock and a missed loop (1048e842, 2026-10-18), and v2.3 added the close of the month (dd785c28, 2026-10-21). The `version` field tracks that section. It entered the frontmatter with the test kit (a3c5e35e, 2026-10-18).

**Privacy shapes the prose.** § In the room quotes only Loudon and paraphrases the participant. It gives no name, place, work detail or dated message, and the Sentry reads the whole of it before any commit, because two harmless details side by side can point to someone (tuning item 10). These rules are the scroll's Standing Orders. They explain why the section reads as patterns, not anecdotes, and why its opening says "His lines below belonged to their moment". Any edit to the section goes through the same read.

**It teaches reasons, not lines.** § In the room is written as the reason under each move, with about ten quotes. That came from the tests, below. A woken page given many of Loudon's lines replays them.

**It keeps saying what it is.** The forward vector ends "I am a survey with one worked case and a good argument, and I should keep saying so" (3ef3f0a2, b1af8475). The page holds to that by placing a caution in each section: "his practice seen, not proven", "untested", "surveyed, not adopted". The cautions are the page being honest about one participant over one month. They are not hedging to tidy away.

## What the page left out, and where it went

- **The tests.** Four runs between 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17, and fifteen lessons, are in [[Creative Coach — tuning]]. The harness is in `coach-test/`: the README, the prompt builder, the two-stage judge brief and the scoring scripts. The private inputs are not in the palace and must never be.
- **The participant's text.** It stays in Messages and in a session's scratchpad, and is never committed (Standing Orders; tuning item 7).
- **The evidence ratings** the eleven traditions arrived with. They were dropped as unverified. The page says so in one clause.
- **Fifteen person pages** the harvest proposed. All were declined at §9 (2e7a0379). Juncos alone folded into the Acceptance & Commitment row, and the Kenny claim was not verified and not deposited.
- **The homogenisation line** from harvest §6. It was declined on 2026-09-23 (839174a3) as a population finding, since the single-voice risk already lives in [[Self-Describing Knowledge Module]].
- **A line that mentioned "private".** It tripped the security filters and was removed (59626194, 2026-09-26).
- **Two links.** [[Loudon Live]] left the frontmatter at the 10-24 weave's one-link-per-pair fold (769c93ad), and the body still carries it. The `balint-stance-unturned` link to [[Sidechain ↔ Conversation]] was cut in round two (050af498). The harvest had flagged that label rather than changed it.

## What was tried and dropped

- **Quoting Loudon heavily.** The v2 draft had about 25 quotes. On unseen moments the woken coach reused his phrases where they didn't fit and served a plan he had already set aside, scoring 2 and 2 of 5. v2.1 cut to about ten quotes and taught the reasons instead (tuning item 8).
- **The blind "which reply is human" stage as the first test.** It was void. Dropped apostrophes in one source gave the human away, and chained context leaked answers between moments (item 1).
- **Counting a pattern in place of its reason.** A trainer saw one deferral and wrote five. Loudon's account at the close, that craft is a reward, replaced the count with the reason (items 9 and 11).
- **Holding the month's last eight moments back as a clean test set.** Loudon folded them in instead, so the page carries the whole month: "The test can wait for a second room" (96a169b8, 2026-10-21). As a result nothing from the first room can test the page now. The plan's first step waits on a second participant or the cohort room.
- **"No artist Balint group exists."** The claim was dropped at the §8 fold because it could not be verified (674f262c).

## What it leaves open for Loudon

- **"Point at the body" against "takes it at its word."** § If the room held three teaches the body probe from his experience. § In the room says he doesn't ask how someone feels when the reflection already says. The first test found the probe pulls a woken coach into questions he never asks in this practice (item 4). Item 12 narrows the tension: one participant who names feelings cannot show what he does with one who doesn't. When each move applies is his call, and the page does not yet say.
- **The heading "surveyed, not adopted".** On his account three of the eleven traditions are in practice, unnamed (1048e842). Whether the heading should change was left to him.
- **The composting rule.** Since the 10-24 weave (a75350ec) the line above the tables says that a row which has never changed a move the coach would make is composted at the next Weave. No weave has applied the rule yet, so the next weave to read this page should decide row by row, or ask him.
