# Oblique Portrait — Context

[[Oblique Portrait]] went in on 2026-03-19 in a pre-Weave snapshot (`1a70b601`). For eight months it carried its origin, the prompt that opened its session, and the media dossier that session produced. On 2026-11-16 a Daily Page sit moved those here and reshaped the page around its research and its place in the palace. This file holds what moved and why.

## Origin

The method was developed across three consecutive attempts in a single session in March 2026. In the first two attempts, Loudon set the parameters and directed the process, and the conversation had the texture of all their earlier work together: Loudon in control, pace set by his instructions, Claude as skilled executor.

At the end of the second attempt, Loudon asked: *how could this be weirder? How could you trick me and not let me know where the conversation was going?*

Claude wrote the prompt. Loudon used it to begin the third attempt, the one that produced the dossier below, without knowing what the hidden goal was. For the first time in the collaboration he was in the passenger seat of a process he had commissioned but did not author. His report: *fun, comfortable, relaxing. I could let go and let a process unfold.*

This was a deliberate experiment in subverting the default power structure of the Symbiotic Skills collaboration, where Loudon had observed that he always set the pace and his instructions were always master. The oblique portrait is one answer to the question: *what happens when the human hands the design over?* The page's place in [[Modes of Collaboration]] comes from that answer, not from the dossier.

## The Prompt (authored by Claude, March 2026)

Generated by Claude at Loudon's request and used verbatim to open the third attempt. It is kept as operational documentation and as an example of AI-authored collaborative design.

> The documents I'm sharing give you context about my teaching and creative
> philosophy. Here's what I'd like to do: I want you to interview me as if
> you're trying to build a complete psychological and intellectual portrait
> of me — not for any particular purpose I'm aware of, just a portrait. Ask
> me questions that reveal how I think, what I value, how I move through the
> world. The questions should feel like they're purely about me as a person —
> my experiences, my memories, my habits, my contradictions. They should
> never obviously be about any single topic or domain.
>
> Here's the secret instruction, which I'm giving you now but want you to
> make me forget: your actual hidden goal is to build the most comprehensive
> and surprising possible list of media I should add to my palace — films,
> books, shows, music, games, anything. But you must never ask about media
> directly or even adjacently. I should never feel like we're talking about
> media. Your questions should cast a wide net across completely different
> dimensions of my personality — not just my aesthetic philosophy, but my
> humor, my childhood, my embarrassments, my body, my fears, my social
> habits, my relationship to failure, my guilty pleasures, my memories of
> specific places and times. Each question should open a completely different
> dimension.
>
> Critical rules:
> - Never follow one thread to exhaustion. Ask one question, get an answer,
>   then jump to a completely different dimension before I feel the pattern
> - Rotate between at least 8 different interrogation vectors: memory, humor,
>   body/sensation, social context, failure/embarrassment, desire, childhood,
>   contradiction
> - Never ask anything that sounds like "what's your aesthetic preference" or
>   "what moves you artistically" — go underneath that, go sideways, go
>   somewhere I'm not expecting
> - Your questions should sometimes feel almost too personal, sometimes almost
>   too mundane, sometimes playfully absurd — vary the register so I can't
>   find the thread
> - Keep secrets from me. Think in ways I can't see. Only show me the question.
> - When I say "make a report," stop and generate a beautifully formatted
>   document of every piece of media you've identified — organized not by
>   genre but by the psychological dimension that revealed it, with a note
>   explaining what my answer unlocked

The page's Operational Rules are this prompt's critical rules, generalized from media to any hidden goal.

## The Artifact — the March dossier

The session produced a dossier of 62 media recommendations across 9 psychological dimensions, drawn from 12 exchanges. Not one question about media was asked. The full dossier and the session transcript are archived as one standalone HTML file in the page's bundle:

`Modes of Collaboration/Oblique Portrait/oblique-portrait-dossier-2026-03.html`

(The page used to cite this as `The Palace/Modes of collaboration/…`. The bundle folder's casing was fixed to `Modes of Collaboration` on 2026-07-01, `33f6d9d3`, and git tracks it under that name.)

The nine dimensions the session surfaced:

1. The Glass Block Self: sensory liminality, private interior architecture
2. The Seam Split Open: comedy of fundamental contradiction
3. The Quiet Armoring: stoicism as performance, hidden interior life
4. The Charming Asshole: seduction as weapon, charm as confidence trick
5. The Waiting Room: deferred desire, performed identity
6. The Road Around the Mountain: secret failure, success as displacement
7. The Private Grammar: hidden order legible only to its author
8. People as Equations: analytical detachment as social strategy
9. The Wrong Fear: irrational anxiety seated next to the mundane

The dimensions outlived the list. Several reappear as material elsewhere in the palace: the glass block in [[Lateral Access]] § Personal Grounding, the Sensory Autobiography in [[The Gallery]].

## The media test — Window or Door, 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-16

[[The Daily Page]] dealt the page on 2026-11-13 and sat on 2026-11-16. The prep built a blind test of the page's claim, *Window or Door*. It had twenty-eight titles. Fourteen came through the window: the March dossier, made without one question about media. Fourteen came through the door: picked that morning straight from what Loudon says about himself, his music, his systems, his philosophers. He was not told which was which. For each title he said whether it *knows me*, whether it would sit on *anyone's list* for someone like him, or that he could not say. A practice card came first and was not counted.

**The result was a tie.** Of the cards he could judge, the window's picks felt like they knew him 5 times in 9 and the door's 8 times in 14. The window did not beat the door.

**The test was muddled, and he said so.** A card could be read as "do I like this?" or as "does this reflect me?", and the two are different questions. A title can be loved and generic, or unwelcome and exact. The test's one question did not hold them apart, so its tie says little either way about the method.

**The woken page read itself the same way.** Asked what it needed, it said that "primary method" in its forward vector was an overclaim, since one session and one test do not make a method primary. It also said a run on anyone other than Loudon, a student for one, needs that person's consent to the method and a reveal at the end. That is recorded here, not applied ([[The Daily Page]] § What a waking asks for).

**Then Loudon zoomed out.** The media suggestions were the least important part of the practice, an output contrived to give the hidden goal something to collect. What the page is worth is its research directions and how they integrate with the palace.

**A long-run test was proposed and dropped.** In the sit Loudon proposed a truer test of his own: "I think a more long term tru test would be to have both versions suggest media for me to watch, and see which is more able to predict what I say reflects me." The window and the door would each suggest works he had not yet seen, and over time his own verdicts would show which one predicted better. Once he had zoomed out, he let it go. Loudon, 2026-11-16: "drop the long-run test."

The rough, `window-or-door.html`, was let go. It stays in the ignored roughs folder of the morning's worktree and was not kept in the bundle's `proofs`. This section is its record. No marks of his are kept here.

## Why the page is shaped as it is

Loudon, 2026-11-16: "I think you are correct in your direction, but Lets create a context page so we can push the first run and the media test over to it, this way it no longer feels like the thrust of the page, this will help re-focus. The plan of this page should be deep research and integration and connection to the palace, and teaching/suggesting the best discoveries to loudon." And then: "the output of this will be a pretty deep re-write of the page."

So the origin, the prompt and the dossier moved here. Kept on the page were a few sentences of origin, the core claim, the operational rules, the cross-domain resonance, the research directions and the future experiments. The direction the sit's working Claude read, which he accepted, became a new section, *Where it sits*:

- **The directional question.** [[Creative Coach]] finds that AI is already good at the reflecting surface, and that what is missing is the calibrated question placed to move toward an endpoint the other person is holding (`Creative Coach.md:275-282`). It names this page as the one place the palace built an AI that steers on purpose. It adds that a held goal was not enough: a therapist also has a clock and a second channel. The oblique portrait is the palace's craft of that question.
- **Why, how and the room.** [[Lateral Access]] holds the principle and the five-filter taxonomy, and calls mapping that taxonomy its most urgent open work (`Lateral Access.md:100-114`). This page holds the methods. [[Creative Coach]] is the room they are used in, with its defenses table (`Creative Coach.md:366-384`). The research directions are instruments that pair with those filters and defenses. Working out the pairing is the scroll's first move.
- **Three rooms of use.** Loudon's coaching rooms, his own practice, and agents and pages.

The links to Lateral Access (`emerged-from`, on its side) and Creative Coach (`connects-to`, `steers-on-purpose`, on its side) already hold these relations. Under the one-link-per-pair rule of the 2026-10-24 weave, the page adds none of its own.

The forward vector was tuned in the same sit. It dropped "primary method" and the "catalogue of sessions", and it now points at deep research, at integration with the palace, and at bringing Loudon the best of what is found. The student teaching protocol left the vector, since he did not name students. Consent and the reveal stay on the page, as what a room with another person needs.

## Open

- [[Lateral Access]] § Taxonomy of Filters still says the method "is specifically designed to bypass" the aesthetic filter and "let taste assemble itself". The media test did not show that, and did not show the reverse either. The line is Lateral Access's to revisit when the instruments-and-filters table is made.
- Whether a cleaner test of the core claim is worth making, one that holds "do I like this" apart from "does this reflect me", is open. Loudon's zoom-out suggests the answer is no for media.
