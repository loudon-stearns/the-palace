Two runners, three ways to keep or lose the lap

Two phasors read one surface: one runs across it, one runs up it. Drawn as runners, each has a track; drawn on the unrolled torus, the pair is one point leaving a trail. The spectrum underneath is what you hear. Grey ticks mark the lattice the runners' set speeds predict, m·f₁ + n·f₂. Amber ticks mark the lattice at the speeds the runners actually keep. Until the last step the two sets of ticks sit on each other.
