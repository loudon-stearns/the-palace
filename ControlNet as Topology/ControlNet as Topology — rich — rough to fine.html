Rough to fine: what each stroke can decide

The studio's claim that the first strokes commit the pose and the last ones the detail, built up from the raw block. Four steps: pure noise, one stroke of fog and the best guess under it, the scales the image is made of against the noise, and last the test across the whole schedule, beside a control image with no coarse shape to give. No model runs here: the noise is real, the guess is the best a linear filter can make, and the numbers come from the image's own spectrum.

the figure in the block
y = x + σ · z
(what the carver sees) = (the figure) + (the fog's size) × (white noise). White noise puts the same power, σ², into every scale, coarse and fine alike.
decided at σ:  every scale f with P(f) ≥ σ²
a scale is decided once (the figure's own power at that scale) is at least (the noise's power there). P(f) is the image's power in waves f cycles across the frame. Photographs and drawings put far more power into coarse waves than fine ones, P(f) ∝ f to the power −2 or so, so as σ falls the line where they cross moves from coarse to fine.
x̂(f) = P(f) ⁄ (P(f) + σ²) · y(f)
the best guess a linear carver can make: (each scale of the guess) = (how much of that scale is figure rather than fog) × (what the carver sees at that scale). Where the figure is louder it is kept; where the fog is louder it is let go.
σ(p) = (σ_max^(1/7) + p · (σ_min^(1/7) − σ_max^(1/7)))^7
the schedule: (the fog after a share p of the strokes) falls from 14.6 to 0.03, the end points Stable Diffusion uses, along the curve Karras and others use (ρ = 7), which spends more strokes on thin fog than thick.
The images are 256 × 256 grey squares cut from the page's own hero and from a GenAI Camera render (render 003, Stable Diffusion XL), scaled to zero mean and unit spread. Stable Diffusion carves a compressed latent, 64 × 64 by 4, not pixels; its latents keep the coarse-heavy shape of a picture, so the order holds there too, at different numbers. A real denoiser is not linear: it can guess fine detail before the fog strictly allows, from the coarse shape it has already seen (an edge implies a sharp step), so read the crossing as what the noise alone permits, not as when a network commits. Power is averaged over rings of the same frequency and smoothed over ±10%. The noise is seeded and the same at every stroke, so moving the slider only moves σ.
