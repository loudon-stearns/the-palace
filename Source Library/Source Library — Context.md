# Source Library — Context

Why [[Source Library]] is shaped as it is, read from its git history. The body has changed very little since its first day. Nearly everything that happened to it happened to its surroundings.

## Born as part of a teaching programme

The page entered git on 2026-03-25 in the palace restructure (`46d904f`), at `Artifacts/4 Pillars Framework/People/`, alongside a framework master plan and about twenty person pages. Its frontmatter at birth already called it a hub, and it `enables` a Weekly Themes Database. It was written as the reading spine of that programme. That is why it speaks to "students," lays out a reading year, and grounds its examples in weekly themes.

## The experiment dissolved around it

On 2026-06-16 the 4 Pillars Framework folder was dissolved (`871cde1`). The commit calls it "an early online-presence experiment (superseded by Loudon Live)." The canon was rescued: the person pages went to `People/`, and this page moved to the root as a hub. The rest was deleted: 35 Weekly Themes, the Weekly Themes Database, and five theme entries including Kick Drum Paradox, Four on the Floor and High Pass Low Pass. Kick Drum Paradox's worked example lives on in [[FOUR PILLARS — Context]].

The same day's weave removed the link to the deleted database (`cc01c30`). It also retyped thirteen person pages' links to this page as `member-of`. The body was not touched. So § How Sources Connect to Themes still gives its three examples from themes that no longer exist as entries, and the teaching-programme framing throughout is the dissolved experiment's.

## A vector came later

The page had no forward vector for its first three months. The 2026-06-16 follow-up weave added one, because it was "a confirmed hub, 33 inbound, had none" (`bcb39b2`). The vector aims at an inhabitable roster in the sense of [[Pages as Agents]], which is newer than the body and is not what the body describes. The [[Stoicism]] link, labelled home-tradition, came in with the unsung-paths bulk of 2026-06-26 (`198833f`). Labels on the [[FOUR PILLARS]] and [[Cross-Domain Resonances]] links came in October (`769c93a`, `17e99b3`).

## The roster took in one library while others let go

On 2026-09-24 the September weave composted the Media Library page (`8972a53`). Some of its sources lived nowhere else. At Loudon's call they were brought here in Media Library's own words (`efdd69a`). The commit counts six; the diff adds eight names: Debussy, Satie, Stockhausen and Merzbow among the musicians; *Particles of Attachment* among the filmmakers, marked unread; Plato's *Timaeus*, Feynman and Turing in a new Scientists & Classical Sources section. That is the only real change to the body since birth.

The same September weave also let go the person pages for Tarkovsky, Malick, Goldberg, Maloof and Schafer (`8972a53`), keeping lines from some of them in neighbouring pages. That commit did not touch this page. The roster still names all five, and the forward vector still names Schafer as a voice it keeps pulling in. The roster never linked its names, so nothing went ghost. A reader can't tell from here which names have a page.

## For the next hand

If the page is relaid, three things are owed, and git explains each. The theme examples need new grounding, since the entries they cite are gone. The roster may want to mark which voices have pages. And the curriculum framing should be weighed against the vector's roster aim. Which way that goes is Loudon's call.
