Six against forty

The page says the top partials set the edge: stretch bites partial n roughly as B·n³, so a tall spectrum tears long before a short one, and forty partials tore over a hundred times sooner than six. This walks up to that and then measures it, live, on the model the sweeps ran. Nothing plays until you press Play.
