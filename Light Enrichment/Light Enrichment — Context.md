# Light Enrichment — Context

What [[Light Enrichment]] left out, and why the card is shaped as it is. Moved here on Loudon's "agreed on th econtext file" (2026-11-11, relayed by the working session).

## The first round

November 2026: seventy pieces on sixty-eight pages. Of the first forty, the challenge kept sixteen and sent back twenty-four; none was retired. Of the last thirty, it kept seven, sent back twenty-two and retired one. Four challengers read those forty for about 800 thousand tokens by their own counts. The forty were committed before they were challenged, which cost a second commit to correct them; step 7 is that lesson. And none of the seventy was made by reading its page as a first-time reader: each was anchored to one sentence found by search, and no maker took stock of what the page already showed. They stand as proposals, made before that principle was said. The round also used almost nothing of the Shop or the projects: every sound was synthesized from nothing and every figure drawn by hand, no Specialist was asked for anything, and no project's instrument made a sound. Its range was the range of one maker's bare hands. The challengers of the last thirty were not asked about it either, and several of their verdicts reach it unasked: a list of another hub's members beside a sentence about something else; twenty-one rectangles that say what the quotation beside them already says. The workshop, with the makers, the challengers' reports whole and Loudon's words in order, is `Enrichment/light-round-2026-11/`; what the challengers found wrong on the pages themselves is gathered there in `offers for the pages.md`.

## The case behind the Shop-and-projects direction

One piece in the first round was about a p5.js recipe. Its maker simulated the recipe by hand in another language instead of running it in p5, and got the recipe wrong: the veil's alpha was read as 14 of 255 where the palace's sketch uses 14 of 100, so the trails faded nearly three times too slowly (`Enrichment/light-round-2026-11/challenge/batch-D.md`). It was remade. It is the round's one piece of evidence for the card's section "Where this is headed": the tool was in the Shop, and the hand went around it.
