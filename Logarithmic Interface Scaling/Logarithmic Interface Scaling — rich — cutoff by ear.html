Cutoff by ear

The page’s diagnostic: does doubling feel like the same gesture as halving? Here it is asked of a filter cutoff, a value that spans three decades, 20 Hz to 20 kHz, and is heard by the ear rather than read off a number. A buzzy tone runs through a resonant low-pass filter, so you hear the cutoff as a bright peak that moves. Four steps; the last is blind. Nothing plays until you press Play.
