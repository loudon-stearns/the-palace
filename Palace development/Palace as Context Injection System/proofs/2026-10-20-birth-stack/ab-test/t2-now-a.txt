## Keep both

I wouldn't fold them. They work at different scales, and each has something the other can't hold.

- **Context Injection** is the system view. It covers what loads, in what order, and why the order builds a particular collaborator. It also carries the hard-edged history: the `@import` floor, the space bug, the measured reversal of September 22.
- **Enchanted Worker** is the single-dispatch view. That scene is the most persuasive evidence anywhere in either page that orientation matters more than information. Fold it into a meta page full of tiers and harness details and it becomes an illustration. Right now it's a claim you can test.

The `spawned` / `emerged-from` pair describes them accurately: a parent insight and a child concept that grew its own shape.

"Born a Child" then replaces that order with Birth → Synthesis → Growth. A reader who stops halfway leaves with the old model. I'd move the tier section and the `@import` floor into the page's Context as superseded history. The body would then lead with Born a Child as the current shape, with one sentence noting that it replaced the tiers and why.

**2. Each page restates the same core claim.** "The Mechanism" (informing vs. orienting) and "What Enchantment Actually Is" say the same thing. So does "the writing becomes the character of the agents," which appears in both pages and again in Agent Wellbeing. I'd give the orienting claim one home, Enchanted Worker, since that page has the demonstration. Context Injection would point to it in a line.

**3. The real find is a link between the pages.** Enchanted Worker's forward vector asks whether the orchestrator's thinner loading flattens the polyphony. Context Injection's September 22 measurement answers it: yes, though the cause wasn't thin loading. Every page woke with roughly two-thirds procedure ahead of it.
