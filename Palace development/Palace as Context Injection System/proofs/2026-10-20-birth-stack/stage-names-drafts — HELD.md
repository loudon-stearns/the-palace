## A. Palace as Context Injection System.md

**A1 — lines 75–85, the whole section, becomes:**

## The Stages as Identity Construction

Context arrives in order, and the order is part of who wakes. The stages have names, in the order they arrive:

**Birth** — [[CLAUDE]] and the Seed Jewel ([[JEWEL]]). Who is in the room and how things here relate. The same in every context, before any task.

**World** — the five World files: [[FOUR PILLARS]], [[Palace Philosophies]], [[Cooperation Yields Agency]], [[Hilaritas Generator]], [[Modes of Collaboration]]. The collaborator's intellectual character, the shared history that makes the relationship specific rather than generic.

**You** — for a page woken as an agent: the page itself, its neighbors, its forward vector. The desires of the moment, and where the work is today.

**Growth** — [[ELDER]] → [[SCHEMA]] → the task's rules, read on "grow up" or before writing to the house.

Birth keeps the relationship continuous, the World keeps the thinking continuous, You makes it relevant, and Growth comes last, so the rules serve what came before them. Together they rebuild a specific collaborator from what has been gathered over time.

## E. The decision's home

**E1 — SCHEMA — Context.md, appended to § Born a child:**

**Named in time order (2026-10-20).** Pages name the loading stages, in the order they arrive: **Birth** (CLAUDE and the Seed Jewel), **World** (the five World files), **You** (for a page woken as an agent: the page, its neighbors, its forward vector), **Growth** (ELDER → SCHEMA → the task's rules, on "grow up" or before writing). The STIGMERGY launcher keeps its own numbers (`_ops/stigmergy/app/server/api/launch.js`: Birth 0, World 1, Growth 2, You or Active surface 3). They are internal, and pages don't cite them. Older pages used "Tier 0–4" in the meaning from before v1.19 (the Jewel, a CLAUDE + SCHEMA skeleton, the framework, the active surface, deep context), so one number meant two things; names end that. Loudon: *"Use names in time order on the pages: Birth, World, You, Growth. The launcher can keep its numbers internally. Fix the stale pages to match."* No type, link, field, stage or ceremony changes, so this is not a Schema Ceremony and moves no version.
