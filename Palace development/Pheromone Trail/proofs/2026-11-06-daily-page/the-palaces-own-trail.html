Does the palace's own trail reinforce itself?

The page's face tests an ant colony. The colony answers for ants. This sketch asks the palace instead, from its own git history: do the pages that were walked keep getting walked, and do the ones nobody walked go cold?

1 · What counts as a step on the trail

A visit is one commit that touches a page or its bundle, and touches no more than 5 pages in all. That keeps the deliberate visits and sets aside the sweeps, a Weave or a batch that touches everything at once. A sweep is the queen, not an ant.

2 · Guess first

Before the numbers, your hunch. Three questions, then the reveal opens.

Of the most-visited quarter of pages in A, how many are still in the top quarter in B? Chance alone would say a quarter.

Of the pages with no visit at all in A, how many stay unvisited through B?

Which predicts a busy B better: being heavily linked to, or having been busy in A?

3 · The rules, fixed before the run

The trail reinforces if the top quarter stays top at least 40% of the time (chance is 25%), and cold stays cold if at least half the unvisited pages stay unvisited. The amber mark is the rule; the white mark is chance.

4 · The catch: B was a busier month

Window B had 6.7× the visiting commits of A, so almost everything warmed: 66% of all pages were visited in B. Read against that, the cold pages were still less likely to be visited (62%) than the warm ones (77%). The trail tilts the odds. It does not decide them.

5 · The ant that walks off the trail

The palace already has one. Of the pages the Daily Page dealt, 12 had no visit in window A. In B they had a median of 6 visits each; the other cold pages had a median of 1. The shuffle does not aim at cold pages. It ignores the trail entirely, and because most pages were cold, chance lands it on them, the way one ant in ten choosing at random frees a worn colony in the face's third piece.

Honest limit: a sat page's own morning commits count as visits, so part of this is the shuffle measuring itself. The fair version counts only visits after the morning was closed. Not run yet.
