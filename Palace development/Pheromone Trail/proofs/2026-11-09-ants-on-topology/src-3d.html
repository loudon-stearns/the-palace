Every ant is a commit.
Where they walk, the paper rises.

One ant = one commit. It leaves the nest, visits the pages that commit touched, and carries the work home to main.
Teal light = ants walking a typed link. Thicker means more ants, more lately. The light floats above the ink it was drawn from.
Amber dashes = no link there. Two pages worked together anyway: a desire path.
Raised, lit paper = pages visited lately. Flat and dim has gone cold.
Drag to turn the sheet. Shift-drag to slide it. Scroll to come closer.
What is real here, and what is a guess

Real: every page, its avatar, the day it was born, the day each typed link first showed in frontmatter, and every non-merge commit with the pages it touched. All read from git by build.py, beside this file.

A guess: the order an ant visits a commit's pages. Git records which pages, not which first. The ant follows a link when one exists, and otherwise goes to the nearest page. So which pairs show as desire paths leans on that choice.

Left out: a commit touching more than 12 pages sends one ant per page, out and back, and lays no trail between them. Those are sweeps, not walks.
