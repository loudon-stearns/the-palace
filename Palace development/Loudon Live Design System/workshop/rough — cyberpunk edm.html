Rough 7 · cyberpunk EDM

Three directions for the third look. Each keeps Worn Broadcast's bones (Anton, the colour fringe, scanlines, grain, a glitch now and then, the sigil) and pushes them toward a future city and a dance floor. All three run on one 128 BPM clock, so they move like a track: a 16-step sequencer lights in time, a bar and beat counter ticks, and every eight bars the drop lands with a tear. H is the neon city, where everything ducks on the kick like a sidechained pad. A is acid: a 303's resonant filter sweeping, warehouse tape, halftone. N is a neon sign in the rain, with the track's waveform running along the bottom.
