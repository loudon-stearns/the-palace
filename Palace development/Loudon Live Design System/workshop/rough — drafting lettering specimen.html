Drafting lettering · specimen

Rough 4's title lines set in six precise faces, all in capitals on pencil guidelines. Every line is straight. The slight looseness comes from placement: each letter sits a fraction of a degree and a fraction of a pixel off its neighbour, the way a hand sets letters against a guide. G is rough 4 as it stands, for comparison. The traditional standard, ISO 3098 lettering (osifont, ISOCPEUR) or Frank Ching's architectural hand (Tekton), isn't on Google Fonts.
