Rough 8 · night signal

The three directions folded into one, and dirtied. Deepest is a generated night plate: rain on dark glass, the city out of focus behind it, drifting slowly. Over that come haze and the old circuit board from Worn Broadcast. Then the instruments, with a real bloom pass so they glow: the 303's filter sweeping, a spectrum analyser, the sigil in three colours (magenta and cyan as a stereo pair, amber behind). Then a synth panel, six neon knobs over a keyboard, playing the 303 line, and under it the neon sign, pumping on the kick. Rain falls in front of the sign at two depths, with droplets on the lens. On top: dust, scratches, light leaks, a VHS tracking band rolling through, heavy grain and hard scanlines. Every eight bars the drop lands: a big tear, datamosh blocks and a shake.
