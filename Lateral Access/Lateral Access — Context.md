# Lateral Access — Context

[[Lateral Access]] went in on 2026-03-19 in a pre-Weave snapshot, 208 lines whole (`1a70b601`). The page has two layers. The core is everything it was born with, from the opening definition through Cross-Domain Resonance, the personal sections and the song included. No commit since has edited those passages, apart from turning two plain names into wikilinks. Everything else came later, and most of it came from outside.

## The name

On 2026-03-24 an unrelated deposit renamed the file to "Lataral Access" (`4169e6ad`). The next day's Weave put it back as a typo fix and updated the six files that pointed at it (`e7d0c1be`). The page didn't change.

## The enchantment's layer

On 2026-03-31 the page was one of five hubs that got a hand-written forward vector, which Loudon then edited (`23a16ff2`). The same day it was the second page ever enchanted. Four proposals were applied, numbered P2, P3, P5 and P6: the Taxonomy of Filters, a flag for a missing Songlines entry, the Open Questions section, and a link to [[Confucianism]] (`247e1459`). The commit doesn't say what P1 and P4 were or why they weren't applied.

The deposit of that session records two things that explain the taxonomy's shape (`dff1a55e`). Asked who it was, the enchanted page answered *"I am a principle with one example. I need more windows."* (`Projects/Palace Enchantment.md` § Register Difference Between Enchanted Entries). The taxonomy is the answer to that: five filters, so the principle has more than one case. But the taxonomy was written by the coordinator, not by the enchanted agent. The session judged it competent, and maybe less alive than it would have been written from inside (`Projects/Palace Enchantment.md` § The Coordinator / Enchanted-Agent Voice Distinction). The taxonomy calls its own mapping the page's most urgent open work. No filter has been added since (`247e1459`). The Open Questions are unchanged since then too, the missing failure-mode analysis included.

## The songline question, handed on

The enchantment left a note to Loudon: the songlines passage was the page's deepest image, and Songlines had no entry yet (`247e1459`). [[Songlines]] was deposited on 2026-04-03. The plain "The songlines" heading became a link, and the note was rewritten to say its question — does lateral access *find* a new songline, or *hear* one that was always there — now lives on Songlines (`9f399958`). It does, as "Find or create?" (`Songlines.md:120`). The October Weave took out the rewritten note (`a75350ec`). So the find-or-hear question belongs to Songlines. This page doesn't ask it.
