### 2026-11-09 — Still True? and The Door

**What it tested:** whether a page that copies other pages' rules stays true. Still True? takes ten sentences from the Guide as it stood at `ddfe9e3f`, plus one practice sentence that doesn't count. For each, the reader says whether the house still does what the sentence says, and the reveal cites the page that owns the subject.

**What it showed:**
- Seven of the ten sentences had drifted from the pages that own them.
- Loudon, playing blind, agreed with the house on five of the ten. A reader can't tell a stale line from a live one.
- So the page was rewritten that morning as a door that holds nothing another page owns (`1992eb9a`). It also became the root `README.md`, generated from the page (`b2b4e4c8`).

The Door is the first sketch of the rich face as a newcomer's map. A stranger picks a reason for coming, and the map lights up the pages that answer it, each in its own forward vector. Its graph is a snapshot of the map built 2026-11-09, embedded in the file. The forward vectors of SCHEMA and ROSETTA did not show, because a regular expression missed them. It goes on to [[Enrichment]] in this bundle's baton.
