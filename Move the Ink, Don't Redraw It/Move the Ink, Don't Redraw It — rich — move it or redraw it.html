Measured here on the mp4 previews in BLUELINE's report-assets, with stylize.py's own formula: mean |frame − frame before| over every pixel, × 255. The bars follow each clip's playhead from a table measured once, frame by frame, not from the pixels your browser decodes. The AI frames (renders/smoke_ai_flick) were never committed and are not on this Mac, so the 1.50 can be read but not re-measured; the bench's debrief says that run "sampled sparse frames with more real motion between them".
ft(x) = I(x − dt(x))
Each frame reads the one inked still I, looking back along the field's arrow d at each pixel. No frame is drawn; every frame is the still, moved.
dt = Σ aj · sin(kj·x + φj − 2π cj t),   cj whole
The field is five slow waves. Each turns a whole number of times c over the loop, so at t = 1 every wave is back where it started, and frame 60 is frame 0.
change = 255 · meanx |ft(x) − ft−k(x)|
The page's meter: how much the picture changed between two frames k apart, averaged over every pixel, out of 255. It cannot tell a mark that moved from a mark that was redrawn. Read it only where nothing moves and what is left is the boil.
