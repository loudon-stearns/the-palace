The cut rides the black line

One real plate, BLUELINE track VI's sky over a ridge, cut two ways and slid apart. Five steps: the edge the artist already drew, one hard cut along it, the sky sliding behind, a feather beside it, and last the test of where the knife should sit. The view is a 280-pixel corner of the plate, the ridge's right shoulder, drawn at its own pixels.

1 · The edge is already drawn

Before anyone cuts anything, the ridge's edge is on the paper: black mass on one side, white paper on the other. The artist did the segmentation with a brush.

4 · The same slide, with a feather

The soft mask we started with: the same ridge, but its opacity fades across the edge instead of stopping at it. Watch the right-hand view as the sky slides.
