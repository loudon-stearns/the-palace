# Mermaid Diagram Standard — Context

## Born in the restructure

The page arrived on 2026-03-25, in the palace restructure that also made [[Image Embedding Standard]] (`46d904f`). The same commit deleted a nine-line "Test Mermaid" page that had tried labelled, dashed and thick edges. Five days later the standard moved into `_ops/` (`b54785f`). Its `version: 1.0` has not moved through any of the edits below.

## From companion to one of three

At birth it called itself Image Embedding's companion, and said the two together formed "the complete visual language". The visual-language workstream of 2026-05-28 created [[Technical Diagram Standard]], and with it the paragraph that takes signal flow, DSP figures and plots away from raw SVG and gives them to LaTeX (`023dd3f`). On 2026-06-09 the opening was rewritten to name three standards (`6fbff69`).

## The argument, shortened

At birth § Why Mermaid Over SVG was five bold-led paragraphs. It argued that Mermaid is "of the same substance as the palace itself", that its diffs join git's narrative where an SVG's are "XML coordinate noise", that it is greppable and Obsidian-native, and that its small grammar is a discipline. The October 2026 weave's folder-ops room called this "the page's most-quoted and least-needed part" and proposed two sentences (`87d2d07`, `lighter-prose.json`). Loudon approved the prose batch (`6c356eb`), and the writer cut it to the two that stand (`a75350e`, `writers/log-set-3.md`). The same pass reduced Technical Diagram Standard's restatement of the argument to one sentence pointing here. Anyone who wants the long version can read `46d904f`.

## Paths that moved under it

The demo SVG the page compares against lived in `Artifacts/Images/`. When Artifacts dissolved into bundles, the file moved into Image Embedding Standard's bundle and two of this page's references were repointed (`cc01c30`). A third, which told external figures to go in `Artifacts/`, survived three more weeks before it was fixed as "an active mis-citation" (`e97c039`). The live example's "140 lines of XML" has never been true. The SVG has been 115 lines since it was committed (`46d904f`), and its move was byte-identical (`cc01c30`). When activity counts were retired, the xychart line changed from "activation counts over time" to "an entry's use over time" (`32cb19e`).

## A proposal it now both makes and declines

The open question about having the Weave produce a Mermaid topology block was answered the other way: the Weave's visual report is HTML. The annotation saying so landed in `a75350e`. The room also asked to delete the matching "longer-term possibility" paragraph in § Mermaid in Ceremonies. The writer left it, because a body deletion was not in the approved batch (`writers/log-set-3.md`). So the page still proposes in one section the thing its questions record as declined.

## Left open

- **Fold or keep.** A room proposed folding this page into Image Embedding Standard. Checkpoint 2 put the question to Loudon differently, since its real sibling is Technical Diagram Standard, which names Mermaid fourteen times: "Fold into Technical Diagram Standard, or keep?" (`70982aa`, `checkpoint-2-for-loudon.md` § E). The merges that landed on his yes name only Walk That Weaves (`6c356eb`). No commit records his answer for this page.
- **Its link vocabulary is behind.** The weave's coherence room found the page says "eight canonical types", and its edge table leaves out `contradicts`, `exemplifies` and `member-of` (`87d2d07`, `coherence-findings.json`). [[SCHEMA]] §4 now has twelve, `contrasts-with` and `made-by` included. The room also found that the caption section repeats Image Embedding's word for word. Neither fix has been made.
- **No forward vector.** It is one of ten `_ops` pages the same room found without one (`87d2d07`).
