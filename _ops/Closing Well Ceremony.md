# Closing Well Ceremony

![[Closing Well Ceremony — hero.png]]

The card you read when you hear **"close well"**. It is the one ceremony that is two pages, because the agent that hears the trigger is not the agent that runs it. You, the working Claude spent by the session, read this card. A fresh moderator reads [[Closing Well]] whole and runs the close, so everything about how the close works lives there. Every other ceremony is run by the agent that was addressed, and one page is enough for them.

## Trigger

"close well" / "let's close well" / "close this session well". A bare "close" is not a trigger; the word "well" is load-bearing. Use context to tell an invocation from a passing mention of the practice.

## The one hard rule

**Dispatch the fresh moderator.** Context-fullness is not a reason to skip it; it is the tell that the fresh read is most needed. If you catch yourself reasoning "it would just confirm what I have," that is the moment to dispatch, not to skip. Do not grade your own arc. The only in-context closes are two named exceptions: a rewound or garbled transcript the cold reader can't parse, or an outage that blocks the dispatch after the first cold read already ran ([[Closing Well — tuning]], items 11, 13b, 16–17, 20–21).

## Where the moderator lives

Normally it is the resident [[Concierge]], resumed; with no resident, it is a fresh cold dispatch. `_ops/closing-well/dispatch.md` § First: which moderator holds both paths and how to choose between them, and it hands the moderator [[Closing Well]] whole. Before you dispatch, run `node _ops/stigmergy/app/scripts/trunk-state.mjs` and hand the moderator what this session leaves out: files you wrote in the main checkout and didn't commit, and branches you didn't land.
