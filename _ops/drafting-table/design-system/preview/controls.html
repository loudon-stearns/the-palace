THE BROWSER'S FURNITURE, ON THE SHEET

Every control here is the bare element with no class. controls.css loaded after the tokens dresses it: square, a hairline is the control, what the hand holds is a slab, every value is also written as text.

Scrolling panel

The scrollbar is the one thing the sheet keeps from the terminal: radius 0, a thumb in rule, no OS chrome. It wakes to ink-2 under the pointer and goes ink while dragged.

A scrolling panel used to arrive with the operating system's own bar, a rounded grey pill in a light gutter, two arrows nobody drew.

Now it reads as a part of the drawing. Keep scrolling: the panel holds more than it shows.

Firefox and Chrome read the standard properties; Safari reads the pseudo-elements.

Textarea

Resize is vertical. Focus is a recess (shadow-recess) and a border in ink, no glow on Paper.

Fields

No spinner: arrows and the wheel step the value, as every control on the sheet does. Pickers and popups follow color-scheme.

Select

The chevron is two hairlines in ink-2 drawn with gradients. No icon, no SVG, no hex.

Checkbox and radio

Checked is ink with the ground ringed inside, the terminal's ● on. A radio is round because it is a turned part.

Range

A fader: a hairline track and a slab cap, outlined in ink, lifted by shadow-slab. The cap never shows its number; the page writes it beside the label.

Buttons and file

The bare element: a quiet slab. The twelve components in bundle.css do the real work; this is the floor under a page that writes a button with no class.
