# Palace Graffiti — Context

Why [[Palace Graffiti]] is shaped as it is, read from its git history. The history is short and falls into three parts: a birth essay that has barely moved, a month when the page was used to hold other pages' drafts, and an October weave that trimmed it.

## Born inside another commit

The page has no commit of its own. It entered git on 2026-03-24 inside a deposit about archiving an oscillator artifact (`4169e6a`). The next day the palace restructure moved it from the root into `_ops/` (`46d904f`), and it has stayed there since. Small changes followed. Open Questions became Forward Vectors (`3633346`). The Oblique Portrait Method link took the shorter name (`b54785f`). The Trickster in the body became a wikilink (`1749553`). Apart from those, the essay part of the page is still close to its first day.

## A holding pen, May 2026

In early May the old card-based Enrichment treated this page as the place to park "larger revision" drafts: changes to other entries, held for Loudon's review and not applied. Five commits between 5 and 9 May:

- **2026-05-05, `8da2e1f`** — the proposed edits to [[Action Potential Oscillator]] on the word "isomorphism." Its commit message says the drafts are held here "NOT committed to the APO entry yet."
- **2026-05-05, `5c9149b`** — a long section on forward vectors as conatus, not stasis, drawn from Loudon's reaction to the [[Phoneme Choir]] 2050 stub.
- **2026-05-05, `5ef3efe`** — that conatus section removed the same day and replaced with a one-line Loudon quote about specialist manifests. The commit is titled for a different page and has no body. It gives no reason for the removal and names no destination. The same principle, in Loudon's same words, is now carried by [[Entry Conatus]], which entered git on 2026-05-09 (`a5ca537`). Git does not record that one moved into the other.
- **2026-05-05, `f83d3ec`** — a dated note that the Trickster / Coordinator / Worker / Palace frame had been re-derived independently, offered as evidence the names hold.
- **2026-05-09, `e58d7b2`** — the proposal to untangle [[Retrospective Delay]] and [[Semantic Delay]], the trickster mythology to one and the looping-the-past trick to the other.

This is why the page ends the way it does. The two surviving drafts sit below the essay's closing line, as visible sections. That cuts against the page's own subject, which is marks left in HTML comments that no renderer shows.

## What the October weave cut, and what it couldn't

On 2026-07-08 the re-derivation note's link to the retired agent-infrastructure spec was unwrapped to plain text (`0932b7b`). The note itself lasted until the weave of 2026-10-24 (`a75350e`). The weave's prose findings and its writer's log, both committed with it, record each call (`_ops/swarm/sessions/weave-2026-10-24/lighter-prose.json`; `writers/log-set-2.md:25-31`):

- **Cut.** The Session A / B / C timeline block under the temporal section, because three paragraphs already said it. The "should be mentioned in Substrate" and "should affirm this connection" lines, as notes to a future editor and not facts. The manifest quote and the re-derivation note, as dated evidence about retired specs: "git holds them."
- **Annotated.** The Graffiti Walk question, marked answered by [[Multi-Lens Worker Prompt Template]], whose workers already flag live graffiti.
- **Skipped.** Moving the two May drafts to the owning pages' ledgers. The files lay outside the writer's set. Neither draft had been applied. And [[Action Potential Oscillator]] points its readers here for the proposed edits, so deleting them would orphan that pointer. Both drafts are still owed a home. The check is easy: the oscillator page still says "structural identities," and Retrospective Delay still has its Ghost Metaphor section.

In the link pass the same day, [[Semantic Delay]] went from `connects-to` to `mirrors`, labelled deferred-meaning, and [[Oblique Portrait]] gained its label (`17e99b3`).

## For the next hand

Once the two drafts land in the oscillator's tuning ledger and the two delay pages, the page's tail can go. The Action Potential Oscillator pointer has to be repointed in the same move. What remains then is the essay as it was born.
