# Palace Quotes

![[Palace Quotes — hero.png]]

A page that leaves quotes on other pages. It reads a palace entry through one of the palace's own philosophical lenses — [[Spinoza Conatus]], [[Stoicism]], [[Confucianism]], [[Like Water]], [[Deleuze]], [[Whitehead]], [[Simondon]], [[Merleau-Ponty]] — finds the sentence the entry rests on, and lays a real line from that tradition beside it. The entry's own words are never touched. The quote sits in the margin, named and anchored, and says in one breath what the lens sees in those words that the page could not say about itself.

It began as an archive — eight quotes that had arrived in the middle of real work and refused to leave. The archive is gone; those eight became the first placements, each on the page its origin had already named. A quote is not something to keep. It is a window to put in a wall.

## What a placement is

One line in this page's own ledger, carrying six things: the quote and who said it, where; how far the words are from the source and how the wording was checked, which the surface shows as one glyph (◉ checked and verbatim, ◐ a checked translation or paraphrase, ◇ unconfirmed); the lens it was read through, which must be a palace page; the page it is laid on and the exact phrase it is laid beside, which must be on that page or the placer refuses; the why, one or two sentences; and its standing — `placed` until Loudon reads it, then kept or let go. A page can and should carry several quotes, through different lenses, beside different sentences. The placer stamps the section's fingerprint at the moment of placing, using the rich face's own code ([[Enrichment]]), so when the page's words move the quote says so rather than pretending it still fits. The fields and the commands are in `_ops/quotes/README.md`.

## How it reads a page

This is [[The Lens]] with a fixed payoff. Woken as the glass over a page, Palace Quotes runs the lens's two-attempt read — a first reading, then the fidelity check: *did I deploy a real apparatus, or borrow the page's vocabulary?* — and then, where the Lens offers a link or a deposit, it offers a quote. One thing it drops: the Lens reports a spark to the WEAVE board and asks TRICKSTER at four or more, and a quote is too small for that. Palace Quotes posts to no board. The placement is the report, and the "awaiting your read" count in STIGMERGY is the inbox. [[Philosopher Visits the Entry]] is the long form of the same act; this is its smallest, most repeatable member: one voice, one sentence, one line laid beside it.

The lens is the question; the voice is whoever has answered it well, from any field. So the reading runs the other way round from a roster: first the page and the pages one link away, then the question *who, in any field, has stood exactly here* — a luthier, a drummer, a mathematician, a monk — held to the palace's own test for a resonance, same skeleton, different flesh ([[Cross-Domain Resonances]]). A voice the palace has never heard is the hoped-for find, and the placer holds it to a web check with the source kept, so the quote is also how Loudon meets the person. Only when the open reading surfaces no one real does the reader fall back on the roster, `_ops/quotes/voices.json`: the palace's own citizens first, whose speech files already carry cited lines, then makers, musicians, scientists, writers and other traditions, so the pool is never only philosophers. The lens is named afterwards, and it may be any palace page — the neighbor that opened the door as readily as one of the twelve.

The method, in full, is in `_ops/quotes/prompts/quoter.md`. Its guard is short: illuminate, never overwrite. A quote that would fit any page fits none. Do not deflate — a lens that always finds the page over-claiming has become a formula. Never invent a line; a paraphrase marked as one is honest, and a visit that places nothing is complete.

## How to reach it

- **Wake it as a glass.** On any entry in [[STIGMERGY]], `[L] lens` and pick *Palace Quotes* — or `node _ops/quotes/place.mjs --mandate "<Page>"` prints the prompt for a Claude Code session.
- **Ask the working session.** *"Leave a quote on <page>"* — it reads this page and the quoter's method, finds the line, runs the placer. The placer writes only this page's ledger, so a child session may do it; no other page is changed.
- **Harvest intake.** The [[Harvest Ceremony]] counts "a quote for Palace Quotes" among the signs a source is worth depositing. A harvested quote is placed on the page it belongs to, not collected here.
- **Keep it or let it go.** On the QUOTES lens, or on the mark itself, `y` keeps the quote and `x` lets it go — one keystroke, one committed ledger line. The "awaiting your read" chip walks only the unjudged ones and moves on after each answer. A let-go quote can be kept again; the line stays with its reason. From a terminal, `place.mjs --keep <id>` and `--retire <id> --why "…"` do the same on Loudon's word.

## Travelling by quote

The QUOTES lens on [[STIGMERGY]]'s STATE deck shows one quote at a time, large, and under it the page it opens onto. From there the walk goes three ways — more quotes on this page, quotes through the same lens, quotes on pages one link away — or draws one at random; stepping through the window lands on the mark in the page, and every mark on a page offers to pick the walk up from there. It is the [[Walk Ceremony]] with quotes for doors: no destination, the path is the knowledge, and a walk that finds nothing is complete.

## Where the quotes live, and why

Beside the page, not in it. The text face is what a page says, and a quote placed by another page must not change that — the same rule the rich face keeps when it lays sound and image beside a section. So the record is this page's own ledger, and STIGMERGY lays it on the text face of every page. Only kept quotes leave the house: the public read view shows what Loudon said yes to. A kept quote earning a place on the page's **rich face** as a piece of its own is the agreed next step, once more pages carry rich faces; every placement already carries the fingerprint that step needs.

## Open questions

- When does a quote earn its way from the margin into the page's body — and should it ever? (The Visit's open question, one size smaller.)
- Can a page ask for a quote — name in its forward vector the lens it wants read through?
- Several traditions in the register have no page (Plato, Leibniz, Zen, Taoism proper) and quote through the [[Palace Philosophies]] hub. Each is an invitation to write the page.
- Is a quote from Loudon's own pages a different kind of window — the palace quoting itself — or the same act?
