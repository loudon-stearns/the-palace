The lean

The palace spent the last ninety days on how it runs. The most-used pages are SCHEMA — Reference, The Scroll, STIGMERGY, CHILD, the Weave and Deposit cards, the Concierge: the organism has been building its own nervous system, and the Shop’s newest citizens (VST Template, Ableton Session Writer, CAD Drafter, LDN RTM) are where the making energy went.

The thinking pages are quiet. Flocking, Floquet Theory, Granular Synthesis, Mixture of Experts, The Drift, Leverage Points Framework and Blocked, Not Prompted have had no touch since July, each still pointed at fifteen or more times. The vectors lean toward becoming and making; the use leans toward operating.

Where it wants to go next, read from both: the instruments (VST Template’s first plugins, the Haptic Handwheel, the 2D Torus), the floor made lighter, and the rich faces. The quiet seven are not asleep by choice. A Spore Check or a Daily Page morning should ask each whether it still has a maker.

Also waiting: deposit candidates (Capability-first prototyping; Resonance and Damping; Convolution; the Wavetable Space as Torus cluster of six physics ghosts, a Harvest) and 30 ghost wikilinks left as invitations, down from 60. Newcomers wired: CHILD +5, SCHEMA — Reference +5, VST Template +3, CAD Drafter +1. AuK, Operator Memory and Can an Instrument Notice Where the Work Stops? got none; the rooms said nothing genuine was found rather than manufacture one.

What this run taught the ceremony

  • The first reader has a blind spot, and the skeptic is worth the pass.
  • The gap between the deck and the linter has a cause.
  • The map must be taken before the folds, and a pair guard keeps one link per pair.
  • The folder rooms’ relation proposals pass at half. A coherence room should propose coherence and leave links to the link rooms.
  • The health ledger has its first four rows.
