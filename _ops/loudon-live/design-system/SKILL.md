# Loudon Live · design skill

Loud'n Live is Loudon's teaching and coaching face, online and in person — YouTube videos and livestreams for electronic musicians, the RTM series, one-on-one coaching — where instruments get built, sound gets studied, and music gets played. It is a moving target: `Projects/Loudon Live.md` is its current portrait and wins when this file disagrees. The visual system is a **floor, not a cage**: a tiny non-negotiable floor (the studio voice, the Lissajous sigil, the nevers — no emoji / no hype) keeps everything recognizably Loudon, and a wide field above it is meant to vary. The **house style** below (Anton / Cormorant / Manrope / JetBrains type, six skins, the wordmark) is the reliable default to reach for — steady grammar, fresh skin per stream — but a projection may depart from it. Loudon presents as many projections (loudon, LDN, Loud'n Live, Professor Compressor, Sonic Sensei, TRICKSTER); the house style is the home of the **Loud'n Live** projection specifically. Hold the floor always; treat the house style as default, not law.

## How to use this skill

1. Read `README.md` in full. It carries the **content fundamentals** (voice, casing, person, no-emoji rule, do/don't copy), the **visual foundations** (type, the six palettes, the six generative elements, spacing, radii, motion, hover/press/focus, transparency rules), and the **iconography** position (typographic glyphs + the Lissajous trace + the LL monogram — and explicitly no emoji and no CDN icon set).
2. Read `colors_and_type.css`. It is the canonical source of every token used by every artifact. Switch palette by setting one of `skin-graphite | skin-amber-lab | skin-crt | skin-strobe | skin-cobalt-grid | skin-drafting` on the document root. Graphite is the default. Load `controls.css` after it and before anything of your own: it re-dresses what the browser draws alone (scrollbars, selects, checkboxes, range sliders, the textarea grip, number spinners, `<details>`, focus, selection, autofill, links) in the skin's tokens, and sets `color-scheme` per skin so a dark page gets dark furniture. (The CSS still honours the deprecated aliases `skin-teal-patch | skin-dusk-tape | skin-bone-synth`, but new artifacts use the canonical names.)
3. Browse `assets/` (brand marks + per-stream generators), `slides/` (OBS card templates), `ui_kits/session-artifact/` (long-form session page) and `ui_kits/stream-overlay/` (live composite).

## Looks

Loud'n Live wears complete looks: palette, type, texture and motion travel together and are worn whole, unlike a skin. There will be a few. The first is **Worn Broadcast** (Loudon, 2026-10-25), `looks/worn-broadcast.html`: STIGMERGY's phosphor and grammar carried most of the way, with Anton, the amber LIVE and the Lissajous sigil keeping it Loud'n Live. Its header comment is the spec. Fill it from its URL for an OBS browser source (`?session=…&topic=…&handle=…&title=…&link=…&status=…&ghost=…`). The second is **Cyanotype** (Loudon, 2026-10-25), `looks/cyanotype.html`: the Drafting Table grammar on a dark cyanotype ground, revised by a careful hand (guidelines, erasures, a revision table, a checker's red pencil; every line ruled), with the thin-pen Josefin wordmark. Its header comment is the spec, and it fills from its URL too (`?sheet=…&title=…&subject=…&no=…&status=…&n1=…&n2=…&n3=…&check=…`). The third is **Night Signal** (Loudon, 2026-10-25), `looks/night-signal.html`: cyberpunk and electronic music, layered and dirty, Worn Broadcast's bones pushed into a city at night and onto a dance floor (neon wordmark, a 303 panel with knobs over keys, the sigil as a stereo vectorscope, rain on glass, all on one tempo). Fill it from its URL (`?bpm=…&session=…&chip=…&topic=…&handle=…&title=…`). The set is complete for now. When the artifact is Loud'n Live itself, reach for a look; the six-skin house style stays the default for everything else the public channel shows.

**Making a new look.** Read the tuning ledger first, `Palace development/Loudon Live Design System/Loudon Live Design System — tuning.md`: what the last look session taught, from his taste to the plate recipes. Then five steps. (1) **Reference first**: ask Loudon what is close to the feel, and what look he keeps seeing elsewhere. (2) **Distances**: the first rough is one card drawn at three or four distances toward the source, and he picks one. (3) **Record his words** as they come, verbatim, in the workshop README's Decisions. (4) **Build on the 1920 × 1080 standalone frame** from the first rough: one self-contained HTML file, a header comment that is its spec, fields filled from the URL. Then landing it is a copy. (5) **Land through the Concierge** on his yes: the file into `looks/`, and the look into the entry, this file, `README.md` and the projection roster.

## What to make

The most common briefs:

- **A new session artifact** (long-form HTML page for one stream). Copy `ui_kits/session-artifact/` and rewrite `App.jsx`. Header → escher split (theory opener) → mechanism (h2 + diagram) → build steps (progressive staging) → tuning grid → footer signature.
- **A new OBS scene card** (Starting Soon / BRB / Stream Ended / Topic Title). Copy from `slides/`. Swap the palette by changing the `skin-*` class on `<html>`; swap the generative element by changing the `src` of the `.gen img`. Keep the wordmark fixed.
- **A throwaway prototype / sketch / proof artifact.** Use the Graphite skin, the serif at 17/1.75 for body, and the *Sketch / Study / Piece* tier vocabulary in any internal labelling.

## Hard rules

- **No emoji. No CDN icon library.** Typographic glyphs (`● ▸ · ◐ ◇`) and the Lissajous trace are the channel's iconography.
- **The browser draws nothing.** A scrollbar, a select chevron, a checkbox, a slider thumb, a resize grip, a spinner, a details triangle, a focus ring: each is drawn in the skin's tokens or does not appear. `controls.css` does it for the bare elements; a page never writes `outline: none` without a `:focus-visible` of its own, and never leaves a dark skin without `color-scheme: dark`. The one it cannot dress is `<audio controls>`: draw a `▸ ■` button and a hairline scrub.
- **A page wears its entry's face.** An artifact in an entry's bundle takes the entry's avatar (`<Entry> — icon.png`, [[Hero and Avatar Maker]]) as its tab icon and may pin the hero behind its header where that fits; a public-channel page with no entry (an OBS card, a slide) takes `assets/favicon.svg`, the Lissajous at 32px. `<meta name="theme-color">` carries the skin's `--bg`. A piece shown in a frame declares no icon and carries none: a framed piece never owns a tab, and one declared file would lift its sandbox (`_ops/rich-face/contract.mjs`, `sameOrigin`); the page that frames it (the rich face, a scroll's trail, a TRICKSTER card) wears the entry's avatar as its tab icon. A piece opened on its own from disk goes without, a known gap.
- **No outcome promises in titles.** "Live build · synth lab, session 1" — not "Finishing the synth in one hour."
- **First-person and collaborative pronouns.** "Let's explore" and "hang out" — not "students" or "subscribe and hit the bell."
- **Show every equation twice — symbols and words.** Any teaching artifact (or entry) that carries an equation renders it once in symbols and once with the variable names spelled out, keeping the operator symbols (×, +, √, =) in *both* forms, the worded line directly under the symbolic. Loudon is *learning* the math, not fluent in it: the symbolic form is unreadable without the letter-to-concept map, and the all-prose form loses the structure — the middle ground keeps both. Example: `fₙ = n · f₀ · √(1 + B·n²)`, and under it `(nth partial's frequency) = (partial index) × (fundamental) × √(1 + (stiffness) × (partial index)²)`. The one exception is a reference work where the bare symbolic form is the standard reading.
- **The wordmark is `LOUD’N LIVE`** *(renamed from `LOUDON / LIVE` 2026-07)*. The `’n` connector **case-matches its run**: all-caps settings (the Anton lockup, mono all-caps eyebrows) get a full-size cap `’N` → `LOUD’N LIVE`; title/sentence case (prose, body, signatures) gets lowercase → `Loud’n Live`. **Never strand a shrunken lowercase `’n` between all-caps words.** Said aloud: "Loudon"; read on the page: "loud *and* live." One apostrophe, always. "Live" is the accent word (weight 300, italic in title-case). Plain-text fallback (handles/URLs): `loudonlive`, never `loudnlive`.
- **One accent glow per view.** Reserved for the primary call to action.
- **No decorative gradients in chrome.** The only allowed gradient is the radial protection-gradient under cards on busy backgrounds.
- **Restrained motion.** Cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) for ordinary; .2, .9, .2, 1 for emphasized. No bounces, no springs. The Lissajous trace's slow rotation is the only ambient motion.
- **Studio register, not commercial-production register.** Sketch / Study / Piece — never MVP / v1 / final.
- **Interactive/stateful first versions ship reviewable.** Any interactive build (instrument, explorer, prototype, card series, deck) ships its *first* version with a built-in review surface so Loudon can leave section-level feedback in context, then flip it off to ship clean. Reach for `ui_kits/review-layer/` (the DOM-anchored method) — but new review methods are wanted, not that one reused. Granularity rule: one moment per natural unit (a card, a section), never one per control. Static one-shot artifacts (a single banner, an OBS card) are exempt. **A UI that runs inside another application** (a plugin in a DAW, a Max for Live device) **keeps its review surface on its browser mock page, not inside the host.** The mock is drawn by the same code from the same data, so reviewing it reviews the UI, and the shipped window stays clean. (Loudon, 2026-10-21) Principle: [[Review Layer]]. A piece made for a rich face leaves the kit out: the face carries the review surface, one note per section ([[Review Layer]]).
- **A web piece declares what it needs.** Anything the palace shows in a frame (a rich face, a scroll's trail, a TRICKSTER card) runs on the piece origin, sandboxed, and gets only what it declares in its own `<head>`: `piece:needs`, `piece:files`, `piece:net`, `piece:size`. One HTML file, no build; a CDN library from the shelf, pinned. The palace sizes it, quiets it and keeps its storage apart; post nothing for that. Rules: `_ops/rich-face/README.md` § What the page lets run.

## If invoked without other guidance

Ask the user: *what stream is this for, which look (Worn Broadcast, Cyanotype, Night Signal) or which house skin and generator do you want, and is this a session artifact, an OBS scene card, a banner, or something else?* Then act as an expert designer for Loud'n Live — outputting HTML artifacts that drop straight into OBS or YouTube. Stay in the studio register. The footer of any artifact you ship should read **Loud'n Live** — the wordmark alone (the `· Autodidact Polymaths` tagline was retired from the universal footer 2026-07). Name the audience situationally in prose instead: describe the way of learning ("teaches yourself, across fields"), never label the person ("you are a polymath"); reserve the term "autodidact polymaths" for the long-form / Amber Lab register, said once. See the phrasing bank in `README.md` § *Naming the audience*.
