# Harvest Ceremony — tuning

What each harvest taught the ceremony. Each item names what the run showed and the spec change it forced, if any, or says **owed** when a change hasn't landed. Newest last. Hashes are commits to `_ops/Harvest Ceremony.md`. The number moves when the procedure does — a mode, a beat, a gate — never for prose; why v1.0 starts where it does is in [[Harvest Ceremony — Context]] § The Version and the Tuning File.

## From the first full harvest — 2026-03-23 to 03-24

1. **Item-by-item review fatigued; an oblique game surfaced more.** Three rounds, 640 cards, answered as quick signals — "burns bright", "deposit seed", "skip" — and auto-triage agreed with Loudon almost entirely after about 60 items. Forced: the oblique approach over item-by-item audit (`46d904fe`; Context § The Oblique Harvest).
2. **A frontier, a queue and an archive were three files doing one job**, and coordinating them across surfaces became its own problem. Forced: "use scripting" — extract what you need from large files instead of pre-structuring them (`46d904fe`; Context § The Simplification).

## From the June–July making — 2026-08-25

3. **A lesson sat unowned in five project files.** "Rich first, stylize last" lived in craft notes and nowhere above them. Forced: the craft-to-concept question — of each finding, what is this a case of? (`2fd29e7c`).

## From *Environments That Hold* — 2026-09-06 to 09-24

4. **A dense source is not a big pile.** The first commissioned harvest needed understanding, not finding. Forced: the close reading, six beats per section, beside the sweep (`2fd29e7c`).
5. **The deposit rule was used as a reading rule**, and seven thinkers were nearly skipped. Forced: "Does naming it change a move?" decides deposits, never what Loudon should learn (`2fd29e7c`).
6. **A fold was written before its map was shown.** "If you are let wild on the palace, it just turns into you." Forced: beat 5 — show the deposit map and wait, no exceptions (`2fd29e7c`).
