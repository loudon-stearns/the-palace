## For Loudon

- **Rich-face pieces carry no tab icon, by decision (2026-11-03).** A framed piece never owns a browser tab; the page that frames it (the rich face, the scroll's trail, a TRICKSTER card) is where the entry's avatar belongs. Today it wears none: neither `_ops/rich-face/rich.html` nor the STIGMERGY app sets a tab icon, a gap of its own. Declaring the icon in `piece:files` would give 235 pieces an origin (`allow-same-origin`) and lasting storage (`_ops/rich-face/contract.mjs:217`) for a file the frame never fetches. Known gap: a piece opened on its own from disk has no icon.
- **One rule does override pages: reduced motion.** Its `!important` sits in the layer, and a layered `!important` beats a page's own, so a reader who asks for stillness gets it everywhere. The sheet means this. Every other rule yields to the page.
- **Pages regenerated from the 22 templates will drop the furniture** the next time their script runs; the templates were left alone.
- **Two framed pieces link `controls.css` by `../`** (FM Synthesis staged patch, SUBSTRATE anatomy), exactly as they already link their token sheet, and both declare it in `piece:files`. conform passes; worth one look in the live frame.
- **A page that hides focus on its own selectors still hides it.** The inlined sheet sits in a layer so the page always wins; that also means a page's own `.btn:focus { outline: none }` beats the house focus line. The census counted 132 such pages. Fixing them is a per-page edit, not a sheet.
- **24 pages keep the OS select chevron.** Their own select rules set `background:` as a shorthand, which would erase the house chevron and leave none (the Meadows diagnostic is one). `color-scheme` now keeps the popup on the right side of the light.
- **Self-dressed pages wear their own colours.** The inlined tokens borrow each page's own names where it has them (`--e1`, `--fg1`, `--amber`, `--amberline`, `--accent`, `--paper` …) and fall back to Graphite on a dark page, the Drafting skin on a light one (Phosphor and Paper for Drafting Table pages). Self-dressed pages get Loudon Live's grammar; Drafting Table pages get theirs.
- **Six "look" lines, all explained:** three `margin-left: auto` buttons moved 0.016 px (the scrollbar is 11 px, not the OS 15), an animated glow and a font-shuffling piece caught mid-change, and a `<sub>` taking its `<code>` parent's new mono face.
- **Fifteen pages sit in five entries with no face yet** (Cycle 5 Explorer, Rational Hearing, Synth Templates, Resonance and Damping, The Gallery): they get their icon when the Weave's face audit reaches them.
