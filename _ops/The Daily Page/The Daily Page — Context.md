# The Daily Page — Context

## Where it came from

The practice was proposed and set up just after midnight on 2026-10-13 and first run that same morning, on [[Frequency-Time Explorer]]. It began as a trial in `_ops/daily-page/README.md` (commit `6ff5d384`), deliberately not a ceremony and not an entry. After the first morning Loudon said the process "worked great," that he wanted it to follow palace procedure, and that he wanted the procedure to be a page in the palace. The Concierge audited the practice the same day, drafted this card, and he said: "land it now."

His words below are quoted from two transcripts and were checked against both: the session he set the practice up in and landed it from (`67813ac5`), and the first morning's own session (`bdae7d49`). Times are Eastern, all 2026-10-13.

## Why it is shaped this way

- **A `practice`, and a ceremony.** It is something done, on a rhythm, with a trigger, a postcondition and repeated runs, and its nearest relatives (the Walk, the Spore Check, the Weave) are all `practice` ([[SCHEMA]] §1). The trial was already keeping a ledger by hand: a process note every morning, a "Pending" list, a week-one watch list. A tuning ledger is the palace's form for exactly that.
- **Card in `_ops/`, bundle beside it, machinery in `_ops/daily-page/`.** The split is the palace's own: `Closing Well Ceremony` beside `_ops/closing-well/`, [[Enrichment]] beside `_ops/rich-face/`, [[Sentry]] beside `_ops/sentry/`. The dealer's state (`lap.json`, `visits.jsonl`) is runtime bookkeeping and stays with the dealer ([[Substrate Skill]], the Machinery/Content Split).
- **Two records, one public and one private.** What a morning changes in the palace goes in its commit, and what the mornings teach the practice goes in the tuning ledger, which is tracked. What a morning shows about Loudon goes in the diary, which with the scroll is kept off git: the old ledger stopped being tracked at `2318bf9f`, and it split into the two at `d598db29`. The mechanism is the palace's own for material that stays on this machine: an ignore rule in the folder, with its reason, as the Sentry's `held/` and the dealer's `roughs/` have. Those two are regenerable or meant to be lost. The diary is neither, and it has one copy: the Mac had no Time Machine destination that night, and Documents is not in iCloud. Git treats an ignored file as expendable, so a revert of `2318bf9f`, or a merge of an older branch that changed the old ledger, would write over it. The diary exists in no worktree. The page is a ceremony in every checkout, because it carries `trigger:`; only its diary and scroll live in the main checkout alone.
- **The session's note is its own line, not a field in the run line.** The tail read sets run lines aside ([[SCHEMA — Reference]] §6), so anything kept only there would never reach the next morning. A line that begins `- from the session ·` is not a run line, an order or a numbered item, so the tail read meets it and the scroll's reader passes over it. It is marked as a reading because an agent's account of another person is a guess, and the session that wrote it will not be there to stand behind it.
- **The close is asked.** The line the page draws, a full close for anything beyond a keep or an open that made nothing, stays as where the morning starts from. What changed is who decides: he is asked. He is asked the size of the close, never asked to be its check; the Concierge still reads the list against the tree either way (ledger item 16). No preference means the quick one, because he said to back off when he is not leaning in.
- **The commit is the record.** Every ceremony here records a run in its commit body and a ledger line, and the palace froze its one appended record file, the Deposit Archive, in favour of that. A ceremony's scroll is then made by machine: each run from its ledger line, each version change from its commit. The trial's `record.md` was a second, hand-kept copy, edited in place by more than one session on its first day; its one morning is kept whole in this bundle's Archive.
- **A verdict in flight is a baton.** A commissioning baton is the palace's form for work decided and not begun, and it lives where the work will happen ([[Baton Ceremony]] § Where the Baton Lives).
- **Nothing is written into a page's bundle except a baton and, with it, a rough kept as a proof in `proofs/`.** What an ordinary page's bundle should hold is an open question the palace is carrying on [[Palace Ceremonies]]. This practice stays behind it.
- **The close matches the work.** [[Closing Well]] § Scope gives two scales and does not say what a session is when it holds one movement. The card settles it for its own mornings by what the morning made, which anyone can check against the tree, not by how long it felt. The morning's maker does not grade it alone: on the first morning a resident who had advised the morning's decisions also judged whether the morning had cut a corner, and missed several things a cold read found.
- **A dormant page is dealt, and its revival is not done at the table.** The [[Revival Ceremony]] has never run, and the baton it carries says the card as written cannot revive a spore. The Daily Page is now a second door into revival, beside the Spore Check's `revive`. Whoever redesigns the Revival Ceremony should know that.

## Tensions kept, not settled

- **A Sketch a day against depth.** Enrichment was rebuilt because "five small cards spread across many entries never transformed one" ([[Enrichment — tuning]] item 1). Loudon chose the bias toward making before that was in front of him. The setup session reports telling him of it the night before the first morning; the morning itself did not read it to him; he has not answered. The practice aims at one piece that carries the whole page, most mornings, and the first morning's piece did change its page's fate. One morning is not evidence. The bias stands until he says otherwise.
- **Daily against weekly.** The [[Walk Ceremony]] says "a garden walked daily becomes familiar to the point of invisibility." This practice is daily. The shuffle answers for the pages, since none comes twice in a lap. It does not answer for the ritual. Watch whether the form goes invisible.
- **One waking a lap against "three consistent ones."** [[The Remembering Page]] earns a vector change across several remembered wakings. Here a page is woken once in about eleven months, and the wake prompt loads no memory, so nothing counts the wakings but a person reading old commits. The same entry calls memory for pages that are not people "untested." Recorded-not-applied is right for now; what "earned" would mean at this cadence is not known.
- **Let it go, against the rough that was nearly lost.** At 22:41 he said an unfinished thing is let go. That same morning a rough worth keeping almost went with its ignored folder, and a careful close is what caught it (ledger item 2). Both stand: a thing is let go by name, in the commit, never by accident.
- **A companion, against the shuffle.** The forward vector now leans toward him. The practice exists so that every page is sat with. The page is still what they sit over; watch that it does not become a pretext.
- **A reading of him, written by a session that will not remember it.** The note is one agent's guess about another person, read the next morning by a different session as if it were knowledge. It is marked, kept to a sentence or two, and open to correction. Whether a line of such notes adds up to understanding or to a caricature is not known.
- **Light, against "very meta" in week one.** His order to assess the process as hard as the content is still owed. The session and the ledger carry that work; he is asked little.
- **Asked, against a man just waking.** The close stopped leaning on his attention (ledger item 16), and now a question is put to him at the close. A shrug means the quick one, and that is the one place a cold read is lost.
- **A morning leaves no mark on a page it keeps.** Use is computed from git and reading is not recorded ([[SCHEMA — Reference]] §3). A page Loudon sat with for an hour and kept will read as untouched to the map.

## Week one

The first seven mornings Loudon sits; a missed day is not one of them. Watch: how long a sit takes and whether he wants that length daily; whether the prep is too much or too little; whether the woken page says anything the outside read did not; whether a playable piece in a morning is realistic, and for which kinds of page; what a morning costs (the first: about 114K tokens for the Concierge's boot, about 32K for the waking, and a full Closing Well on top); whether title-first is a pleasure or a test; whether the session's note says anything true about him, and whether asking the size of the close is one question too many. After the seventh, read the seven together and move the version. The ledger carries this as an order from him, owed until then, so every tail read finds it.
