Segment or inpaint: who decides where the figure stands

The multi-camera move puts several lenses on one camera, each with its own prompt, and composites what they draw. Four steps: the environment lens alone, a figure lens from the same camera laid over it, the region the figure may grow in, and last the record’s shootout (renders 012 and 013, one env, one seed), counted: which pixels each method changed, and how many stayed inside the region.

laid over
inside = | changed ∩ region | ÷ | changed |
(how much of the change stayed home) = (pixels that changed and lie in the barbarian’s region) ÷ (all pixels that changed). Changed means some channel moved more than 24 levels from the base it was painted into. The region is his plate’s silhouette grown 40 pixels each way, as shootout.py grew it, then about 8 more, as VAEEncodeForInpaint grows it (its grow_mask_by: 16 is a 16-pixel kernel, padded 8). The sorceress’s region is set aside in both counts, since the base already holds her.
