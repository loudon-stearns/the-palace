Depth auto-range: how much of the gradient the body gets

The section’s depth gotcha: with near and far set wider than the subject, the depth pass reads as a flat silhouette; bracket the subject every render and it carries volume. Four steps: one depth pass, the same figure before the fix, both stretched to show what each one holds, and last the measure of how much of 0 to 255 the body uses, before and after. The plates are the bundle’s own: render 005 (fixed range) and render 008 (the first auto-ranged render), same figure, same camera.

v = 1 − (z − near) ÷ (far − near), clamped to 0…1
(the pixel’s value) = one minus (how far the surface is past the near plane) ÷ (the distance from near to far). Near is bright, far is dark. A body 0.4 deep inside a range 1.2 deep can use at most a third of the scale; bracketing it with cam_z_range() lets it use more.
