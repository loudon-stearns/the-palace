The costume dial: what depth's weight does to the cloth

The section's rule, that depth locks the surface and pose frees it, and its dial from 0.0 to 0.8, built up from the two plates the camera sends. Four steps: the plates, pose alone with the body's outline laid over it, the dial turned, and last a count of the light ink each drawing grows beyond its body. The drawings are the bundle's own sweeps, renders 021 and 022: pose held at 0.9, denoise 0.9, seed 21, only depth's weight changing.

(ink beyond the body) = (the light pixels of the drawing that fall outside the body's outline, widened by four pixels) ÷ (the body's own area). Light means brighter than 128 and 60 levels above the cell's background. The body is the depth plate brighter than 40 of 255. It counts white robes, fur, hair and drips; it cannot see black cloth on a dark ground.
