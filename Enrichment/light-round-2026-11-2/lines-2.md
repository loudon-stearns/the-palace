## 1. Marcus Aurelius — being made
- **Place:** The life that shaped the thought. "He learned his Stoicism from a book — his tutor Rusticus handed him the *Discourses* of Epictetus, a former *slave*, and the emperor took a slave's philosophy as his own."
- **Gap:** told he learned from a book, never told why it was a book; the page sets emperor against slave and never puts the two on one line of years; the notebook was written "through plague, endless frontier war" and the reader never sees how fully it sits inside them.
- **Claim:** the slave's philosophy reached the emperor only as a book (Epictetus died c. 135, when Marcus was a boy, and wrote nothing); the notebook Marcus wrote back was written inside the plague and the war. "From a book" is on the page; the why leaks in from Epictetus's page; the single axis is the maker's.
- **Piece:** one timeline, 50 to 180 CE, two lifelines, uncertain dates drawn soft.
- **Made with:** Drawn SVG. Every date to carry two sources.
