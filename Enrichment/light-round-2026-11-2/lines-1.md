# The first five lines — for Loudon, before anything is made

2026-11-17. Each from its own reader (Opus), who read its page whole and the Shop's menu, and wrote nothing. Brief: `brief-v1.md`. Not yet seen by Loudon when written.

## 1. Like Water
- **Place:** Cross-Domain Resonance. "Water never tries to go through a rock; it tries to go downward, and the detour around the rock is simply the form that downwardness takes here."
- **Gap:** the page says its six qualities are "one nature … manifesting differently depending on context", and shows that as a table of six separate rows. The hero shows a channel bending among stones and nothing says why it bends.
- **Claim:** the detour is not a second rule; it is the one rule, go downward, meeting different ground. First half on the page; the demonstration is the maker's.
- **Piece:** two panels from one simulation on one seeded slope. Smooth ground: drops run straight down. The same ground with one mound: the same drops, same rule, bend round it. The script holds no rule about the rock.
- **Made with:** Drawn SVG (a Python simulation, then SVG, PNG through headless Chrome).
- **Passed over, with reasons:** a generated stream plate with Depth Anything as the height (camera depth is not elevation, so the paths would lie); Manim or p5 for motion (the claim is a structure).

## 2. Mycelial Reverb
- **Place:** the opening. "Feed it sound and threads reach out, branch, and fuse where they meet; the network that grows is the room you hear."
- **Gap:** the page says twice that growing the network changes the room, and nowhere can a reader hear one room become another.
- **Claim:** one knock through one colony as it grows comes back from a longer room each time, and a frost, by taking the dead ends, makes it longer still. On the page in general; the figures will be recomputed for this colony.
- **Piece:** about 15 to 20 s. The same dry knock five times, each followed by its room: newborn, three sizes, then frosted. A picture of five decay curves on one time axis, each with its thread count and measured decay time.
- **Made with:** the project's own instrument (the plugin's engine, `plugin/src/Algorithm.h`, compiled with a small harness in the maker's own folder, nothing written in the project) → Sound from Code → Drawn SVG.

## 3. Search Before You Build
- **Place:** Why it exists. "The knowledge wasn't missing — retrieval was manual, and it made Loudon the index."
- **Gap:** the reader is told the fix "was already in" a file, and not how long it had sat there.
- **Claim:** the answer was in the palace's records 25 days before the miss (committed 2026-06-14; missed and found 2026-07-09, twelve minutes apart). "Already there" is on the page; the days and minutes are the maker's, from git and the render log.
- **Piece:** a thin timeline from git, and at its July end the two real depth plates from that morning, flat and bracketed.
- **Made with:** Drawn SVG, placing two existing plates.
- **Risk the reader named:** GenAI Camera's face already uses those two plates.

## 4. Walk Ceremony
- **Place:** How to Walk. "The link type is a suggestion about what kind of attention to bring…"
- **Gap:** the page gives a question for three link types and calls a fourth open. A walker at a real door meets types the page never names.
- **Claim:** nearly half the palace's typed links are the open `connects-to`; the three with a question are about a fifth; about a third are types the page does not name. The proportions are the maker's, from the map of 2026-11-16.
- **Piece:** about 2,500 small door marks grouped by type; the page's four in ink with its own questions beside them, the rest grey.
- **Made with:** the palace map's script for the count, a second count by YAML, Drawn SVG.
- **Risk the reader named:** it may be only a chart.

## 5. Stable Audio Open
- **Place:** Gotchas. "A rate or a count asked for in words is not held either. 'One single drop falling into water every second' came back with 40 to 69 onsets in 26 s over four seeds. Generate one event and place it at the rate yourself."
- **Gap:** the gotcha and the recipe that answers it sit thirty lines apart, and a reader of a sound tool's page hears nothing.
- **Claim:** asked in words for one drop a second, the model gives a busy uneven drip; asked for one drop, then placed by hand, the same model keeps the rate. On the page.
- **Piece:** about 21 s. Ten seconds of the drip as asked in words (the page's own prompt and its nearest-to-right seed), a breath, ten seconds of one generated drop placed once a second. Two lanes of onsets on one time axis.
- **Made with:** Stable Audio Open → Sound from Code → Drawn SVG → ffmpeg.

## What the session sees across the five

- Three of five are Drawn SVG alone. Two reach past the hand: one to a Specialist, one to a project's instrument. None is generated imagery, motion or voice.
- Two of the three drawn ones (Like Water, Walk Ceremony) are near to "only a chart", and their readers said so.
- Every reader found faults on its page: 22 in all. Kept in each report; to be gathered for the pages.
- One reader assumed a neighbour page was already assigned to another maker. It is not. The brief should say that each maker knows only its own page.
