# Candidates — light round 2026-11-2

Gathered by the Concierge on 2026-11-17, read-only, for the working session to choose fifty from after reading. 86 pages.

**How it was made.** Every tracked entry was read by script for its type, stage, pillars, whether its bundle holds a `— rich.json` or a `— scroll.md`, and how many figures its text embeds (image embeds other than the hero, plus mermaid fences). "Opens" is the first long prose line of the page's body, copied, not a summary. No page was read whole, so this slate says where a page stands, not where a first-time reader loses the thread; that reading is step 3 of [[Light Enrichment]] and is the maker's. The Shop lines are the bench's own (`node Shop/shop.mjs menu --all`, host mac).

- 1 from 2 and JSUI, both dormant: a sleeping page is not dressed without asking.
