## Leibniz

- **File:** `Leibniz — two clocks, no wire.wav`
- **Its picture:** `Leibniz — two clocks, no wire — picture.svg` (drawn from what the sound did; same caption)
- **Title:** Two clocks, no wire
- **Caption / alt:** First, two clocks set once and never touching: a wooden tock in the left ear, a metal tick in the right. They stay together because each keeps its own time. Then a wired pair: they start apart, plainly at different rates, and as the wire tightens they pull into step. Both pairs end in unison. Only the second needed a wire.
- **What is real, what is a choice:** The first half is Leibniz's pre-established harmony; the second is two Kuramoto phases with rising coupling.

## Endosymbiosis

- **File:** `Endosymbiosis — two rhythms become one.wav`
- **Its picture:** `Endosymbiosis — two rhythms become one — picture.svg` (drawn from what the sound did; same caption)
- **Title:** Two rhythms become one
- **Caption / alt:** Two tones seven hertz apart, beating against each other. From four seconds the coupling between them rises: the beat slows, lurches, and near nine seconds they lock onto one pitch that neither started on. - **What is real, what is a choice:** A two-oscillator Kuramoto model, the page's own image. It is not a model of a cell.
