# Challenge — the light round's first forty — 2026-11-10

On Loudon's "do the challenger pass and the workshop commit, and 30 more light enrichments" (2026-11-10, relayed by the working session).

## How it was run

Four read-only challengers, one per batch, about ten pieces each, dispatched by the working session with one prompt the Concierge wrote. None made or placed a piece. Each read the page whole, opened the piece and the code that made it, and recomputed its numbers. Their reports are beside this file, verbatim: `batch-A.md` to `batch-D.md`.

Three departures from [[Enrichment]] step 6, each on purpose:

- One challenger read many pages, not one. The step fixes who challenges and that every piece gets a reason; it does not fix one per page.
- Two of its three questions were set aside: "does it test the claim or decorate it" and "does it walk up". A light piece is not a test and has no walk. The questions asked instead: does it say what the sentence says; is anything false or overstated; does the "what is real" line tell the truth; would the reader be better off without it.
- The forty were already committed (`72e4b9ac`), so "retire is dropped before landing" could not hold. None was retired.

Nobody ran `conform.mjs` or loaded the live face as part of the challenge; the Concierge did both before and after. Nobody has heard the ten sounds.

## What the Concierge changed afterward, and what it did not

Changed, in the manifests only, as form:

- Every one of the forty sections carries a `challenge` block with the verdict and its reason.
- Every piece moved from `place: margin` to `place: band`. All four reports found the sheets' and diagrams' lettering at 4 to 6 px in a 440 px margin. One field, no file touched, and it goes back the same way.
- On the ten sounds, the claim now sits under the picture and the sound's own line carries "what is real and what is a choice". Batch A found every picture captioned "The same sound, drawn.", which claims nothing; that caption was the Concierge's, not the maker's.

Not changed: no piece, no caption's words as the maker wrote them, no page's text. The twenty-four remakes are the working session's to make, on Loudon's yes. The offers the challengers wrote for the pages themselves are not in any manifest's `offers` list yet.
